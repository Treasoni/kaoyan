## Part 2: 搭配填空题

**6. The government should take effective _____ to reduce pollution.**
   - [ ] A. ways
   - [ ] B. methods
   - [ ] C. measures ✅
   - [ ] D. means

> [!answer] 答案：C（measures - 措施）⚠️ 僻义词

**7. There is _____ concern regarding the future of the economy.**
   - [ ] A. a great deal of ✅
   - [ ] B. great deal
   - [ ] C. a great deal
   - [ ] D. deal of

> [!answer] 答案：A（a great deal of - 大量）⚠️ deal作"大量"讲

   - [ ] A. make
   - [ ] B. strike ✅
   - [ ] C. have
   - [ ] D. keep

**9. The team managed to _____ a victory in the final match.**
   - [ ] A. get
   - [ ] B. obtain
   - [ ] C. secure ✅
   - [ ] D. acquire

> [!answer] 答案：C（secure - 获得、赢得）⚠️ 僻义词

**10. Many rare species are _____ with extinction due to habitat loss.**

   - [ ] B. threatened for

   - [ ] D. threaten

> [!answer] 答案：A（be threatened with - 受到...威胁）

## 重点词汇辨析

### efficient vs effective
- **efficient**: 效率高的（强调不浪费）
- **effective**: 有效的（强调达到效果）

### economic vs economical
- **economic**: 经济的（与经济有关）
- **economical**: 节俭的（省钱、节约）

### worth vs worthy vs worthwhile
- **worth**: 值得（后接doing或名词）
- **worthy**: 值得的（be worthy of）
- **worthwhile**: 值得的（it is worthwhile to do）
