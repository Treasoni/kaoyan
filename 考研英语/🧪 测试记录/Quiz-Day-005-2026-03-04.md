## 题型一：词义选择（10题）

### 1. The word "decline" in "The company declined to comment on the rumor" means:
- [ ] A. decrease
- [ ] B. refuse politely
- [ ] C. slope downward
- [ ] D. become weaker

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：此处的 **decline** 是僻义"婉言拒绝"，非常见义"下降"。
> ⚠️ 考研高频考点：decline an invitation / decline to comment

### 2. "He solved the problem in a strange fashion." Here "fashion" means:
- [ ] A. clothing style
- [ ] B. trend
- [ ] C. way, manner
- [ ] D. industry

> [!answer]- 答案：C
> **解析**：**fashion** 此处是僻义"方式、方法"，非常见义"时尚"。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：in a fashion = in a way

### 3. "A certain Mr. Smith is waiting for you." The word "certain" means:
- [ ] A. sure
- [ ] B. confident
- [ ] C. some, particular
- [ ] D. inevitable

> [!answer]- 答案：C
> **解析**：**certain** 此处是僻义"某个、某些"，用于指不确定但特定的人/物。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：a certain person / to a certain extent

### 4. "She has a gift for languages." The word "gift" means:
- [ ] A. present
- [ ] B. talent
- [ ] C. donation
- [ ] D. reward

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：**gift** 此处是僻义"天赋"，非常见义"礼物"。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：have a gift for = be gifted in

### 5. "The law favors large corporations." The word "favors" means:
- [ ] A. helps
- [ ] B. prefers, shows bias toward
- [ ] C. supports
- [ ] D. approves

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：**favor** 此处是僻义"偏爱、偏袒"，含有不公平的意味。
> ⚠️ 相关：show favor to / in favor of

### 6. The word "skeptical" is closest in meaning to:
- [ ] A. doubtful
- [ ] B. hopeful
- [ ] C. certain
- [ ] D. optimistic

> [!answer]- 答案：A
> **解析**：**skeptical** = sceptical，意为"怀疑的"。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：be skeptical of/about

### 7. "The skeleton of the plan was approved." Here "skeleton" means:
- [ ] A. bones
- [ ] B. framework, outline
- [ ] C. thin person
- [ ] D. structure

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：**skeleton** 此处是引申义"框架、梗概"。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：skeleton staff / skeleton crew（基本人员）

### 8. "Cease fire immediately!" The word "cease" means:
- [ ] A. pause briefly
- [ ] B. stop completely
- [ ] C. reduce
- [ ] D. delay

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：**cease** 意为"停止、终止"，比 stop 更正式。
> ⚠️ 区别：cease doing（停止正在做的）vs cease to do（不再去做）

### 9. "Deduce the conclusion from the facts." The word "deduce" means:
- [ ] A. guess
- [ ] B. infer, conclude
- [ ] C. reduce
- [ ] D. produce

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：**deduce** 意为"推断、演绎"，强调逻辑推理。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：deduce from

### 10. "The committee deemed the proposal unacceptable." The word "deemed" means:
- [ ] A. thought, considered
- [ ] B. decided
- [ ] C. rejected
- [ ] D. accepted

> [!answer]- 答案：A
> **解析**：**deem** 意为"认为、视为"，常用于正式场合。
> ⚠️ 固定搭配：deem it necessary / deem highly of

## 题型二：搭配填空（5题）

### 11. The court _____ to hear his appeal.
> [!answer]- 答案：**declined**
> 解析：decline to do sth = 婉言拒绝做某事

### 12. Critics are _____ of the government's claims.
> [!answer]- 答案：**skeptical** / **sceptical**
> 解析：be skeptical of = 对...持怀疑态度

### 13. The new policy is _____ small businesses.
> [!answer]- 答案：**favorable to** / **in favor of**
> 解析：favorable to = 有利于；in favor of = 支持

### 14. She _____ her life _____ helping the poor.
> [!answer]- 答案：**dedicated...to**
> 解析：dedicate...to = 致力于

### 15. We can _____ the truth _____ the evidence.
> [!answer]- 答案：**deduce...from**
> 解析：deduce...from = 从...推断

## ⚠️ 题型三：僻义专项测试（5题）

### 16. "DECLINE" - 在考研中最常见的僻义是：
- [ ] A. 下降
- [ ] B. 婉言拒绝
- [ ] C. 倾斜
- [ ] D. 衰退

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：考研常考 decline 作为动词的僻义"婉言拒绝"。
> 高频搭配：decline an invitation / decline to comment / decline an offer

### 17. "FASHION" - 作为动词或表示"方式"时，下列哪个正确？
- [ ] A. fashion industry（时尚产业）- 常见义
- [ ] B. in a fashion（以某种方式）- 僻义
- [ ] C. fashion show（时装秀）- 常见义
- [ ] D. fashion design（服装设计）- 常见义

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：in a fashion = in a way，是僻义用法。

### 18. "CERTAIN" - 在 "to a certain extent" 中，certain 的意思是：
- [ ] A. 确定的
- [ ] B. 某种程度的
- [ ] C. 确信的
- [ ] D. 必然的

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：to a certain extent = to some extent，意为"在某种程度上"。

### 19. 下列哪个句子中的 "gift" 使用了僻义？
- [ ] A. I received a gift on my birthday.
- [ ] B. The store sells gift cards.
- [ ] C. She has a gift for music.
- [ ] D. This is a gift shop.

> [!answer]- 答案：C
> **解析**：have a gift for = 有...天赋，是僻义用法。

### 20. "The legislation favors the wealthy." 这句话的意思是：
- [ ] A. 法律帮助富人
- [ ] B. 法律偏爱富人
- [ ] C. 法律支持富人
- [ ] D. 法律批准富人

> [!answer]- 答案：B
> **解析**：favor 在此处含有"偏袒、不公平对待"的意味。
