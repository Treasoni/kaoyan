## 📝 词义选择题 (1-8)

### 1. compensate
The company promised to **compensate** the workers for their overtime work.
- [ ] A. 抱怨
- [ ] B. 补偿
- [ ] C. 编译
- [ ] D. 竞争

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>

**正确答案**: B. 补偿

**解析**: compensate vi. 补偿；弥补；vt. 补偿，赔偿
- 搭配: compensate for sth 对…进行弥补；compensate sb for sth 就…对某人进行补偿

</details>

### 5. sensible vs sensitive (易混淆词)
She made a **sensible** decision to save money for emergencies.
- [ ] A. 敏感的
- [ ] B. 明智的，理智的
- [ ] C. 感觉的
- [ ] D. 知觉的

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>

**正确答案**: B. 明智的，理智的

**解析**: 易混淆词辨析
- **sensible**: adj. 明智的，理智的；意识到的；实用耐穿的
  - 搭配: be sensible of 知道
- **sensitive**: adj. 体贴的；易生气的；敏感的；灵敏的
  - 搭配: be sensitive to/about sth 对…敏感
- **sensation**: n. 感觉；知觉；轰动一时

</details>

## 📝 词义辨析题 (13-15)

### 13. compare...with vs compare...to
In his speech, the professor _______ the human brain a computer.
- [ ] A. compared ... with
- [ ] B. compared ... to
- [ ] C. competitive ... with
- [ ] D. comparable ... to

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>

**正确答案**: B. compared ... to

**解析**: compare 搭配辨析
- **compare ... with ...**: 与…相比较（强调对比差异）
- **compare ... to ...**: 把... 比作...（强调比喻相似性）

</details>

### 14. ⚠️ boom 多义辨析

- [ ] C. 以低沉声音说
- [ ] D. 吃草

- vi. 轰鸣；繁荣
- v. 以低沉有力的声音说
- 搭配: economic boom 经济繁荣；boom period 繁荣时期

### 15. real系列词义辨析
She finally **realized** her dream of becoming a doctor.
- [ ] A. 意识到
- [ ] B. 实现
- [ ] C. 现实的
- [ ] D. 实际情况

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>

**正确答案**: B. 实现

**解析**: realize v.
- 实现（梦想、目标）
- 理解；领会；意识到
- 搭配: realize one's dream 实现梦想；realize that... 意识到…

**real系列词汇**:
- realise/realize: v. 意识到；实现
- reality: n. 现实；实际情况；事实
- realistic: adj. 现实的；实际的；可实现的；逼真的

</details>
