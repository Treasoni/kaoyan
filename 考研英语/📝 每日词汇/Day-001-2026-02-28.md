## ⚠️ 僻义预警

### deal [warning]
- **常见义**: n. 交易，协议
- **考研僻义**: n. 大量；vt. 发牌、处理、对付
- **高频搭配**:
  - `a great deal of` - 大量
  - `deal with` - 处理、对付
  - `get a fair deal` - 受到公正对待
- **真题例句**: We must deal with the problem of climate change.

### means [warning]
- **常见义**: n. 方法
- **考研僻义**: n. 财富、手段
- **高频搭配**:
  - `by no means` - 一点也不
  - `by means of` - 通过...方式
  - `a means to do sth` - 做某事的手段
- **真题例句**: By no means should we ignore the importance of education.

### secure [warning]
- **常见义**: adj. 安全的
- **考研僻义**: vt. 获得、保护；adj. 可靠的
- **高频搭配**:
  - `be secured against` - 以某物作为担保
  - `make sth secure` - 加固某物
- **真题例句**: The company secured a contract with the government.

### measure [warning]
- **常见义**: vt./n. 测量
- **考研僻义**: n. 措施、方法
- **高频搭配**:
  - `take measures to do` - 采取措施做某事
  - `effective measure` - 有效措施
  - `measure up` - 测量、达到标准
- **真题例句**: The government must take measures to reduce pollution.

### date [attention]
- **常见义**: n. 日期
- **考研僻义**: v. 谈恋爱、追溯到；n. 约会
- **高频搭配**:
  - `have a date with sb` - 和某人约会
  - `out of date` - 过时的
  - `date back` - 追溯到
- **真题例句**: This tradition dates back to the 18th century.
