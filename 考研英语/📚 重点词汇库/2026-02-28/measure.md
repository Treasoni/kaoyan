# measure

## 基本信息
**音标**: /ˈmeʒə(r)/
**词性**: vt./n.
**难度**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

## ⚠️ 僻义预警 [warning]

> [!warning] 陷阱提示
> 此词在考研中**50%**考查名词"措施"义，而非仅是"测量"

**考研常考僻义**:
- n. **措施、办法**（take measures to do）
- n. **程度、数量**（in some measure）
- vt. **衡量、判断**（measure A against B）

### 真题风格例句
> [!example] 阅读/完形风格
> The government must take **effective measures** to reduce pollution.
> (政府必须采取有效措施减少污染。)

> [!example] 阅读/翻译风格
> We should **measure** the benefits **against** the costs before making a decision.
> (在做决定之前，我们应该权衡收益和成本。)

> [!example] 阅读风格
> The new policy failed to **measure up** to public expectations.
> (新政策没有达到公众的期望。)

### 常用搭配

| 搭配 | 释义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **take measures to do** | 采取措施做某事 | Take measures to protect environment |
| **effective measure** | 有效措施 | Adopt effective measures |
| **measure A against B** | 使AB相比较/权衡 | Measure benefits against costs |
| **measure up** | 测量；达到标准 | Measure up to expectations |
| **beyond measure** | 不可估量、极度 | Grateful beyond measure |
| **in some measure** | 在某种程度上 | Success in some measure |
| **in a large measure** | 很大程度上 | Due in a large measure to |

### 同义词辨析
- **measure** (n.): 措施（正式，政策性）
- **step**: 措施、步骤（具体行动）
- **action**: 行动（通用）
- **method**: 方法（系统性）
- **means**: 手段（工具、途径）

## 常见义（对比）
vt. 测量；n. 测量量度、尺寸

⚠️ 易错点：在阅读中遇到 measure（名词）时，优先考虑"措施"义

## 写作应用

### 高级替换
- **初级**: way to solve / method to deal with
- **高级**: take **measures** to do

### 写作例句
> The government should take **effective measures** to address the problem of income inequality.
> (政府应该采取有效措施来解决收入不平等问题。)

> We must **measure** the potential benefits **against** the possible risks.
> (我们必须权衡潜在收益和可能风险。)

> In a large **measure**, the success of the project depends on teamwork.
> (很大程度上，项目的成功取决于团队合作。)

## 词族
- measurement (n.) - 测量、度量
- measured (adj.) - 量过的、慎重的
- measurable (adj.) - 可测量的、显著的
- immeasurable (adj.) - 不可估量的
