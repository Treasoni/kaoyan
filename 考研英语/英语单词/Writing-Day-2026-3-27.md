## 🔄 Part 1: 写作词汇替换

> [!tip] 说明
> 将下列简单词汇替换为今日学习的高级词汇，提升写作水平

### 1.1 形容词替换

| 原词 | 高级替换 | 例句改写 |
|------|----------|----------|
| boring | **tedious** | ❌ The lecture was boring.<br>✅ The lecture was **tedious** and failed to engage students. |
| important | **imperative** | ❌ It is important to act now.<br>✅ It is **imperative** that we take immediate action. |
| useful | **versatile** | ❌ This tool is useful.<br>✅ This **versatile** tool can serve multiple purposes. |
| dangerous | **reckless** | ❌ His behavior was dangerous.<br>✅ His **reckless** disregard for safety endangered everyone. |
| strange | **odd** | ❌ There was something strange about him.<br>✅ There was something **odd** about his behavior. |

### 1.2 名词替换

| 原词 | 高级替换 | 例句改写 |
|------|----------|----------|
| energy | **impetus** | ❌ The project needs more energy.<br>✅ The new funding provided fresh **impetus** to the project. |
| difficulty | **obstacle** | ❌ We faced many difficulties.<br>✅ We encountered numerous **obstacles** along the way. |
| examination | **scrutiny** | ❌ The plan needs close examination.<br>✅ The proposal deserves close **scrutiny** before implementation. |
| part | **segment** | ❌ A large part of the population...<br>✅ A significant **segment** of the population... |
| problem | **hardship** | ❌ They faced many problems.<br>✅ They endured tremendous **hardship** during the crisis. |

### 1.3 动词替换

| 原词 | 高级替换 | 例句改写 |
|------|----------|----------|
| start | **launch** | ❌ They started a new campaign.<br>✅ They **launched** a comprehensive campaign. |
| fill | **saturate** | ❌ The market is filled with products.<br>✅ The market is **saturated** with similar products. |
| make | **manufacture** | ❌ The factory makes cars.<br>✅ The facility **manufactures** automotive components. |
| hold | **harbor** | ❌ He holds resentment.<br>✅ He **harbors** deep resentment toward his critics. |
| consider | **reckon** | ❌ We must consider this possibility.<br>✅ We must **reckon with** this possibility. |

## 📝 Part 2: 汉译英练习

> [!info] 使用今日学习的词汇翻译下列句子

### 2.1 基础翻译

1. **彻底审查这些数据是至关重要的。**
   - 关键词: scrutiny, imperative
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: It is **imperative** to subject this data to close **scrutiny**.

2. **这家多才多艺的公司正在发起一项新活动。**
   - 关键词: versatile, launch
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: This **versatile** company is **launching** a new campaign.

3. **我们必须考虑到潜在的障碍。**
   - 关键词: reckon with, obstacle
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: We must **reckon with** potential **obstacles**.

4. **市场已经饱和，新公司难以进入。**
   - 关键词: saturate, from scratch
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: The market is **saturated**, making it hard for new companies to start **from scratch**.

5. **他与同事们的意见存在分歧。**
   - 关键词: at odds with
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: He is **at odds with** his colleagues.

---

### 2.2 进阶翻译（写作句型）

6. **经仔细审查，这项政策存在明显缺陷。**
   - 句型: On close scrutiny...
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: **On close scrutiny**, this policy reveals obvious flaws.

7. **这项研究濒临被废弃的边缘。**
   - 句型: on the verge of
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: The research is **on the verge of** being **scrapped**.

8. **从头开始建立信任是至关重要的。**
   - 句型: It is imperative that... / from scratch
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: **It is imperative that** trust be built **from scratch**.

9. **经济的动力来自于创新。**
   - 句型: The impetus for... comes from...
   - 你的翻译: _______________________
   - 参考答案: The **impetus** for economic growth **comes from** innovation.

10. **我们必须分辨事实与虚构。**
    - 句型: tell A from B
    - 你的翻译: _______________________
    - 参考答案: We must **tell** fact **from** fiction.

## 🔀 Part 3: 词义辨析

> [!warning] 区分下列易混淆词汇

### 3.1 heroine vs heroin

| 单词 | 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **heroine** | n. | 女英雄；女主角 | She became a **heroine** of the resistance. |
| **heroin** | n. | 海洛因 | **Heroin** addiction is a serious social problem. |

**练习:**
1. The main character in the novel is a tragic ___. (heroine/heroin)
2. The police seized a large quantity of ___. (heroine/heroin)

---

### 3.2 hardly vs hard

| 单词 | 词性 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **hard** | adv. | 努力地 | work hard (努力工作) |
| **hardly** | adv. | 几乎不 (否定) | can hardly (几乎不能) |

**练习:**
1. She works very ___. (hard/hardly)
2. I can ___ believe my eyes. (hard/hardly)
3. ___ had I arrived when the phone rang. (Hard/Hardly)

---

### 3.3 odd vs odds

| 单词 | 词性 | 含义 | 常用搭配 |
|------|------|------|----------|
| **odd** | adj. | 奇怪的；奇数的 | odd number (奇数) |
| **odds** | n. | 概率；赔率 | at odds with (与...有分歧) |

**练习:**
1. There is something ___ about his behavior. (odd/odds)
2. The ___ of winning are slim. (odd/odds)
3. His views are at ___ with mainstream opinion. (odd/odds)

---

### 3.4 tear (n.) vs tear (v.)

| 单词 | 发音 | 词性 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|------|
| **tear** | /tɪər/ | n. | 眼泪 |
| **tear** | /teər/ | v. | 撕裂 |

**练习:**
1. A ___ rolled down her cheek. (tear/tear - 眼泪)
2. Don't ___ the paper. (tear/tear - 撕)
3. She burst into ___. (tears/tears)

## 📚 Part 4: 写作模板

> [!success] 使用今日词汇的高级写作模板

### 4.1 议论文开头

```
In contemporary society, [TOPIC] has become a subject of intense
scrutiny. It is imperative that we reckon with both the benefits
and obstacles associated with this issue.
```

**示例:**
In contemporary society, artificial intelligence has become a subject of intense **scrutiny**. It is **imperative** that we **reckon with** both the benefits and **obstacles** associated with this technology.

---

### 4.2 原因分析

```
The primary impetus for [PHENOMENON] stems from [CAUSE].
On close scrutiny, we find that [ANALYSIS].
```

**示例:**
The primary **impetus** for remote work stems from technological advancement. **On close scrutiny**, we find that companies launching such policies often report increased productivity.

---

### 4.3 观点对比

```
Proponents argue that [VIEW A], while critics remain at odds with
this perspective, contending that [VIEW B].
```

**示例:**
Proponents argue that automation **satisfies** efficiency demands, while critics remain **at odds with** this perspective, contending that it creates unemployment.

---

### 4.4 结论段

```
In conclusion, addressing [ISSUE] requires launching comprehensive
efforts from scratch. Only by satisfying both [CONDITION A] and
[CONDITION B] can we overcome the obstacles ahead.
```

**示例:**
In conclusion, addressing climate change requires **launching** comprehensive efforts **from scratch**. Only by **satisfying** both economic and environmental demands can we overcome the **obstacles** ahead.
