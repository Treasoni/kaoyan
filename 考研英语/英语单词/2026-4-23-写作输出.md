## 一、高级词汇替换练习

考研写作中，使用高级词汇替换基础词汇是提分关键。以下从今日词汇中提取可用于写作替换的词汇。

### 1.1 基础词 → 高级替换

| 基础表达 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|---------|---------|------|
| big | **quite** large | The impact is **quite** large. |
| change | **picture** (描述) | As **pictured** in the data... |
| force | **push** (推动) | We should **push** for reform. |
| have | **own** (拥有) | Every citizen should **own** the right to... |
| however | **nonetheless** | **Nonetheless**, the results are promising. |
| power | **might** (力量) | The **might** of collective action... |
| put | **place** (放置) | We should **place** emphasis on... |
| think | **picture** (想象) | **Picture** a world where... |
| control | **police** (监管) | The market must be properly **policed**. |
| doubt | **question** (质疑) | Some **question** the effectiveness of... |

### 1.2 写作替换练习

请用高级替换词改写以下句子：

**题目1**：The problem is very big.
> **参考答案**：The problem is **quite** significant.

**题目2**：We should think about a better future.
> **参考答案**：We should **picture** a more promising future.

**题目3**：However, there are still many difficulties.
> **参考答案**：**Nonetheless**, numerous challenges remain.

**题目4**：The government has a lot of power.
> **参考答案**：The government wields considerable **might**.

**题目5**：We should pay attention to education.
> **参考答案**：We should **place** greater emphasis on education.

## 三、词义辨析练习

### 3.1 quiet vs quite

**题目1**：The library is very ______.
> **答案**：quiet（形容词，安静的）

**题目2**：The result is ______ surprising.
> **答案**：quite（副词，相当）

**题目3**：Please keep ______ in the classroom.
> **答案**：quiet（形容词，安静的）

**记忆口诀**：quiet 有 "e" 在中间 = 安静的；quite 没有 "e" = 相当的

### 3.2 a number of vs the number of

**题目4**：______ students have passed the exam. (A number of / The number of)
> **答案**：A number of��许多学生通过了��试，谓语用复数）

**题目5**：______ students in our class is 45. (A number of / The number of)
> **答案**：The number of（我们班学生的数量是45，谓语用单数）

### 3.3 no more vs no longer

**题目6**：He ______ lives here. (no more / no longer)
> **答案**：no longer（他不再住这里了 — 强调时间）

**题目7**：I have ______ money left. (no more / no longer)
> **答案**：no more（我没有更多的钱了 — 强调数量）

### 3.4 pass away vs pass out

**题目8**：The old man ______ last night. (passed away / passed out)
> **答案**：passed away（老人昨晚去世了）

**题目9**：She ______ from the heat. (passed away / passed out)
> **答案**：passed out（她因为酷热而失去知觉）

## 四、写作模板积累

以下是使用今日词汇构建的考研写作模板句：

### 4.1 议论文开头段

> The **picture** of [topic] has changed dramatically **over** the past few years. **Once** considered [old view], [topic] is now widely recognized as [new view]. **Nonetheless**, the **question** of whether [debate point] remains a subject of intense discussion.

### 4.2 议论文论述段

> We should **place** greater emphasis on [point]. The **might** of [force] should not be underestimated. **A number of** studies have shown that [evidence]. Those who **question** this view fail to recognize that [counter-argument].

### 4.3 议论文结尾段

> **Once** we **own** the responsibility to [action], we can **push** for meaningful change. The **need** for [goal] has never been more urgent. It is **quite** clear that [conclusion].

## 五、今日写作任务

请用以下至少5个词汇写一段80-100字的英文短文（话题：城市发展与社区保护）：

**必用词汇**：picture, nonetheless, push, place (emphasis), own, once, might, question

> **参考范文**：
>
> The picture of urban development has raised an important question: who truly owns the right to shape our communities? Nonetheless, as cities push for modernization, we must place greater emphasis on protecting existing neighborhoods. Once residents are displaced, the might of collective action often proves too late. The need for inclusive planning has never been more urgent. It is quite clear that sustainable development requires balancing progress with the rights of those who call these places home.
