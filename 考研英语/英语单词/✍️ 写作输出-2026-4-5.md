## 🔄 Part 1: 高级词汇替换

**将下列句子中的简单词汇替换为更高级的词汇：**

1. The government needs to **explain** (→ clarify) its position on this issue.
   - The government needs to **clarify** its position on this issue.

2. The company decided to **use** (→ deploy) new technology.
   - The company decided to **deploy** new technology.

3. This will **raise** (→ elevate) the quality of education.

7. Only **qualified** (→ eligible) candidates can apply.

## 🔄 Part 2: 汉译英练习
**将下列中文句子翻译成英文，使用括号中的高级词汇：**

1. 政府需要阐明其在这项议题上的立场。（clarify）
   - The government needs to **clarify** its position on this issue.

2. 公司决定部署新技术来提高效率。（deploy）
   - The company decided to **deploy** new technology to improve efficiency.

3. 这项政策将提升公众对环境问题的意识。（elevate）
   - This policy will **elevate** public awareness of environmental issues.

4. 各国必须联合起来应对气候变化。（unite）
   - Nations must **unite** to address climate change.

5. 只有符合条件的学生才能申请奖学金。（eligible）
   - Only **eligible** students can apply for the scholarship.

6. 他决定着手一项新的事业。（embark on）
   - He decided to **embark on** a new career.

7. 议会需要表达选民的担忧。（voice）
   - The council needs to **voice** voters' concerns.

8. 这一悲剧给整个社区带来了深切的悲痛。（grief）
   - The tragedy brought deep **grief** to the entire community.

9. 我们需要统一的政策来解决这个问题。（uniform）
   - We need a **uniform** policy to address this issue.

10. 当太阳落山时，景色令人叹为观止。（sight）
   - The **sight** was breathtaking as the sun set.

## 🔄 Part 3: 写作搭配练习
**使用给定的词汇和搭配造句：**

1. **clarify** + the situation / one's position
   - The spokesperson tried to clarify the situation regarding the incident.
   - Let me clarify my position on this matter.

2. **deploy** + resources / technology / strategies
   - The military decided to deploy additional resources to the region.
   - Companies are deploying new strategies to attract customers.

3. **elevate** + public awareness / standards / the debate
   - The campaign aims to elevate public awareness of mental health.
   - We need to elevate our standards of customer service.

4. **unite** + against / in support of / efforts
   - Communities united against the proposed development.
   - We must unite our efforts to combat poverty.

5. **embark on** + a new career / a journey / a project
   - After graduation, she decided to embark on a new career in finance.
   - The team will embark on a challenging project next month.
