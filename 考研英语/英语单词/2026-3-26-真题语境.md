With the **advent** of the digital revolution, traditional industries have been forced to **adhere** to new standards or risk becoming obsolete. The **agency** of change has been swift and sometimes **adverse**, leaving many workers in a state of **fatigue** as they struggle to adapt.

In the **wake** of these developments, how are ordinary citizens **faring**? Many express **agony** over the loss of privacy. The **ego** of tech giants seems boundless—they **advertise** their services as **decent** and ethical while collecting personal data on an unprecedented scale. The **glow** of innovation has dimmed, replaced by **hatred** and distrust.

The **unanimous** call for transparency grows louder. Consumers demand **latitude** in choosing how their data is used. They seek **joint** responsibility from companies—a **cement** of trust rather than exploitation. Yet some corporations still **haul** their feet, resisting change.

**Hasty** reforms have proven ineffective. What's needed is a **joint** effort from all stakeholders: governments must establish clear regulations, companies must **adhere** to ethical standards, and consumers must remain vigilant. Only through such **joint** action can we **harvest** the benefits of technology while avoiding its **fatal** flaws.

As one analyst noted, "We are witnessing the **decay** of the old model. The question is whether something better will emerge from the **vacant** space left behind." The **glimpse** of hope lies in the **zeal** of young entrepreneurs who approach technology with genuine **adore** for its potential to improve lives.

## 📝 阅读理解练习

### 根据文章内容，选择最佳答案：

**1. According to the passage, the "hidden agenda" of corporations refers to:**
- A. Their secret plans to innovate
- B. Their goal to maximize profits regardless of consequences
- C. Their efforts to protect user privacy
- D. Their desire to collaborate with governments

**2. The author mentions "hawks and doves" to illustrate:**
- A. Different bird species affected by technology
- B. Conflicting views on internet regulation
- C. Political parties in government
- D. Types of social media users

**3. What does the phrase "in the wake of" (paragraph 4) most likely mean?**
- A. Before something happens
- B. As a result of something
- C. In opposition to something
- D. Independent of something

**4. The author's attitude toward technology companies can best be described as:**
- A. Unconditionally supportive
- B. Deeply hostile
- C. Cautiously critical
- D. Completely indifferent

**5. According to the passage, what is necessary for meaningful reform?**
- A. Government regulation alone
- B. Corporate self-regulation
- C. Joint effort from all stakeholders
- D. Consumer boycotts

---

### 答案与解析

1. **B** - 文中明确提到 "maximizing profits at any cost" 是公司的隐秘目的
2. **B** - hawks（鹰派）主张严格监管，doves（鸽派）主张最小干预
3. **B** - "in the wake of" 意为"紧随...之后；作为...的结果"
4. **C** - 作者既承认技术的潜在好处，也批评了企业的不道德行为
5. **C** - 文中强调需要 "joint effort from all stakeholders"（各方共同努力）
