> [!warning] 僻义预警
> - 常见义：地址、演讲

## 按写作用途分类

### 议论文写作必备
> 替换初级词汇，提升写作水平

| 初级词汇 | 高级替换 | 词性 |
|----------|----------|------|
| solve/deal with | **address** | vt. ⚠️ 僻义：解决 |
| say/acknowledge | **admit** | v. 承认 |
| enough | **adequate** | adj. 足够的 |
| give | **grant** | vt. 授予 |
| very big | **immense** | adj. 巨大的 |
| at once | **immediate** | adj. 立即的 |
| rule | **govern** | v. 统治 |

### 词族记忆
> 利用词根词缀批量记忆

| 词根/词缀 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----------|------|------|
| **ad-** | 向、加强 | adopt, adapt, admit, admire, adequate |
| **im-** | 向内、加强 | immune, imminent, immigrant, immerse, immense, immediate |
| **-ion** | 名词后缀 | admission, addition, federation |
| **-ive** | 形容词后缀 | active, respective |
