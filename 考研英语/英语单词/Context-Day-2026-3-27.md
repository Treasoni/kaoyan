## 📖 Passage 1: 学术研究类

### The Challenges of Scientific Research

Scientific research is often a **tedious** and demanding process. Researchers must **satisfy** rigorous standards before their work can pass **scrutiny**. When a new theory emerges, it faces close examination from the academic community, and any flaws are quickly identified.

Many breakthroughs begin **from scratch**, requiring scientists to **manufacture** new equipment or develop novel methodologies. The **imperative** to publish results creates pressure, yet researchers must remain patient and thorough. Those who are **reckless** in their claims risk having their work **scrapped** by peer reviewers.

The **impetus** for discovery often comes from unexpected places. A casual observation might **seize** a researcher's attention, leading to years of investigation. The **odds** of success may seem slim, but dedicated scientists **launch** into their work with enthusiasm, knowing that each experiment brings them closer to understanding.

> [!tip] 本段关键词
> tedious, satisfy, scrutiny, from scratch, manufacture, imperative, reckless, scrap, impetus, seize, odds, launch

---

### Questions

1. What must researchers do before their work passes scrutiny?
2. Why is being reckless dangerous for researchers?
3. Where does the impetus for discovery often come from?

## 📖 Passage 2: 社会经济类

### Urban Development and Its Obstacles

Modern cities face numerous **obstacles** in their development. The **sector** of public transportation, for instance, must **maneuver** around budget constraints while **satisfying** growing demand. City planners often find themselves **at odds with** residents who oppose new infrastructure projects.

When examining urban problems **on close scrutiny**, we find that many issues are interconnected. A **pedestrian** crossing might seem like a simple addition, but it requires coordination across multiple **sections** of government. The **vertical** growth of skyscrapers creates new challenges for emergency services, while **horizontal** expansion consumes valuable land.

Economic development can **saturate** local markets, leaving small businesses struggling. Some **harbor** resentment toward large corporations that **scrape** by with minimal regulation. The **launch** of new businesses often occurs **on the verge of** financial collapse, with entrepreneurs **scrambling** for funding.

> [!note] 写作句型
> - "When examining...on close scrutiny, we find that..." (经仔细审查，我们发现...)
> - "The...of...creates new challenges for..." (...的...为...创造了新挑战)

---

### Questions

1. What obstacles do modern cities face?
2. Why are city planners often at odds with residents?
3. What happens when economic development saturates local markets?

## 📖 Passage 3: 科技与伦理

### The Double-Edged Sword of Technology

In our **imperial** pursuit of technological advancement, we must not lose sight of ethical considerations. The **manufacture** of surveillance equipment raises concerns about privacy. Governments might **screen** citizens' communications under the **veil** of national security, yet such actions put democracies **at odds with** their founding principles.

Artificial intelligence has become **versatile** enough to perform tasks that once required human **manual** labor. This **segment** of the economy is undergoing rapid transformation. Workers fear their skills will become **secondary** to machine capabilities, and many **lament** the loss of traditional employment.

Yet technology also offers solutions. **Laser** surgery saves lives; **telegraph** and instant communication connect distant communities. The key is to **reckon** with both benefits and risks, ensuring that progress serves all of **mankind** rather than a privileged few.

> [!important] 核心观点
> 科技发展需要伦理考量，既要看到益处也要reckon with（考虑到）风险。

---

### Questions

1. What concerns does the manufacture of surveillance equipment raise?
2. Why do workers fear their skills will become secondary?
3. What is the key to ensuring progress serves everyone?

## 📖 Passage 4: 教育与成长

### The Journey of Learning

Learning is **imperative** for personal growth, yet the path is often filled with **hardship**. Students must **acquaint** themselves with new concepts repeatedly, and **occasional** failures are inevitable. The **satisfaction** of mastering a difficult subject, however, makes the effort worthwhile.

Good educators **scaffold** learning, breaking complex topics into manageable **segments**. They recognize that each student has a unique **velocity** of learning. Some may **struggle** with mathematics while excelling in literature; others might find **tedious** memorization easy but creative writing challenging.

The **imperative** for educators is to **tell** genuine difficulties from simple lack of effort. A student who **harbors** anxiety about exams needs different support than one who is merely **impatient** with the learning process. Understanding these distinctions is **undoubtedly** crucial for effective teaching.

---

### Questions

1. What makes the effort of learning worthwhile?
2. How do good educators scaffold learning?
3. Why is it important to tell genuine difficulties from lack of effort?
