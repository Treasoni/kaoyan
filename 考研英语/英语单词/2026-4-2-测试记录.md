## part4: 句子翻译题 (5题)
> [!tip] 将以下句子翻译成中文

### 1. The company's profits climbed by 50% last year.

**答案**: 公司的利润去年增长了50%。

### 2. She refrained from making any comments on the sensitive topic.

**答案**: 她克制在这个敏感话题上发表表任何评论。

### 3. after years of rapid growth, the economy has reached a plateau.

**答案**: 经过多年的快速增长，经济已进入停滞期。

### 4. the city has a dense population and limited housing.

**答案**: 这座城市人口密集，住房有限。

### 5. he is skilled at negotiating complex deals.

**答案**: 他擅长谈判复杂的交易。
