- D) 安全的

The government's new policy has come under close (1)______ from opposition parties. Critics argue that the (2)______ process of implementation was not properly planned. They claim it is (3)______ that officials (4)______ with all potential risks before proceeding.

The market is already (5)______ with similar products, making it a major (6)______ for new entrants. However, the company plans to (7)______ its new line next month. The CEO believes their (8)______ approach to innovation gives them an edge over competitors who are too (9)______ in their strategies.

The primary (10)______ for this expansion comes from growing consumer demand.
