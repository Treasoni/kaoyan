## ✍️ 写作词汇库

### 替换升级词

| 原词 | 升级词 | 例句 |
|------|--------|------|
| important | **crucial / critical** | This is a **crucial** issue. |
| very good | **superb** | The performance was **superb**. |
| big change | **dramatic change** | A **dramatic** increase occurred. |
| enough | **sufficient** | **Sufficient** evidence is needed. |
| use | **exploit** (负) / **employ** | **exploit** resources |
| say/show | **suggest** | Data **suggests** a trend. |

| 搭配 | 含义 | 语域 |
|------|------|------|

| **as a last resort** | 作为最后手段 | 正式 |
