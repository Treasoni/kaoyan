## ✍️ 写作词汇库

### 议论文高频词汇

#### 表"重要"（替换 important）

| 基础词 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|--------|---------|------|
| important | **crucial** | It is **crucial** to address this issue. |
| important | **critical** | This factor is **critical** to our success. |
| important | **vital** | Education plays a **vital** role in development. |

#### 表"变化"（替换 change/increase）

| 基础词 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|--------|---------|------|
| big change | **dramatic** change | The policy led to **dramatic** changes. |
| increase | **dramatic** increase | There has been a **dramatic** increase in... |

#### 表"成功/战胜"

| 基础词 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|--------|---------|------|
| success | **triumph** | This represents a **triumph** of human spirit. |
| win | **triumph over** | They **triumphed over** numerous obstacles. |

#### 表"导致/引起"

| 基础词 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|--------|---------|------|
| cause | **trigger** | The incident **triggered** a heated debate. |
| cause | **prompt** | The crisis **prompted** immediate action. |

### 表达观点类

| 功能 | 词汇 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| 批评 | **criticize** | criticize the government's policy |
| 建议 | **suggest** | suggest that... (虚拟语气) |
| 回应 | **respond** | respond to the challenge |
| 抵制 | **resist** | resist the temptation |

## 🔗 词根词缀速记

### 前缀

| 前缀 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **super-** | 上面、超过 | superior, superficial, supervise |
| **suc-/suf-/sug-** | 在下面（sub-变体） | succeed, suffer, suggest |
| **re-** | 回、再 | respond, resist, reserve |
| **intel-** | 在...之间、理解 | intelligent, intellectual |

### 后缀

| 后缀 | 含义 | 示例 |
|------|------|------|
| **-tion/-sion** | 名词化 | succession, response, reservation |
| **-ial/-ual** | 形容词化 | superficial, crucial, dual |
| **-ity** | 名词化（性质） | superiority, authority, vulnerability |
| **-ant/-ent** | 形容词/名词 | resistant, sufficient, intellect |
