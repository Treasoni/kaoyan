### 词族详情

#### 1. efficiency 族（4个）
- **efficient** (adj.) - 高效的

> [!tip] 考研重点：efficient 与 effective 的区分
> - efficient: 强调"效率高，不浪费"
> - effective: 强调"有效果，达到预期目的"

## 📚 写作词汇库

### 议论文必备词

#### 表达观点
- **contend** (v.) - 声称，主张
  - Many scholars **contend** that...
  - Critics **contend** that...

- **argue** (v.) - 争论，主张
  - Some **argue** that...
  - It can be **argued** that...

#### 表示程度
- **decisive** (adj.) - 决定性的
  - play a **decisive** role in...
  - have a **decisive** impact on...

- **urgent** (adj.) - 紧急的
  - It is **imperative** that we **address** **urgent** issues...

#### 表示转折
- **however** (adv.) - 然而
  - **However**, some argue that...
  - **However**, this approach has limitations...

- **conversely** (adv.) - 相反地
  - **Conversely**, others suggest...

#### 表示因果关系
- **factor** (n.) - 因素
  - be a critical **factor** in...
  - various **factors** contribute to...

- **consequence** (n.) - 结果，后果
  - as a **consequence** of...
  - face the **consequences**
