## ✍️ 写作词汇库

### 议论文推荐词汇

#### 替换"认为/说"
| 基础词 | 升级词 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| think | speculate | Experts speculate that... |
| say | specify | The report specifies that... |
| show | indicate | Studies indicate that... |

#### 替换"重要/强调"
| 基础词 | 升级词 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| important | specific | It is specific to... |
| stress | emphasize | We must emphasize... |
| focus | specialize | Students should specialize in... |

#### 替换"做/行动"
| 基础词 | 升级词 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| do | engage in | Students should engage in... |
| try | endeavor | We should endeavor to... |
| make | forge | Forge a new path... |

#### 替换"困难/复杂"
| 基础词 | 升级词 | 例句 |
|--------|--------|------|
| hard | acute | The acute shortage of... |
| complex | complicated | A complicated problem... |
| bad | grim | The grim reality... |
