## 📚 写作词汇库

### 议论文必备词

#### 表达观点/态度
- **consensus** (n.) - 共识
  - reach a consensus on
  - build a broad consensus

- **consult** (v.) - 咨询、商量
  - consult with experts
  - consult sb about sth

- **withhold** (v.) - 保留、隐瞒
  - withhold information from
  - withhold judgment

#### 表示程度/状态
- **conscientious** (adj.) - 认真的、尽责的
  - a conscientious student
  - conscientious efforts

- **conspicuous** (adj.) - 显眼的
  - a conspicuous success
  - conspicuous consumption

- **predominant** (adj.) - 主要的、占优势的
  - the predominant feature
  - predominant culture

- **bleak** (adj.) - 黯淡的、凄凉的
  - a bleak outlook
  - look bleak

#### 表示行动/措施
- **preclude** (vt.) - 排除、阻止
  - preclude sb from doing sth
  - preclude the possibility of

- **insulate** (vt.) - 隔离、使绝缘
  - insulate ... from/against
  - insulate the economy from shocks

- **withstand** (vt.) - 抵抗、经得起
  - withstand pressure
  - withstand the test of time

- **consolidate** (v.) - 巩固、合并
  - consolidate one's position
  - consolidate the gains
