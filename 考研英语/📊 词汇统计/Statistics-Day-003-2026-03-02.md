## ✍️ 写作词汇库

### 议论文必备词

#### 表达观点
- **contend** (非本日词，需补充): Many scholars contend that...
- **characterize**: This trend can be **characterized** as...
- **define**: Success must be **defined** in broader terms.
- **challenge**: Some critics **challenge** this view.

#### 表示程度
- **beneficial**: be **beneficial** to...
- **definite**: a **definite** conclusion
- **delicate**: a **delicate** balance

#### 表示因果关系
- **benefit**: **benefit** from...
- **access**: **have access to**...

#### 表示转折
- **challenge**: **challenge** the assumption
- **defy**: **defy** conventional wisdom

### 高级替换表

| 初级表达 | 高级替换 | 使用场景 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| good for | **beneficial to** | 议论文 |
| very careful | **deliberate** | 正式写作 |
| describe as | **characterize as** | 学术写作 |
| oppose | **defy** | 强烈反对 |
| get | **have access to** | 正式表达 |
| start | **begin / inaugurate** | 开场表达 |

## 💡 记忆策略

### 词族记忆口诀

> **deliver 族**: deliver (传递) → delivery (名词) → delicate (精致的) → deliberate (故意的) → delay (延迟)
>
> **def- 族**: define (定义) → definite (确定的) → definition (定义的名词) → defend (防卫) → defy (反抗)
>
> **benefit 族**: benefit (好处) → beneficial (有益的) → benevolent (仁慈的) → benign (良性的)
>
> **character 族**: character (性格) → characteristic (特点) → characterize (以...为特征)

### 搭配记忆法

- **deliver on sth.** - 兑现承诺
- **defy description** - 难以形容
- **beneficial to** - 对...有益
- **characterize as** - 描述为...
- **have access to** - 有权使用
- **on behalf of** - 代表...
- **withdraw from** - 从...退出
