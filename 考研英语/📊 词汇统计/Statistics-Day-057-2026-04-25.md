## 六、易混淆词组

| 词组 | 辨析要点 |
|------|---------|
| **used to do** vs **be used to doing** vs **be used to do** | used to do = 过去常常；be used to doing = 习惯于；be used to do = 被用来做 |
| **have a word with** vs **have words with** | have a word with = 谈一谈；have words with = 争吵 |
| **think about** vs **think of** vs **think over** | think about = 考虑；think of = 想到/认为；think over = 仔细考虑 |
| **be tired of** vs **be tired from** | be tired of = 厌倦（心理）；be tired from = 因...而疲劳（生理） |
| **wish** vs **hope** | wish + 虚拟语气（难以实现）；hope + 真实语气（可能实现） |

### 注意辨析
- used to do vs be used to doing vs be used to do（考研语法高频考点）

- wish vs hope（虚拟语气 vs 真实语气）
