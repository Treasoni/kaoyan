## Part A：词义辨析（5题)

```

### Q1. The word "viable" in the context of "a viable business model" most closely means:
A. successful
B. feasible
C. valuable
D. variable
```
**答案**： B. feasible

---

### Q2. The word "reconcile" in the context of "reconcile the conflicting views" most closely means:
A. separate
B. combine
C. harmonize
D. resolve
```
**答案**： C. harmonize

---

### Q3. The word "rectify" in the context of "rectify the injustice" most closely means:
A. understand
B. correct
C. accept
D. ignore
```
**答案**： B. correct

---

### Q4. The word "mask" in the context of "mask the truth" most closely means:
A. hide
B. cover
C. protect
D. decorate
```
**答案**： A. hide

---

### Q5. The word "pitch" in the context of "sales pitch" most closely means:
A. throw
B. presentation
C. level
D. tone
```
**答案**： B. presentation

---

## Part B：僻义识别（5题）
```

### Q6. In academic writing, "plagiarism" refers to:
A. using quotations without citation
B. copying ideas without attribution
C. poor grammar
D. weak arguments
```
**答案**： B. copying ideas without attribution

---

### Q7. The word "plant" in "manufacturing plant" is used as:
A. a type of vegetation
b. a factory
c. a gardening tool
d. a power source
```
**答案**： B. a factory

---

### Q8. "The situation warrants immediate action" - here "warrant" means:
A. requires
B. promises
C. guarantees
D. deserves
```
**答案**： D. deserves

---

### Q9. The coach coached the team to victory" - here "coach" is used as:
A. a vehicle
b. trained
c. instructed
d. shouted at
```
**答案**： b. trained

---

### Q10. "they made the data redundant" - here "redundant" means:
A. important
B. unnecessary
C. complex
d. outdated
```
**答案**： b. unnecessary
```
---

## Part C：搭配选择（5题）
```

### Q11. Choose the correct phrase to complete the sentence:
```
We need to find a **_____** to this problem.
A. viable solution
B. viable answer
C. viable question
D. viable method
```
**答案**： A. viable solution

---

### Q12. The government plans to **_____** more money into the economy.
A. inject
B. inject with
C. inject into
D. inject for
```
**答案**： C. inject into

---

### Q13. He has **_____** of failures in his career.
A. a catalogue
B. a list
C. a series
d. a collection
```
**答案**： A. a catalogue

---

### Q14. We must not **_____** to these warning signs.
A. turn a blind eye
B. turn a deaf ear
c. close our eyes
d. shut our ears
```
**答案**： B. turn a deaf ear

---

### Q15. The company **_____** last year due to poor management.
A. sank
B. tanked
C. failed
D. collapsed
```
**答案**： B. tanked
