## Part 3: 辨析题（5题）

区分每组词的不同用法。

**1.** some time vs sometime vs sometimes vs somewhat
- I'll finish the report __________ next week.（某时）
- We __________ go hiking on weekends.（有时）
- It took me __________ to understand the concept.（一段时间）
- The result was __________ disappointing.（有点儿）

**2.** stop doing vs stop to do
- He stopped __________ (smoke) and started working.（停止吸烟）
- He stopped __________ (smoke) a cigarette during the break.（停下来抽烟）

**3.** spend...doing vs spend...on
- She spent three hours __________ (read) the report.（做某事花费）
- She spent $50 __________ books.（在某事上花费）

**4.** speak vs say vs tell vs talk
- He __________ English fluently.（说某种语言）
- She __________ that she would be late.（说出具体内容）
- Please __________ me the truth.（告诉某人某事）
- Let's __________ about the plan.（谈论）

**5.** right (正确) vs right (右边) vs right (权利) vs right (纠正)
- You have the __________ to remain silent.（权利）
- Turn __________ at the next intersection.（右边）
- Her answer is __________.（正确的）
- We must __________ the wrongs of the past.（纠正）
