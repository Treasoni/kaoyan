## 🔍 Part II: 词义辨析

**说明**: 选择最合适的词填空。

**11. ______ you like it or not, the decision has been made.**
A. Whether  B. Until  C. Thus  D. Upon

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. Whether** — whether...or not 无论是否
</details>

---

**12. The athlete trained with ______ energy, determined to win the ______.**
A. vigorous / victory  B. strict / story  C. stupid / task  D. wary / truth

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. vigorous / victory** — vigorous energy 精力旺盛；win the victory 赢得胜利
</details>

---

**13. The witness gave her ______ in court, providing crucial evidence.**
A. textbook  B. testimony  C. timber  D. temperament

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. testimony** — give testimony 作证
</details>

---

**14. The teacher was very ______ about deadlines — no exceptions.**
A. stupid  B. wary  C. strict  D. warm

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**C. strict** — be strict about 对...严格
</details>

---

**15. The company decided to ______ the report into three key points.**
A. visit  B. summarize  C. wait  D. store

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. summarize** — summarize...into... 把...总结为...
</details>

---

**16. The ______ of the football match was announced at the press conference.**
A. venue  B. vase  C. voltage  D. visa

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. venue** — venue of the match 比赛场地
</details>

---

**17. Be ______ of strangers offering free gifts.**
A. sure  B. stupid  C. wary  D. warm

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**C. wary** — be wary of 对...警惕
</details>

---

**18. She has been ______ to getting up early since she started the new job.**
A. used  B. sure  C. strict  D. wary

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. used** — be used to doing 习惯于做某事
</details>

---

**19. The farmer ______ the soil before planting the seeds.**
A. tiled  B. tilled  C. toured  D. tasked

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. tilled** — till the soil 耕作土壤
</details>

---

**20. He ______ his savings in a secret bank account.**
A. squirreled  B. spooned  C. sponged  D. steeled

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

</details>

## ⚠️ Part III: 僻义识别

**说明**: 判断下列单词的考研僻义。

**21. till 除了"直到"还有什么含义和词性？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vt. 耕作，犁地（例：till the soil 耕作土壤）
</details>

---

**22. squirrel 除了"松鼠"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vt. 储藏，贮存（squirrel away 储藏起来）
</details>

---

**23. tail 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vt. 跟踪，尾随（例：The police tailed the suspect for three days.）
</details>

---

**24. steel 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vt. 下决心应付，坚定地做（例：steel oneself to do sth 下定决心做某事）
</details>

---

**25. sponge 除了"海绵"还有什么僻义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vi. 吃白食，揩油，蹭��吃）（例：He's been sponging off his friends for months.）
</details>

---

**26. used to do 和 be used to doing 的区别是什么？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
used to do = 过去经常做（现在已经不做了）；be used to doing = 习惯于做某事
</details>

---

**27. whether 和 if 引导宾语从句时有什么区别？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
whether 后可接 or not，if 不行；whether 可引导主语从句、表语从句、同位语从句，if 不行；whether 可用于介词之后，if 不行
</details>

---

**28. story 和 storey 的区别是什么？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
story = 故事；storey = 楼层（美式英语中均拼作 story，但英式英语区分）
</details>

---

**29. stool 除了"凳子"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 大便，粪便（医学语境：stool test 验粪）
</details>

---

**30. testimony 的常见搭配是什么？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
be testimony to sth 是...的证明；give testimony 作证；bear testimony 证明
</details>
