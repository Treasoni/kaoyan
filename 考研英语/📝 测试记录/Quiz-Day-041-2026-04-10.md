## Part B: 僻义辨析（5题）

选择单词在特定语境中的正确含义：

**1. "He was **drafted** into the army during the war."**
- A. 起草文件  B. 征兵，征召入伍  C. 画草图  D. 草稿
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 征兵，征召入伍（draft常见义为"草稿"，此处僻义为"征兵"，be drafted into 被征召入伍）

**2. "The old man suffered a severe **stroke** and was rushed to hospital."**
- A. 击球  B. 轻抚  C. 中风  D. 一笔
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 中风（stroke常见义为"击球/轻抚"，此处僻义为"中风"，suffer a stroke 患中风）

**3. "She spoke with deep **conviction** about the need for reform."**
- A. 定罪  B. 坚定的信念  C. 说服  D. 判决
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 坚定的信念（conviction常见义为"定罪"，此处僻义为"坚定的信念"，with conviction 坚定地）

**4. "The politician used every **craft** to avoid answering the question."**
- A. 手艺  B. 飞行器  C. 诡计，手腕  D. 工艺品
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 诡计，手腕（craft常见义为"手艺"，此处僻义为"诡计，手腕"，强调狡猾手段）

**5. "The company **froze** all its assets abroad due to the sanctions."**
- A. 结冰  B. 冻僵  C. 冻结（资产）  D. 冷冻保存
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 冻结（资产）（freeze常见义为"结冰"，此处僻义为"冻结资产"，freeze assets 冻结资产）

## Part C: 完形填空（5题）

选择最合适的词填入空白处：

**1.** The government decided to ______ the old temple, which had remained ______ through two world wars.
A. destroy...intact  B. preserve...intact  C. damage...transparent  D. abandon...sturdy

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. preserve...intact（决定保护那座历经两次世界大战仍完好无损的古庙，intact表示"完好无损的"）

**2.** The prime minister ______ to find a solution to the economic crisis, despite fierce ______ from opposition parties.
A. contrived...friction  B. stumbled...strife  C. strived...interference  D. contemplated...transition

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. strived...interference（首相努力寻求经济危机的解决方案，尽管遭到反对党的强烈干预。strive努力，interference干预）

**3.** The new policy aims to make public transportation more ______ for commuters.
A. frequent  B. convenient  C. sturdy  D. brutal

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. convenient（新政策旨在使公共交通对通勤者更加便利，convenient便利的）

**4.** The investigation revealed widespread ______ in the company's financial records, leading to an official ______.
A. framework...drone  B. fraud...audit  C. friction...expenditure  D. freight...transition

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. fraud...audit（调查显示公司财务记录中存在广泛的欺诈行为，引发正式审计。fraud欺诈，audit审计）

**5.** The expedition team faced ______ weather conditions but managed to reach the summit through sheer ______.
A. explicit...draft  B. brutal...struggle  C. frequent...buffet  D. transparent...craft

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. brutal...struggle（探险队面临恶劣的天气条件，但凭借艰苦的奋斗成功登顶。brutal恶劣的，struggle奋斗）

## Part D: 词族联想（5题）

根据给定词根，写出相关单词：

**1. con-族** (共同/确信)
- ______ (vt. 使确信，说服)
- ______ (n. 定罪；坚定的信念)
- ______ (vt. 使改变信仰；转变)
- ______ (n. 对话)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> convince, conviction, convert, conversation

**2. inter-族** (在...之间)
- ______ (vi. 介入，干涉)
- ______ (n. 干涉，干预)
- ______ (n. 接口，界面)
- ______ (adj. 完好无损的)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> intervene, interference, interface, intact

**3. trans-族** (跨越/转变)
- ______ (n. 过渡，转变)
- ______ (v. 翻译；转化)
- ______ (vt. 超越，超出)
- ______ (adj. 透明的；显而易见的)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> transition, translate, transcend, transparent

**4. cr-族** (crawl/craft/crash族)
- ______ (vi. 爬行，缓慢移动)
- ______ (n. 手艺；诡计)
- ______ (v. 碰撞，坠毁)
- ______ (v. 破裂， crack open)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> crawl, craft, crash, crack

**5. dr-族** (drift/drone/drown族)
- ______ (v. 漂流，漂移)
- ______ (n. 无人机；嗡嗡声)
- ______ (v. 淹没，溺死)
- ______ (n. 旱灾，干旱)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> drift, drone, drown, drought
