## Part B: 僻义辨析（5题）

选择单词在特定语境��的正确含义：

**1. "She ordered a **custom** suit from the tailor on Savile Row."**
- A. 风俗，习俗  B. 定做的，定制的  C. 海关  D. 顾客
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 定做的，定制的（custom常见义为"风俗/习俗"，此处僻义为"定制的"，custom suit = 定做的西装，custom-made的缩略用法）

**2. "Long-submerged resentments finally **surfaced** during the family meeting."**
- A. 飞到空中  B. 给...铺路面  C. 浮出水面，显露  D. 抹去表面
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 浮出水面，显露（surface常见义为名词"表面"，此处僻义为动词"浮出水面/显露"，表示隐藏的情感或事实被揭露）

**3. "The president was accompanied by several **attendant** risks."**
- A. 伴随的，随之而来的  B. 服务员，侍者  C. 出席者  D. 细心的
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> A. 伴随的，随之而来的（attendant常见义为名词"服务员/侍者"，此处僻义为形容词"伴随的"，attendant risks = 随之而来的风险）

**4. "He opened the **trunk** of the car and loaded the groceries."**
- A. 树干  B. 躯干  C. 后备箱  D. 象鼻
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 后备箱（trunk常见义为"树干"，此处僻义为"汽车后备箱"，是美式英语中常用含义）

**5. "The pitcher threw a nasty **curve** that the batter completely missed."**
- A. 弯曲的路线  B. 曲线球  C. 曲线图表  D. 拐弯处
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 曲线球（curve常见义为"曲线"，此处僻义为棒球术语"曲线球"，指投手投出的弧线球，也引申为"出人意料的手段"）

## Part C: 完形填空（5题）

选择最合适的词填入空白处：

**1.** The new traffic regulations aim to ______ the number of accidents on rural roads, which have been ______ in recent months.
A. curb...multiplying  B. cure...surging  C. burn...cycling  D. sweep...submerging

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> A. curb...multiplying（新交通法规旨在控制乡村道路事故数量，这些事故近几个月在成倍增加。curb控制/抑制，multiply成倍增加）

**2.** The ______ pulled the woman into a dark alley and stole her ______ containing all her savings.
A. burglar...purse  B. attendant...pulse  C. puppet...bundle  D. attorney...tube

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> A. burglar...purse（窃贼将那个女人拽进暗巷，抢走了装着她所有积蓄的钱包。burglar窃贼，purse钱包）

**3.** The government needs to simplify its ______ procedures; the current ______ is driving away potential investors.
A. tropical...rumour  B. municipal...criterion  C. bureaucratic...bureaucracy  D. synthetic...symptom

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. bureaucratic...bureaucracy（政府需要简化其官僚程序；当前的官僚体制正在赶走潜在投资者。bureaucratic官僚的，bureaucracy官僚体制）

**4.** After the earthquake, a massive ______ of support ______ across the nation, with donations pouring in from every city.
A. symptom...swept  B. wave...surged  C. curve...rushed  D. syndrome...swam

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. wave...surged（地震后，一股巨大的支援浪潮席卷全国，各城市纷纷捐款涌入。surge涌动/激增）

**5.** The surgeon was ______ for the operation, arriving precisely at the scheduled time, which reflected his ______ attitude toward every procedure.
A. punctual...professional  B. sudden...troublesome  C. swift...criminal  D. rural...cunning

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> A. punctual...professional（外科医生准时到达进行手术，准时到场反映了他对每台手术的专业态度。punctual准时的）

- ______ (n. 交响乐)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案

- ______ (n. 曲线；v. 弯曲)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
