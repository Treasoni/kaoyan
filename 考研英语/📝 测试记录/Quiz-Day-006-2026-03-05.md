## Part 1: 词义选择 (1-8)

### 1. particular
The professor is very **particular** about the formatting of research papers.
- [ ] A. 特别的，特殊的
- [ ] B. 特定的，专指的；挑剔的
- [ ] C. 普遍的，一般的
- [ ] D. 私人的，个人的

### 2. manner
The committee handled the complaint **in a professional manner**.
- [ ] A. 礼貌，规矩
- [ ] B. 方式，方法
- [ ] C. 态度，举止
- [ ] D. 风格，时尚

### 3. manifest
The symptoms of the disease **manifested** themselves within 48 hours.
- [ ] A. 掩盖，隐藏
- [ ] B. 显现，表明
- [ ] C. 加重，恶化
- [ ] D. 缓解，减轻

### 4. lead (⚠️ 僻义预警)
The investigation finally found a new **lead** in the case.
- [ ] A. 领导地位
- [ ] B. 铅（金属）
- [ ] C. 线索，头绪
- [ ] D. 榜样

### 5. manage
Despite the difficulties, she **managed to** complete the project on time.
- [ ] A. 管理，经营
- [ ] B. 设法做到，成功完成
- [ ] C. 控制，操纵
- [ ] D. 应付，对付

### 6. image
The scandal severely damaged the company's public **image**.
- [ ] A. 图像，画面
- [ ] B. 镜像，副本
- [ ] C. 形象，声誉
- [ ] D. 想象，幻想

### 7. legitimate
The court(法院) ruled that the company had a **legitimate** claim to the patent.
- [ ] A. 合法的，正当的
- [ ] B. 合理的，适当的
- [ ] C. 真实的，确实的
- [ ] D. 重要的，重大的

### 8. illustrate
The professor **illustrated** his theory with several case studies.
- [ ] A. 插图，配图
- [ ] B. (举例) 说明，阐明
- [ ] C. 证明，证实
- [ ] D. 描述，叙述
