## Part A：词义辨析（5题）

### Q1. The word "tackle" in the context of "tackle the problem" most closely means:
- A) kick the problem
- B) **deal with the problem**
- C) ignore the problem
- D) create the problem
---
### Q2. In academic contexts, "school" often refers to:
- A) a building for education
- B) a group of fish
- C) **a particular way of thinking or a set of theories**
- D) a period of instruction
---
### Q3. The phrase "a vehicle for change" means:
- A) a car used for transportation
- B) **a means or instrument for achieving change**
- C) a type of bicycle
- D) a political party
---
### Q4. When a business "tanks", it:
- A) fills up with water
- B) attacks its competitors
- C) **fails completely or goes bankrupt**
- D) buys military equipment
---
### Q5. "A catalogue of disasters" implies:
- A) a book listing products
- B) **a series or list of unfortunate events**
- C) an organized collection
- D) a type of magazine
---

## Part B：僻义识别（5题）
### Q6. In "the recession fears receded," the second "receded" means:
- A) went backwards
- B) **diminished or weakened**
- C) withdrew formally
- D) moved further away
---
### Q7. "The evidence was barely tangible" suggests the evidence was:
- A) edible
- B) **barely real or concrete**
- C) very heavy
- D) touchable
---
### Q8. "Scarcely had he arrived when..." is closest in meaning to:
- A) he arrived loudly
- B) **he had hardly arrived when...**
- C) he arrived often
- D) he arrived late
---
### Q9. In "the badge of poverty," "badge" means:
- A) a piece of metal worn on clothing
- B) **a symbol or sign**
- C) a type of pass
- D) a police identification
---
### Q10. "Turn a deaf ear to criticism" means:
- A) listen carefully to criticism
- B) **refuse to listen to criticism**
- C) become deaf from criticism
- D) respond angrily to criticism
---

## Part C：搭配选择（5题）
### Q11. Complete the phrase: "______ for disaster"
- A) A menu
- B) **A recipe**
- C) A formula
- D) A plan
---
### Q12. Which phrase means "to become less available or more difficult to find"?
- A) abound in
- B) **be scarce**
- C) be abundant
- D) be familiar
---
### Q13. "Work to a deadline" means:
- A) work without any time limit
- B) **complete work by a specified time**
- C) work slowly
- D) work on a newspaper
---
### Q14. Which word best completes: "The Great ______ of 2008"?
- A) Depression
- B) **Recession**
- C) Regression
- D) Recession
---
### Q15. "Rise in ______ against the government" uses:
- A) rebel
- B) **rebellion**
- C) recede
- D) receipt
---

## 📋 答案与解析
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|:----:|:----:|------|
| Q1 | **B** | tackle = 处理，解决 |
| Q2 | **C** | school = 学派，流派（僻义） |
| Q3 | **B** | vehicle = 工具，手段（僻义） |
| Q4 | **C** | tank = 彻底失败，倒闭（僻义） |
| Q5 | **B** | catalogue = 一连串（坏事）（僻义） |
| Q6 | **B** | recede = 减弱，消退（僻义） |
| Q7 | **B** | tangible = 真实的，可感知的 |
| Q8 | **B** | scarcely = 几乎不 = hardly |
| Q9 | **B** | badge = 象征，标志（僻义） |
| Q10 | **B** | turn a deaf ear = 充耳不闻 |
| Q11 | **B** | a recipe for disaster = 导致灾难的因素 |
| Q12 | **B** | be scarce = 变得稀缺 |
| Q13 | **B** | work to a deadline = 按期限完成工作 |
| Q14 | **B** | Great Recession = 大衰退 |
| Q15 | **B** | rise in rebellion = 发动叛乱 |
