## Part A：词义选择题（5题）

**1. The research team made an ______ effort to verify the experimental results.**
- A) faint
- B) earnest
- C) idle
- D) vague

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) earnest**
> earnest = 认真的，真诚的
> make an earnest effort = 做出认真的努力
</details>

---

**2. The company's ______ performance exceeded market expectations.**
- A) internal
- B) eternal
- C) external
- D) infernal

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：A) internal**
> internal = 内部的
> internal performance = 内部绩效/表现
</details>

---

**3. Several ______ have been proposed to address urban traffic congestion.**
- A) facilities
- B) faculties
- C) formulas
- D) schemes

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：D) schemes**
> scheme = 计划，方案
> proposed schemes = 提出的方案
</details>

---

**4. The new policy was designed to ______ economic growth in rural areas.**
- A) fascinate
- B) facilitate
- C) fabricate
- D) fluctuate

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) facilitate**
> facilitate = 促进，使便利
> facilitate economic growth = 促进经济增长
</details>

---

**5. The committee's decision was influenced by multiple ______ factors.**
- A) factor
- B) faction
- C) fracture
- D) friction

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：A) factor（此处应为 factors）**
> factor = 因素
> multiple factors = 多种因素
> 注：题目中应使用复数形式 factors
</details>

## Part B：搭配填空题（5题）

**6. The government should implement effective ______ to protect consumer rights.**
> 请填写：measures / facilities / schemes

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：measures**
> effective measures = 有效措施
> implement measures = 实施措施
</details>

---

**7. Her ______ for languages enabled her to master three foreign languages within two years.**
> 请填写：faculty / facility / familiarity

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：faculty**

> faculty for languages = 语言天赋
</details>

**8. The study aims to ______ the relationship between sleep quality and academic performance.**
> 请填写：elicit / facilitate / elaborate

</details>

**9. The new software will ______ the workflow and reduce manual errors.**

## Part C：僻义词专项测试（5题）

**11. The word "faculty" most likely means ______ in "the faculty of reason."**
- A) 全体教员
- B) 能力，官能
- C) 学院
- D) 设备

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) 能力，官能**
> faculty 常见义：全体教员、学院
> 僻义：官能，能力（the faculty of reason = 理性能力）
</details>

---

**12. In "The project is still in embryo," the word "embryo" means:**
- A) 胚胎
- B) 萌芽阶段
- C) 生物实验
- D) 完成状态

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) 萌芽阶段**
> embryo 常见义：胚胎
> 僻义/引申义：萌芽阶段，未发展成熟的状态
> in embryo = 在萌芽中，在酝酿中
</details>

---

**13. The word "elicit" in "elicit a response" means:**
- A) 逃避
- B) 引出，诱发
- C) 删除
- D) 合法化

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) 引出，诱发**
> elicit = 引出，诱出，探出
> elicit a response = 引发回应
</details>

---

**14. In "generalize the findings," "generalize" means:**
- A) 普遍化，推广
- B) 将军化
- C) 专门化
- D) 局部化

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：A) 普遍化，推广**
> generalize = 概括，推广，归纳
> generalize the findings = 推广研究发现/概括结论
</details>

---

**15. The word "scheme" in "a pension scheme" refers to:**
- A) 阴谋
- B) 计划，方案
- C) 图表
- D) 时间表

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>
**答案：B) 计划，方案**
> scheme 常见义：计划，方案（中性词）
> 注：scheme 有时带有"阴谋"含义，但在 "pension scheme" 中指养老金计划
</details>
