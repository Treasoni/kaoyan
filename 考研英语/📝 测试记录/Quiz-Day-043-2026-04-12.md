## Part 2: 选词填空（10题）

从以下词库中选出最合适的词填入空白处（每个词只能用一次）：

**词库：** overtime, overflow, overthrow, humid, hurricane, await, rouse, summon, prone, rotten, rotate, crowd, extract, funeral, automation

**1.** The factory introduced a new ______ system to reduce labor costs on the assembly line.

**2.** The southern coast was devastated by a powerful ______ that destroyed hundreds of homes.

**3.** Workers are demanding higher ______ pay for the extra hours they put in during the holiday season.

**4.** The river began to ______ its banks after three days of heavy rain.

**5.** The rebels attempted to ______ the government and establish a new regime.

**6.** The weather was extremely ______, making everyone feel uncomfortable and sticky.

**7.** We ______ your reply with great interest and look forward to hearing from you soon.

**8.** The speaker tried to ______ the audience to action with his passionate speech about climate change.

**9.** The lawyer managed to ______ a confession from the reluctant witness during the cross-examination.

**10.** The smell of ______ food in the refrigerator made her lose her appetite entirely.

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> 1. automation（工厂引入新的自动化系统以降低装配线上的劳动力成本）
> 2. hurricane（南部海岸被一场摧毁了数百户住宅的强飓风破坏）
> 3. overtime（工人们要求提高假期加班期间的加班费）
> 4. overflow（连续三天暴雨后，河水开始漫过河岸）
> 5. overthrow（叛军试图推翻政府并建立新政权）
> 6. humid（天气极其潮湿，让每个人都感到不舒服）
> 7. await（我们怀着极大的兴趣等待你的回复）
> 8. rouse（演讲者试图用关于气候变化的激情演讲唤起听众行动起来）
> 9. extract（律师在盘问中设法从不愿配合的证人那里提取了供词）
> 10. rotten（冰箱里腐烂食物的气味让她完全失去了食欲）

## Part 3: 僻义辨析（6题）

选择单词在特定语境中的正确含义：

**1. "Everyone attended the meeting **but** the department head."**
- A. 但是，然而  B. 除了  C. 仅仅  D. 几乎
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 除了（but常见义为连词"但是/然而"，此处僻义为介词"除了"，相当于except。everyone...but... = 除了...之外所有人都...）

**2. "The discovery **fueled** a debate that had been simmering for decades."**
- A. 加燃料  B. 加剧，激起  C. 燃烧  D. 提供动力
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 加剧，激起（fuel常见义为名词"燃料"，此处僻义为动词"加剧/激起"，fuel a debate = 激起/加剧一场辩论）

**3. "The defendant was **summoned** to appear before the magistrate's court next Monday."**
- A. 召唤（呼唤过来）  B. 传唤（出庭）  C. 激起勇气  D. 集合
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 传唤（出庭）（summon常见义为"召唤/鼓起"，此处僻义为法律用语"传唤出庭"，be summoned to appear before the court 被传唤出庭）

**4. "The research team is working at the **frontier** of artificial intelligence."**
- A. 国界，边境  B. 尖端，前沿  C. 前线  D. 边远地区
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 尖端，前沿（frontier常见义为"国界/边境"，此处僻义为"（学科的）尖端/前沿"，at the frontier of AI = 在人工智能的前沿领域）

**5. "New problems kept **cropping** up throughout the project."**
- A. 种植庄稼  B. 收获  C. 突然出现  D. 剪短
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 突然出现（crop常见义为名词"庄稼/收成"，此处僻义为动词词组crop up = 突然出现/意外发生）

**6. "The region is **prone** to earthquakes, experiencing tremors several times a year."**
- A. 俯卧的  B. 易于遭受...的  C. 倾斜的  D. 接近的
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> B. 易于遭受...的（prone常见搭配为"俯卧"lie prone，此处僻义为"易于遭受/有...倾向"，be prone to = 易于遭受...）

**补充：bonus 僻义题**

**7. "She was appointed as **extraordinary** ambassador to the United Nations."**
- A. 非凡的，特别的  B. 令人惊奇的  C. 特派的  D. 不平常的
> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> C. 特派的（extraordinary常见义为"非凡的/令人惊奇的"，此处僻义为外交用语"特派的"，extraordinary ambassador = 特派大使）

- ______ (v. 漫出；溢出)
- ______ (v. 超过；赶上；压倒)
- ______ (vt. 推翻；打倒)
- ______ (adj. 公开的；明显的)

> [!note]- 🔑 答案
> overtime, overhaul, overnight, overhear, overhead, overflow, overtake, overthrow, overt
