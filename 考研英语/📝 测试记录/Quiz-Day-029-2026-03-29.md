## ✅ 参考答案

### Part 1: 词义选择题
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|------|------|------|
| 1 | B | accord在此处为vt. 给予（僻义） |
| 2 | D | faculty不含"建筑物"之意 |
| 3 | B | facilitate意为"使容易、促进" |
| 4 | B | language barrier指语言障碍 |
| 5 | C | carry out意为"实施、执行" |
| 6 | B | handy意为"便利的、好用的" |
| 7 | B | halt意为"停止" |
| 8 | B | traffic jam意为"交通拥堵" |
| 9 | B | bankrupt意为"破产的" |
| 10 | C | fabulous意为"极好的" |
| 11 | B | campus指"校园" |
| 12 | B | ecology研究"生态、环境" |
| 13 | C | eager意为"渴望的、热切的" |
| 14 | B | barrier指"障碍" |
| 15 | B | ease意为"减轻、缓解" |

### Part 2: 词组搭配题
| 题号 | 答案 | 完整句子 |
|------|------|----------|
| 16 | facilitate | The new policy will **facilitate** trade... |
| 17 | carry out | Students should **carry out** their research... |
| 18 | in accordance with | We must act **in accordance with**... |
| 19 | come in handy | These skills will **come in handy**... |
| 20 | language barrier | ...face a **language barrier**... |
| 21 | accord | The professor **accord** great importance... |
| 22 | traffic jam | I'm stuck in a **traffic jam**... |
| 23 | gather | Let's **gather** some information... |
| 24 | accord (or: allocate) | ...should **accord** more funding... |
| 25 | halt | Please **halt** your work... |

### Part 3: 僻义预警测试
| 题号 | 答案 | 解析 |
|------|------|------|
| 26 | B | of one's own accord = 自愿地 |
| 27 | B | faculty of hearing = 听觉能力（天赋） |
| 28 | B | cancel out = 抵消、中和 |
| 29 | B | by accident = 意外地、偶然地 |
| 30 | B | accord作vt.时常考"给予"义 |
