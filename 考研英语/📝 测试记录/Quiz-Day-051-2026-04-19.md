## 🔍 Part II: 词义辨析

**说明**: 选择最合适的词填空。

**11. The team made steady ______ on the research project.**
A. prepare  B. progress  C. prefer  D. pretend

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. progress** — make progress 意为"取得进展"
</details>

---

**12. She would ______ stay at home than go to the party.**
A. rather  B. real  C. soon  D. seldom

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. rather** — would rather...than... 宁愿...而不...
</details>

---

**13. The government needs to ______ up the economy during the crisis.**
A. speed  B. shore  C. shout  D. slave

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. shore** — shore up 意为"支撑，加固"
</details>

---

**14. Prices have ______ in recent months, causing concern among consumers.**
A. rocketed  B. slaved  C. shrugged  D. searched

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. rocketed** — rocket vi. 猛增
</details>

---

**15. He ______ through the drawers looking for his lost keys.**
A. rifle  B. rifled  C. return  D. recorded

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. rifled** — rifle through 快速翻找
</details>

---

**16. The scientist studied the animal ______ in the rainforest.**
A. population  B. pottery  C. prisoner  D. pupil

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. population** — population 此处指"种群"
</details>

---

**17. ______ have I seen such a ______ performance.**
A. Seldom / spectacular  B. Seldom / soft  C. Soon / salient  D. Rather / real

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. Seldom / spectacular** — seldom 置于句首倒装；spectacular 壮观的
</details>

---

**18. I ______ to inform you that your application has been rejected.**
A. regret  B. prefer  C. prepare  D. pretend

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. regret** — regret to do 遗憾要做某事（正式通知用法）
</details>

---

**19. The spider ______ its web near the pond every morning.**
A. speeds  B. spins  C. slaves  D. shores

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. spins** — spider spins a web 蜘蛛织网（虽然spin不在本日单词中，但这是固定搭配）
</details>

---

**20. After the storm, a beautiful ______ appeared in the sky.**
A. rainbow  B. radar  C. reptile  D. razor

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. rainbow** — rainbow 彩虹
</details>

## ⚠️ Part III: 僻义识别

**说明**: 判断下列单词的考研僻义。

**21. slave 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
拼命地工作；辛苦地干（slave away at sth）
</details>

---

**22. shore 作动词时的核心搭配是什么？含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
shore up — 支撑，加固（shore up the economy 支撑经济）
</details>

---

**23. rocket 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vi. 猛增；迅速成功（prices rocketed 价格猛涨；rocket to fame 一夜成名）
</details>

---

**24. rifle 作动词时是什么意思？核心搭配？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
快速翻找，搜寻（rifle through the drawers 快速翻找抽屉）
</details>

---

**25. pupil 的两个完全不同的含义分别是什么？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
① 小学生 ② 瞳孔（注意：两个含义源自不同的拉丁词根）
</details>

---

**26. regret to do 和 regret doing 的区别是什么？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
regret to do = 遗憾要做（事还没做）；regret doing = 后悔做过（事已做完）
</details>

---

**27. seldom 置于句首时有什么语法要求？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
主句需要倒装。例：Seldom have I seen such a beautiful sunset.
</details>

---

**28. population 除了"人口"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 种群（biology/ecology语境：animal population 动物种群）
</details>

---

**29. seed 除了"种子"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 种子选手（seeded player）；比喻义"萌芽"（seeds of change 变革的萌芽）
</details>

---

**30. speed 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
vt. 加速，促进（speed up the process 加速进程）；vi. 快速前行
</details>
