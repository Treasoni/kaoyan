## Part A：词义辨析（5题）

### Q1. The word "alleviate" in "alleviate the burden" most closely means:
A. increase
B. reduce
C. create
D. maintain

**答案**： B. reduce

---

### Q2. The word "simultaneous" in "simultaneous efforts" most closely means:
A. consecutive
B. concurrent
C. separate
D. delayed

**答案**： B. concurrent

---

### Q3. The word "redundant" in "made redundant" most closely means:
A. promoted
B. unnecessary
C. essential
D. transferred

**答案**： B. unnecessary

---

### Q4. The word "mechanism" in "the mechanism of action" most closely means:
A. tool
B. process
C. result
D. goal

**答案**： B. process

---

### Q5. The word "analytical" in "analytical skills" most closely means:
A. creative
B. logical
C. practical
D. emotional

**答案**： B. logical

---

## Part B：僻义识别（5题）

### Q6. In "lean management", "lean" means:
A. fat
B. efficient
C. wasteful
D. slow

**答案**： B. efficient

---

### Q7. "The situation warrants attention" - here "warrant" means:
A. prevent
B. deserve
C. avoid
D. delay

**答案**： B. deserve

---

### Q8. "Upright citizen" - here "upright" means:
A. tall
B. honest
C. standing
D. strict

**答案**： B. honest

---

### Q9. "Singular achievement" - here "singular" means:
A. single
B. remarkable
C. ordinary
D. rare

**答案**： B. remarkable

---

### Q10. "Simulate interest" - here "simulate" means:
A. show genuine
B. pretend
C. hide
D. lose

**答案**： B. pretend

---

## Part C：搭配选择（5题）

### Q11. Choose the correct phrase:
We need to draw an **_____** between the two situations.
A. analogy
B. analysis
C. answer
D. argument

**答案**： A. analogy

---

### Q12. Complete the sentence:
**_____** of the cost, we must complete the project.
A. Regarding
B. Regardless
C. Respect
D. Related

**答案**： B. Regardless

---

### Q13. Choose the correct phrase:
The company formed a strategic **_____** with its competitor.
A. alliance
B. allowance
C. allegiance
D. alone

**答案**： A. alliance

---

### Q14. Complete the sentence:
In the **_____** analysis, success depends on hard work.
A. final
B. first
C. last
D. initial

**答案**： A. final

---

### Q15. Choose the correct word:
He was **_____** to the board of directors.
A. nominated
B. named
C. noted
D. numbered

**答案**： A. nominated
