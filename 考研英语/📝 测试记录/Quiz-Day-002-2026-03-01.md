## Part 2: 搭配填空题

**6. The committee failed to _____ the concerns raised by the public.**
   - [ ] A. elicit
   - [ ] B. embody
   - [ ] C. address
   - [ ] D. embrace

> [!answer] 答案：C（address - 处理，解决）⚠️ 僻义词

**7. There is a _____ of concern regarding the future of the economy.**
   - [ ] A. great deal
   - [ ] B. threat
   - [ ] C. gap
   - [ ] D. factor

> [!answer] 答案：A（a great deal of - 大量）

**8. We must _____ a balance between economic development and environmental protection.**
   - [ ] A. strike
   - [ ] B. shift
   - [ ] C. show
   - [ ] D. face

> [!answer] 答案：A（strike a balance - 达成平衡）

**9. The new policy is designed to _____ the living standards of rural areas.**
   - [ ] A. uphold
   - [ ] B. upgrade
   - [ ] C. threaten
   - [ ] D. fabricate

> [!answer] 答案：B（upgrade - 提升）

**10. Many rare species are _____ with extinction due to habitat loss.**
   - [ ] A. fascinated
   - [ ] B. threatened
   - [ ] C. embedded
   - [ ] D. embodied

> [!answer] 答案：B（threatened - 受到威胁）

## Part 3: ⚠️ 僻义词专项测试

**11. address 在考研阅读中最常见的意义是**
   - [ ] A. 地址
   - [ ] B. 称呼
   - [ ] C. 处理，解决 ✅

> [!warning] 考研80%考查"处理"义，而非"地址"

**12.下列句子中 means 的意思是**
   > "We need to find _____ to reduce pollution."
   - [ ] A. 意思
   - [ ] B. 方法，手段 ✅
   - [ ] C. 财富
   - [ ] D. 平均

> [!warning] 固定搭配：means to do sth（做某事的方法）

**13. secure 在下列句子中的意思是**

   - [ ] C. 保护

**14. measures 在下列短语中的意思是**
