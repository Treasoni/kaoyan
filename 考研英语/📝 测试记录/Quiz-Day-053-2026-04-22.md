## 🔍 Part II: 词义辨析

**说明**: 选择最合适的词填空。

**11. It doesn't ______ whether you succeed or fail — what ______ is that you tried.**
A. matter / matters  B. last / lasts  C. hope / hopes  D. leave / leaves

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. matter / matters** — It doesn't matter 无所谓；what matters is... 重要的是...
</details>

---

**12. The meeting ______ for three hours, which was the ______ straw.**
A. hoped / last  B. lasted / last  C. left / little  D. learned / large

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. lasted / last** — last(v.)持续；the last straw 最后一根稻草/忍无可忍的事
</details>

---

**13. The concert was broadcast ______, attracting a ______ audience.**
A. live / large  B. alive / last  C. live / little  D. leave / many

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. live / large** — live(adj.)现场直播的；large audience 大量观众
</details>

---

**14. He is on ______ from the army, visiting his ______ at her house.**
A. homework / grandmother  B. holiday / husband  C. leave / lady  D. lesson / language

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**C. leave / lady** — on leave 在休假中；lady 女士
</details>

---

**15. The detective followed the ______ of the suspect's ______ carefully.**
A. letter / language  B. lesson / love  C. line / last  D. hit / job

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. letter / language** — letter 字母/信件；follow the letter 追踪信件。注意 letter 也可指"字面确切含义"
</details>

---

**16. ______ into a wealthy family doesn't guarantee ______.**
A. Marrying / happy  B. Marriage / happiness  C. Married / hope  D. Marry / house

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**B. Marriage / happiness** — marry into 通过结婚成为...的一员
</details>

---

**17. She ______ the news suddenly — it was a ______ song that everyone loved.**
A. hit / hit  B. left / last  C. hoped / happy  D. knew / kind

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**A. hit / hit** — It hit her 她突然意识到；hit song 热门歌曲。注意 hit 的一词多义
</details>

---

**18. It's ______ to finish the project without any help.**
A. kind  B. happy  C. impossible  D. large

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
**C. impossible** — impossible 不可能的
</details>

---

**19. The old man tried to ______ his limited resources during the crisis.**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

</details>

<details>

## ⚠️ Part III: 僻义识别

**说明**: 判断下列单词的考研僻义。

**21. matter 作动词时是什么意思？请造句。**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
v. 要紧，有关系。例句：It doesn't matter whether you agree or not.（你同不同意无所谓。）
</details>

---

**22. last 作动词时是什么意思？请造句。**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
v. 持续。例句：The battery lasts for 10 hours.（电池能持续10小时。）
</details>

---

**23. live 作形容词时有哪些含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
adj. 活的；现场演出的；现场直播的。例：live broadcast（现场直播），live animals（活的动物），live music（现场音乐）
</details>

---

**24. leave 除了"离开"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 休假（例：on leave 在休假中）；v. 把…留下，剩下（例：leave the door open 让门开着）
</details>

---

**25. husband 除了"丈夫"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
v. 节约使用（例：husband one's resources 节约使用资源）
</details>

---

**26. hit 除了"打"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 很受欢迎的人或事物（例：a hit song 热门歌曲）；v. 使某人突然意识到（例：It hit me that... 我突然意识到...）
</details>

---

**27. letter 除了"信"和"字母"还有什么含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
n. 字面确切含义。搭配：to the letter 严格地，一丝不苟地（例：follow the rules to the letter）
</details>

---

**28. house 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
v. 给…提供住房，给…提供场地（例：The building houses 200 families. 这栋楼容纳了200户家庭。）
</details>

---

**29. kind 除了"种类"还有什么词性和含义？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
adj. 善良的；和蔼的；友好的。搭配：be kind to sb 对某人友善；It's kind of sb to do sth 某人做某事真是太好了
</details>

---

**30. man 作动词时是什么意思？**
<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>
v. 负责操控（例：man the controls 负责操控仪器；man the desk 值班）
</details>
