## Part I: 词义辨析（10题）

**1. The company decided to ______ a team of experts to handle the crisis.**
A. dessert　　B. dispatch　　C. despise　　D. display

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: B. dispatch**
- dispatch: vt. 派遣，调遣；发送
- A. dessert: n. 饭后甜点（形近干扰）
- C. despise: vt. 鄙视（无关词）
- D. display: vt. 展示（无关词）
</details>

---

**2. After hours of negotiation, the suspect finally ______ and confessed.**
A. caved in　　B. cooked up　　C. combed out　　D. carted off

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: A. caved in**
- cave in: 塌落；认输（僻义预警）
- B. cook up: 虚构，伪造
- C. comb out: 仔细搜寻
- D. cart off: 带走；运走
</details>

---

**3. The ______ between the two vehicles caused serious injuries.**
A. corridor　　B. collision　　C. custody　　D. cushion

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: B. collision**
- collision: n. 碰撞；冲突
- A. corridor: n. 走廊
- C. custody: n. 监护（权）；拘留
- D. cushion: n. 坐垫；缓冲物
</details>

---

**4. She was awarded ______ of the children after the divorce.**
A. defence　　B. danger　　C. custody　　D. curtain

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: C. custody**
- custody: n. 监护（权）→ get/award custody of
- A. defence: n. 防御；辩护
- B. danger: n. 危险
- D. curtain: n. 窗帘
</details>

---

**5. The management tried to ______ the impact of the economic downturn.**
A. crane　　B. clutch　　C. cushion　　D. claw

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: C. cushion**
- cushion: vt. 缓冲；减轻…的影响 → cushion the blow/impact
- A. crane: v. 伸长(脖子)
- B. clutch: v. 紧抓（非本日词）
- D. claw: v. 抓，撕
</details>

---

**6. He managed to ______ his way to the top of the company through sheer determination.**
A. clawed　　B. combed　　C. caved　　D. cooked

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: A. clawed**
- claw one's way: 艰难向前（固定搭配）
- B. comb: v. 搜索
- C. cave: v. 塌落；认输
- D. cook: v. 烹饪
</details>

---

**7. The old building had beautiful ______ displayed in glass cabinets.**
A. china　　B. chalk　　C. cheese　　D. clay

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: A. china**
- china: n. 瓷器（僻义预警，小写）
- B. chalk: n. 粉笔
- C. cheese: n. 奶酪
- D. clay: n. 黏土
</details>

---

**8. The politician was ______ into a corner by the journalist's tough questions.**
A. coughed　　B. cornered　　C. coaxed　　D. coiled

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: B. cornered**
- corner: v. 把…逼入绝境（僻义预警）→ be cornered into
- A. cough: vi. 咳嗽
- C. coax: v. 哄劝（非本日词）
- D. coil: v. 缠绕
</details>

---

**9. The project was so ______ that it nearly bankrupted the company.**
A. clever　　B. cold　　C. costly　　D. cheap

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: C. costly**
- costly: adj. 昂贵的；代价高的
- A. clever: adj. 聪明的
- B. cold: adj. 冷的
- D. cheap: adj. 便宜的
</details>

---

**10. After the car ran out of fuel, it ______ to a stop at the side of the road.**
A. coasted　　B. cooked　　C. cried　　D. cracked

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案与解析</summary>

**答案: A. coasted**
- coast: vi. 靠惯性滑行（僻义预警）→ coast to a stop
- B. cook: v. 烹饪
- C. cry: v. 哭；喊叫
- D. crack: v. 破裂（非本日词）
</details>

## Part II: 僻义识别（5题）

**11. 下列哪个单词的僻义是"靠惯性滑行"？**
A. crane　　B. coast　　C. corner　　D. cave

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

**B. coast** — 常见义: 海岸 → 僻义: 靠惯性滑行
</details>

---

**12. 下列哪个单词的僻义是"把…逼入绝境"？**
A. corner　　B. crane　　C. cushion　　D. cliff

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

**A. corner** — 常见义: 角/拐角 → 僻义: 把…逼入绝境；垄断
</details>

---

**13. "china" 在考研中可能表示什么含义？**
A. 中国　　B. 瓷器　　C. 通道　　D. 教堂

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

**B. 瓷器** — 小写china表示"瓷器"，大写China表示"中国"
</details>

---

**14. "crane" 作为动词时，最可能的意思是？**
A. 起飞　　B. 伸长(脖子)　　C. 碰撞　　D. 哭泣

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

**B. 伸长(脖子)** — crane: n. 起重机/鹤 → v. 探着身子；伸长(脖子)
</details>

---

**15. "cave in" 在非字面语境中表示什么？**
A. 探索洞穴　　B. 塌落；认输　　C. 建造房屋　　D. 隐藏秘密

<details>
<summary>✅ 答案</summary>

**B. 塌落；认输** — cave in: 字面义=塌落 → 引申义=认输，屈服
</details>
