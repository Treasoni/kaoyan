## Part 3: 僻义辨析（8词）

> 选出句子中划线单词的僻义含义。

**1.** The effects of climate change will **last** for decades.
- A. 最后的
- B. 持续 ✓
- C. 最近的

**2.** The concert was broadcast **live** on national television.
- A. 居住的
- B. 活的
- C. 现场直播的 ✓

**3.** She is on maternity **leave** from her company.
- A. 离开
- B. 休假 ✓
- C. 留下

**4.** It doesn't **matter** whether you agree or not.
- A. 事情
- B. 物质
- C. 要紧 ✓

**5.** The new product was a **hit** in the Asian market.
- A. 打击
- B. 成功 ✓
- C. 碰撞

**6.** The country exports various **goods** to Europe.
- A. 好的
- B. 商品 ✓
- C. 善意

**7.** Follow the instructions to the **letter**.
- A. 信件
- B. 字母
- C. 严格地（字面含义）✓

**8.** We must **husband** our limited resources carefully.
- A. 丈夫
- B. 节约使用 ✓
- C. 结婚
