## 📖 原文

In the **widespread** debate over technological innovation, many scholars **contend** that traditional industries must undergo a **decisive** **upgrade** to remain competitive. The **threat** of obsolescence looms large as companies fail to keep their systems **up-to-date** with rapidly evolving market demands.

**Though** some **elite** institutions have managed to maintain their position through **continuous** innovation, the **gap** between them and emerging competitors continues to narrow. This **shift** in the competitive landscape has **elicited** a **thorough** reexamination of business strategies across various sectors.

One critical **factor** in this transformation is the ability to **embrace** change rather than resist it. Organizations that attempt to **fabricate** an illusion of stability while ignoring market realities inevitably **face** **failure**. Conversely, those willing to **venture** into uncharted territories often **gain** a significant advantage.

However, this process is not without its challenges. Many workers feel **threatened** by automation and fear that their skills may become irrelevant. The **prospect** of unemployment creates a **thirst** for security that can hinder adaptation. **It is imperative that** we **address** these concerns through **effective** **measures** such as retraining programs and social support systems.

Furthermore, the environmental impact of rapid industrialization cannot be ignored. Many natural habitats are under **threat**, and some species face the risk of extinction. This has **sparked** a call for sustainable development that balances economic growth with environmental protection.

**In conclusion**, achieving technological advancement requires a **thoughtful** approach that considers both economic and social dimensions. We must **strike** a balance between innovation and inclusivity, ensuring that the benefits of progress are **widely** shared across all segments of society.

## 📖 阅读理解练习

### Questions

**1. What is the passage mainly concerned with?**
A. The environmental impact of industrialization
B. The necessity of technological transformation for businesses
C. The gap between elite institutions and emerging competitors
D. The threat of unemployment in the automation era

**2. According to the passage, what happens to companies that ignore market realities?**
A. They gain a significant competitive advantage
B. They maintain an illusion of stability
C. They inevitably face failure
D. They successfully embrace change

**3. The author mentions "retraining programs" in Paragraph 4 to show that ______.**
A. automation creates more job opportunities
B. workers' fears about skills are unfounded
C. measures are needed to address workers' concerns
D. unemployment is inevitable in technological transformation

**4. What can be inferred about sustainable development from the passage?**
A. It prioritizes environmental protection over economic growth
B. It requires balancing economic and ecological considerations
C. It is not compatible with technological advancement
D. It has been successfully implemented across all sectors

---

### 参考答案与解析

**1. 【答案】B**
**解析**：主旨大意题。文章围绕传统产业必须进行技术升级以保持竞争力这一主题展开。
- A项：环境影响是文章中的一个方面，不是主旨
- C项：精英机构与新兴竞争者的差距是细节，不是重点
- D项：失业威胁是第四段的内容，偏离文章主旨

**2. 【答案】C**
**解析**：细节理解题。根据第3段 "Organizations that attempt to fabricate an illusion of stability while ignoring market realities inevitably face failure."
- A项：与文意相反
- B项：维持假象不是结果
- D项：与文意相反

**3. 【答案】C**
**解析**：推理判断题。第4段提到"It is imperative that we address these concerns through effective measures such as retraining programs"，说明需要采取措施来解决工人的关切。
- A项：文章未提及自动化创造更多就业机会
- B项：工人的恐惧是有根据的
- D项：过于绝对，文章未说失业不可避免

**4. 【答案】B**
**解析**：推理判断题。根据最后一段"balances economic growth with environmental protection"可知，可持续发展需要平衡经济和生态两个方面。
- A项：文章强调的是平衡，而非单方面优先
- C项：文章认为技术进步与可持续发展可以结合
- D项：过于绝对，文章未提及所有部门都已成功实施
