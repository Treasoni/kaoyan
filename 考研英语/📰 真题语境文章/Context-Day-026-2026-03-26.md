## 📖 原文

**The Advent of a New Era**

**Lately**, the world has witnessed unprecedented changes. With the **advent** of artificial intelligence and **satellite** technology, human society has entered a new **century** of transformation. This **adventure** into the unknown has brought both opportunities and challenges that test our **fate** as a species.

The **central** question of our time is how to **address** the **adverse** effects of technological progress while harnessing its benefits. **Yet**, this is not a task for the faint-hearted. It requires **unanimous** action and **zeal** from all sectors of society.

**Consider** the environmental crisis. **Glacier** melt at an unprecedented rate, and **ultraviolet** radiation penetrates the atmosphere due to climate change. Scientists **hazard** predictions about rising temperatures—already above **zero** in the Arctic—and warn of **fatal** consequences if we fail to act. The **decay** of ecosystems and the **hazard** to biodiversity are **fatal** to our planet's future.

In response, **agency** after **agency** has placed climate action on their **agenda**. A **battery** of policies has been proposed, from carbon taxes to **vacuum** technology that captures emissions. Some argue for a **fast** transition to renewable energy, while others move at a more **decent** pace, fearing that **hasty** decisions could lead to **faulty** outcomes.

The **battle** against climate change has created unlikely alliances. **Hawks** and **doves** in politics find common ground, setting aside their **hatred** for each other. **Adolescent** activists, **yet** to reach voting age, **adore** their role in shaping policy. They **jolly** supporters into action and refuse to **yawn** at inaction.

Technology plays a crucial role. **Satellite** imagery allows us to **glimpse** the changing Earth from space. We can **glitter** with hope as we see **glow** ing progress in renewable energy. Yet, we must not **glance** away from the **agony** of those already affected—communities facing **fatigue** from disasters, farmers suffering **harvest** failures, and families living in **vacant** homes after floods.

The economic implications are profound. **National debt** rises as countries invest in green infrastructure. **Decent** jobs in renewable energy replace those in fading industries. Some **advertise** the benefits of a green economy, while others **deceive** themselves into thinking business can continue as usual.

The **latitude** for action is narrowing. **More haste, less speed**—rushed decisions may **fasten** us onto the wrong path. We must **adhere** to **decent** principles and **adjacent** values of sustainability.

**Haul** ing ourselves out of this crisis requires **joint** responsibility. Whether you're a **kid** in school or a **centenarian**, your actions matter. **Wake up** to the reality—**sooner or later**, we must **fare** better or suffer the consequences.

As we **jog** towards solutions, let us not **kill** time with empty rhetoric. Let us **uncover** innovative approaches and **hatch** plans that work. The **harvest** of our efforts will determine whether we leave a **decent** world for future generations.

**Farewell** to the old ways—**zero** emissions, **unanimous** action, and **zeal** for change must be our **creed**.

## 📖 参考译文

**新纪元的来临**

**近来**，世界见证了前所未有的变化。随着人工智能和**卫星**技术的**出现**，人类社会已进入变革的新**世纪**。这场通往未知的**冒险**既带来了机遇，也带来了考验人类**命运**的挑战。

我们时代的**核心**问题是如何在利用技术进步益处的同时，**解决**其**不利**影响。**然而**，这并非一项适合胆怯者的任务。它需要社会各阶层的**一致**行动和**热忱**。

**考虑**环境危机。**冰川**以前所未有的速度融化，由于气候变化，**紫外线**辐射穿透大气层。科学家们**冒**着风险预测气温上升——北极气温已**零上**——并警告如果我们不采取行动，后果将是**致命的**。生态系统的**衰退**和对生物多样性的**危害**对地球的未来是**致命的**。

作为回应，一个又一个**机构**将气候行动列入其**议事日程**。**一系列**政策已被提出，从碳税到可以捕获排放物的**真空**技术。一些人主张向可再生能源**快速**过渡，而其他人则以更**体面**的节奏推进，担心**草率的**决定可能导致**有缺陷的**结果。

对抗气候变化的**战役**形成了不太可能的联盟。政治中的**鹰派**和**鸽派**找到了共同点，搁置了对彼此的**仇恨**。**青少年**活动家**还**没到投票年龄，却**热爱**自己在塑造政策中的作用。他们**使**支持者**高兴起来**，拒绝对不作为**打哈欠**。

技术发挥着关键作用。**卫星**图像让我们从太空**一瞥**变化中的地球。看到可再生能源进步的**微光**，我们可以**闪耀**着希望。然而，我们不能对那些已经受影响者的**极度痛苦**视而不���——面临灾难**疲劳**的社区、遭受**收成**失败的农民，以及洪水后住在**空**房子里的家庭。

经济影响是深远的。随着各国投资绿色基础设施，**国债**上升。可再生能源领域的**体面**工作取代了衰落行业的工作。有些人**宣扬**绿色经济的好处，而其他人**欺骗**自己，认为一切可以照常进行。

行动的**余地**正在缩小。"**欲速则不达**"——匆忙的决定可能会把我们**固定**在错误的路径上。我们必须**坚持**可持续的**体面**原则和**邻近的**价值观。

把自己从危机中**拖**出来需要**共同**责任。无论你是学校里的**小孩**还是**百岁老人**，你的行动都很重要。**醒来**面对现实——**迟早**，我们必须**表现得**更好，否则就要承受后果。

当我们**慢跑**着走向解决方案时，让我们不要用空洞的修辞来**消磨**时间。让我们**揭露**创新方法并**策划**有效的计划。我们努力的**收获**将决定我们是否为后代留下一个**体面的**世界。

**告别**旧的方式——**零**排放、**一致**行动和对变革的**热忱**必须成为我们的**信条**。

A. How to develop artificial intelligence

A. They are indifferent to climate action
B. They adore their role in shaping policy

D. They are too young to understand

**3. What does "more haste, less speed" suggest about climate action?**

A. We should act as quickly as possible
B. Rushed decisions may lead to faulty outcomes
C. Speed is not important
D. We should not act at all

**4. What is the main purpose of this passage?**

A. To advertise renewable energy
B. To describe the advent of satellites
C. To call for unanimous action on climate change
D. To explain the history of glaciers

1. **B** - "The central question of our time is how to address the adverse effects of technological progress while harnessing its benefits."

2. **B** - "Adolescent activists, yet to reach voting age, adore their role in shaping policy."

3. **B** - The phrase suggests that rushed (hasty) decisions may lead to faulty outcomes.

4. **C** - The passage calls for unanimous action, zeal, and joint responsibility to address climate change.

## 💡 重点句型分析

### 1. With the advent of..., ... has...
> "With the advent of artificial intelligence and satellite technology, human society has entered a new century of transformation."

**结构**: with + 名词短语，主句
**用法**: 表示"随着...的出现"
**仿写**: With the advent of renewable energy, we have entered a new era of sustainability.

### 2. ...not... for the faint-hearted
> "this is not a task for the faint-hearted"

**结构**: 否定句 + for the faint-hearted
**用法**: 表示某事需要勇气或决心
**仿写**: Addressing climate change is not a challenge for the faint-hearted.

### 3. Whether... or..., ...
> "Whether you're a kid in school or a centenarian, your actions matter."

**结构**: Whether A or B, 主句
**用法**: 表示"无论A还是B，都..."
**仿写**: Whether you're a student or a professional, your contribution counts.
