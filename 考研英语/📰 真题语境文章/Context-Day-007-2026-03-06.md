# 真题语境文章 - Day 007

**主题**: 民主治理与社会变革
**风格**: 经济学人式议论文
**词汇密度**: 26/285 (9.1%)

---

## 📖 原文

In recent years, a new ==pattern== has **emerged** in democratic societies. As **demographic** changes ==sweep== across nations, the **demand** for more ==transparent== governance has ==intensified==. Citizens are no longer **passive** observers; they actively **demonstrate** their dissatisfaction with established institutions.

The **feature** that most clearly ==distinguishes== modern democracies is the public's **passion** for ==accountability==. When governments fail to **address** the **emotional** needs of their ==constituents==, people become **fearful** of the future. This **fear** can **depress** ==civic== participation and **deny** citizens their voice in the **democratic** process.

Consider the case of a ==landmark== **bill** recently passed by Congress. The legislation, which aims to **secure** ==voting== rights, was initially **met with bias** from opponents who claimed it would ==**undermine**== the **scheme** of ==federalism==. However, supporters **acclaimed** the measure as a necessary step toward ensuring that every citizen's voice is heard.

The **quest** for political reform has become a defining characteristic of our era. **Eminent** scholars have **demonstrated** that societies with higher levels of civic engagement tend to be more **democratic** and economically prosperous. Yet, this relationship is **partly dependent** on institutional trust.

A **scientific** approach to policy-making requires that we **account** for these complexities. We must not **quit** the pursuit of better governance simply because the path is difficult. Instead, we should **partner** with civil society organizations and **quote** successful examples from other nations.

The **schedule** for implementing reforms is often behind, as political **bids** for power take precedence over public needs. But the **charm** of democracy lies in its capacity for ==self-correction==. Those **in charge** of governments must **spare** no effort to rebuild trust and **accompany** citizens on this journey toward a more perfect union.

## 📝 中文翻译

近年来，民主社会出现了一种新模式。随着人口结构的变化席卷各国，对更透明治理的需求日益强烈。公民不再是被动的旁观者；他们积极表达对既定机构的不满。

现代民主最显著的特征是公众对问责制的热情。当政府未能满足选民的情感需求时，人们对未来感到担忧。这种恐惧会抑制公民参与，剥夺公民在民主进程中的发言权。

以国会最近通过的一项具有里程碑意义的法案为例。这项旨在确保投票权的立法最初遭到反对者的偏见，他们声称这会破坏联邦制的计划。然而，支持者称赞该措施是确保每位公民声音被听到所必需的一步。

对政治改革的追求已成为我们这个时代的显著特征。杰出学者已经证明，公民参与度较高的社会往往更加民主和经济繁荣。然而，这种关系部分取决于制度信任。

科学的政策制定方法要求我们考虑这些复杂性。我们不能仅仅因为道路艰难就放弃追求更好的治理。相反，我们应该与民间社会组织合作，引用其他国家的成功案例。

实施改革的时间表往往落后于计划，因为政治权力争夺优先于公共需求。但民主的魅力在于其自我纠错的能力。那些主管政府的人必须不遗余力地重建信任，陪伴公民走向更完美联盟的旅程。

## 📖 阅读理解练习

### 1. 根据文章，现代民主社会的特征是什么？

> [!example] 参考答案
> 现代民主社会的特征是公众对问责制的热情（passion for accountability）。公民不再是被动的旁观者，而是积极表达对既定机构的不满。

### 2. 文章中提到的"bill"指的是什么？

> [!example] 参考答案
> 文章中的"bill"指的是**法案**，而非"账单"。这是一项旨在确保投票权的具有里程碑意义的立法。

> 因为政治权力争夺（political bids for power）优先于公共需求。

### 4. "in charge" 在文章中的意思是什么？

> [!example] 参考答案
> "in charge" 意为**主管，负责**。Those in charge of governments = 那些主管政府的人。
