## The Power of Social Solidarity in Environmental Protection

In an era where climate change has **escalated** into a global crisis, the need for collective action has never been more urgent. The **erosion** of our natural environment continues at an alarming rate, threatening to **eradicate** entire ecosystems and the communities that depend on them.

Governments alone cannot shoulder this burden. What is needed is **solidarity**—a unified commitment from all sectors of society to **preserve** our planet for future generations. This **solemn** responsibility must be shared by corporations, communities, and individuals alike.

The **insight** that environmental protection and economic development need not be mutually exclusive has gained momentum. **In spite of** initial skepticism, green technologies have demonstrated their potential to drive sustainable growth. The key is to **discern** which approaches offer genuine solutions rather than mere marketing gimmicks.

Education plays a crucial role in this transformation. We must **inspire** the younger generation to embrace environmental stewardship. By **nourishing** their curiosity and providing them with the tools to analyze complex problems, we empower them to develop **spontaneous** solutions to emerging challenges.

The success of environmental initiatives **hinges on** our ability to **conquer** short-term thinking. Some politicians and business leaders have attempted to **discard** environmental concerns as secondary to economic growth. This **disastrous** approach fails to recognize that ecological health is the foundation upon which all economic activity ultimately depends.

**In conjunction with** policy reforms, we must cultivate a spirit of environmental consciousness. This does not require **toxic** partisanship but rather a **sole** focus on our shared future. When communities **split** along ideological lines, progress stagnates. When they show **solidarity**, even the most daunting challenges become manageable.

The path forward requires us to **inspect** our consumption patterns, **withhold** support from environmentally harmful practices, and **erect** new systems that prioritize sustainability. We must be prepared to **discharge** our responsibilities not just to current stakeholders but to the **nucleus** of future generations who will inherit the consequences of our choices.

Time is of the essence. The window for effective action is narrowing. But with **spirited** determination and collective will, we can still turn the tide. The question is not whether we can afford to act, but whether we can afford not to.

## 🧠 记忆方法体系

### 一、词根词缀法

| 词根/词缀 | 含义 | 示例 |
|----------|------|------|
| **es-** | 向外 | escalate (升级), erupt (爆发), erode (侵蚀) |
| **dis-** | 分开、不 | discharge (释放), discard (丢弃), discern (辨别) |
| **sol-** | 单独/整体 | sole (唯一的), solidarity (团结), solar (太阳的) |
| **spir-** | 呼吸/精神 | spirit (精神), inspire (激励), respire (呼吸) |
| **hing-** | 铰链 | hinge (铰链) → hinge on (取决于) |

### 二、联想记忆法

1. **hinge (铰链 → 取决于)**
   - 门的开合依赖于铰链 → 事情的发展取决于关键因素

2. **solidarity (团结)**
   - solid (坚固的) + arity → 像固体一样连在一起 → 团结

3. **eradicate (根除)**
   - e- (出) + radic (根) + -ate → 把根拔出来 → 根除

4. **nucleus (核心)**
   - 原子核 → 细胞核 → 核心 → 团队核心

### 三、语境记忆法（推荐！）

**将单词放入文章句子中记忆**：

1. **escalate**: "climate change has **escalated** into a global crisis..." → 气候变化升级为全球危机

2. **solidarity**: "What is needed is **solidarity**—a unified commitment..." → 我们需要的是团结

3. **hinge on**: "The success **hinges on** our ability..." → 成功取决于我们的能力

4. **in spite of**: "**In spite of** initial skepticism..." → 尽管最初有怀疑

5. **nucleus**: "...the **nucleus** of future generations..." → 未来一代的核心

### 四、僻义专项记忆

| 单词 | 本义 | 僻义 | 语境提示 | 记忆技巧 |
|------|------|------|----------|----------|
| **hinge** | 铰链 | 取决于 | hinge on/upon | 门的开合依赖于铰链 → 取决于 |
| **spite** | 恶意 | 尽管 | in spite of | 固定搭配，整体记忆 |
| **spot** | 地点 | 发现 | spot the difference | "点"到即止 → 发现 |

### 📖 中文译文

**社会团结在环境保护中的力量**

在气候变化已**升级**为全球危机的时代，集体行动的需求从未如此紧迫。自然环境的**侵蚀**以惊人的速度持续着，威胁着**根除**整个生态系统以及依赖它们的社区。

仅靠政府无法承担这一重担。我们需要的是**团结**——社会各部门对为子孙后代**保护**我们星球的统一承诺。这一**庄严的**责任必须由企业、社区和个人共同分担。

环境保护与经济发展不必相互排斥的**洞察**已获得动力。**尽管**最初存在怀疑，绿色技术已证明其推动可持续增长的潜力。关键是要**辨别**哪些方法提供真正的解决方案，而不仅仅是营销噱头。

教育在这一转型中发挥着关键作用。我们必须**激励**年轻一代拥抱环境管理。通过**培养**他们的好奇心并为他们提供分析复杂问题的工具，我们使他们能够针对新出现的挑战开发**自发的**解决方案。

环境倡议的成功**取决于**我们**战胜**短期思维的能力。一些政客和商界领袖试图将环境关切作为经济增长的次要因素而**抛弃**。这种**灾难性的**方法未能认识到生态健康是所有经济活动最终赖以生存的基础。

**与**政策改革**配合**，我们必须培养环境意识精神。这不需要**有毒的**党派之争，而是对我们共同未来的**唯一**关注。当社区沿意识形态**分裂**时，进步就会停滞。当它们表现出**团结**时，即使是最艰巨的挑战也变得可控。

前进的道路要求我们**检查**消费模式，**拒绝支持**环境有害的做法，并**建立**优先考虑可持续性的新系统。我们必须准备**履行**我们的责任，不仅对当前利益相关者，而且对将继承我们选择后果的未来几代人的**核心**。

时间至关重要。有效行动的窗口正在缩小。但凭借**精神饱满的**决心和集体意志，我们仍能力挽狂澜。问题不是我们能否承担行动的代价，而是我们能否承担不行动的代价。

**2. The word "hinge on" (Paragraph 5) is closest in meaning to:**

B. close
