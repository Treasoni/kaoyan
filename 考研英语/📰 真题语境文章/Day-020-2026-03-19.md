## 📖 原文

In the corporate world, building a **consensus** among stakeholders is essential for effective governance. When companies face major decisions, they must **consult with** experts and **arrange for** thorough assessments before proceeding.

> 📖 **译文**：在企业界，在利益相关者之间建立**共识**对于有效治理至关重要。当公司面临重大决策时，必须**咨询**专家并**安排**全面评估后再进行。

The **conscientious** ==executive== understands that short-term profits should not **preclude** long-term sustainability. They **instruct** their teams in ethical practices and **insulate** the company from market ==volatility==. Such leaders **withstand** pressure from shareholders who demand immediate returns.

> 📖 **译文**：**尽责的**高管明白，短期利润不应**阻碍**长期可持续发展。他们**指导**团队遵循道德规范，使公司**免受**市场波动的影响。这样的领导者能够**顶住**要求即时回报的股东压力。

However, **a conspiracy** of silence often surrounds corporate misconduct. When executives **withhold** critical information from the public, they betray the **conscience** of their profession. The **consequences** are often **conspicuous**: damaged reputation, legal penalties, and loss of public trust.

> 📖 **译文**：然而，企业不当行为周围常常存在**共谋的沉默**。当高管向公众**隐瞒**关键信息时，他们背叛了职业**良心**。**后果**往往是**显而易见的**：声誉受损、法律处罚和公众信任的丧失。

The **mission** of modern corporations has evolved beyond profit maximization. Like a **missionary** spreading ethical values, responsible companies **consolidate** their position by demonstrating social responsibility. Their **predominant** focus shifts from quarterly earnings to sustainable impact.

> 📖 **译文**：现代企业的**使命**已超越利润最大化。像传播道德价值观的**传教士**一样，负责任的公司通过展示社会责任来**巩固**其地位。他们的**主要**关注点从季度收益转向可持续影响。

**In consecutive** years of economic uncertainty, the outlook may **look bleak**. Companies **greedy for** quick gains often fail, while those that **withstand** the **horrible** storms of market turbulence emerge stronger. **Wisdom** suggests that **restless** pursuit of profit without purpose leads to **misfortune**.

> 📖 **译文**：在经济不确定的**连续**年份中，前景可能**显得黯淡**。**贪婪**追求快速收益的公司往往会失败，而那些能够**经受住**市场动荡**可怕**风暴的公司则会变得更强大。**智慧**表明，没有目标的**不安**追求利润会导致**不幸**。

**In conclusion**, corporate leaders must **greet** challenges with integrity. They should not be **pregnant** with hidden agendas but transparent in their dealings. The **greenhouse** of corporate culture must nurture ethical growth, for the **conscience** of business determines its ultimate destiny.

> 📖 **译文**：**总之**，企业领导者必须以诚信**迎接**挑战。他们不应**怀有**隐藏的议程，而应在交易中保持透明。企业文化的**温室**必须培育道德成长，因为商业**良心**决定了其最终命运。

## 🔑 词汇记忆技巧

### 词根词缀法

1. **consensus** = con-（共同）+ sens（感觉）+ -us → 共同的感觉 → 共识
2. **conspiracy** = con-（共同）+ spir（呼吸）+ -acy → 共同呼吸 → 共谋
3. **conscientious** = con-（共同）+ sci（知道）+ -entious → 知道什么是对的 → 认真的
4. **conspicuous** = con-（完全）+ spic（看）+ -uous → 完全能看见 → 显眼的
5. **preclude** = pre-（提前）+ clud（关闭）→ 提前关闭 → 阻止

### 联想记忆法

- **consensus** → census（人口普查）→ 大家都同意才能普查 → 共识
- **consecutive** → con（一起）+ secu（跟随）→ 一个跟着一个 → 连续的
- **missionary** → mission（任务）+ -ary（人）→ 被派出去执行任务的人 → 传教士
- **withhold** → with（对抗）+ hold（持有）→ 对抗性地持有 → 扣留、隐瞒
- **bleak** → break（打破）→ 希望被打碎 → 黯淡的

### 对比记忆法

| 单词 | 含义 | 对比词 |
|------|------|--------|
| consensus | 共识 | consent 同意 |
| conscience | 良心 | conscious 有意识的 |
| consecutive | 连续的 | consequent 随之而来的 |
