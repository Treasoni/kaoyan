## Passage: The Changing Landscape of Urban Development

The **picture** of modern urban development has changed **quite** dramatically **over** the past two decades. Cities **once** considered industrial wastelands have been transformed into vibrant communities, **often** through the **push** of ambitious **policymakers** who sought to **place** renewed emphasis on sustainable living.

Consider the **northern** district of Millbrook. Ten years ago, this area was known **mostly** for its abandoned factories and **poor** air quality. The **noise** from heavy machinery was deafening, and the **number** of residents had dwindled to a few hundred. The **need** for revitalization was obvious, and **meetings** were held to discuss possible solutions.

One **question** dominated every discussion: how to balance economic growth with environmental protection? The **moment** a comprehensive plan was approved, things began to move rapidly. Old factories were converted into art studios and **notebook**-filled coworking spaces. Parks were designed with both recreation and ecology in mind — including a large **national park** on the city's **northeast** edge.

The transformation was nothing short of **spectacular**. Within months, **photographs** and **pictures** of the renewed district appeared in every major **newspaper**. Property values began to rise as families sought homes **near** these new green spaces. The **opening** of several cultural venues further boosted the area's appeal.

**Nonetheless**, not everyone was **pleased** with the changes. Some long-time residents found themselves **pushed** out by rising rents. They had **no** intention of leaving the **place** they had called home for decades. "We've been living **on** our **own** here since before the developers came," said one elderly resident. "We don't need someone to tell us what our **neighborhood** should look like."

The **problem** highlights a central tension in urban planning: the desire to improve living conditions must be weighed against the rights of existing communities. **Past** experiences have shown that when planners fail to account for local voices, the results can be disastrous. **Most** successful redevelopment projects are those that involve residents in the decision-making **process** from the very beginning.

The **might** of public opinion should not be underestimated. When community leaders organized a **peaceful** protest, city officials were forced to reconsider their approach. They eventually **named** a community representative to the planning board, ensuring that future development would be more inclusive.

Today, the Millbrook district serves as a model for other cities. The **economic picture** has improved considerably — **millions** of dollars in new investment have flowed into the area. **New** businesses have **opened**, ranging from small cafes to technology startups. The **music** from outdoor concerts fills the air every **weekend** **morning**, and the once-quiet streets are now alive with activity.

But perhaps the most remarkable change is in how residents **picture** their future. Where there was once despair, there is now hope. As one community organizer put it, "We've learned that real change doesn't just happen **overnight** — it takes **patience**, persistence, and above all, the willingness to **listen**."

The Millbrook story reminds us that urban development is not merely about buildings and infrastructure. It is fundamentally about **people** — their lives, their **needs**, and their right to **own** the spaces they inhabit. **Once** we recognize this, we can begin to build cities that truly serve everyone.

## 重点词汇语境解析

### 1. picture (n. 情况/局面)
> The **picture** of modern urban development has changed...

**考研用法**：picture 作"情况/局面"解，常见搭配 the economic picture（经济形势），the political picture（政治局面）

### 2. over (prep. 在...期间)
> ...changed **quite** dramatically **over** the past two decades.

**考研用法**：over 表示"在...期间"，如 over the past decade（在过去十年间），over the years（多年来）

### 3. push (v. 推动/鞭策)
> ...through the **push** of ambitious policymakers...

**考研用法**：push 作名词，表示"推动力/鞭策"，如 give a push to reform（推动改革）

### 4. once (conj. 一旦)
> Cities **once** considered industrial wastelands...

**考研用法**：once 作连词引导从句，"一旦"，也可作副词表示"曾经"

### 5. nonetheless (adv. 尽管如此)
> **Nonetheless**, not everyone was pleased...

**考研用法**：转折副词，等同 nevertheless/however，在阅读理解中常出现在段落转折处

### 6. might (n. 力量)
> The **might** of public opinion should not be underestimated.

**考研用法**：might 作名词"力量/威力"，如 military might（军事力量），with all one's might（用尽全力）

### 7. place (vt. 安放/放置)
> ...who sought to **place** renewed emphasis on...

**考研用法**：place emphasis on = put emphasis on = 强调，place 代替 put 更正式

### 8. own (vt. 拥有)
> ...their right to **own** the spaces they inhabit.

**考研用法**：own 作动词"拥有"，比 have/possess 更强调归属感

### 9. question (n./vt.)
> One **question** dominated every discussion...

**考研用法**：question 作动词表示"质疑"很常见，如 question the assumption（质疑这个假设）

### 10. poor (adj. 差劲的/缺少的)
> ...abandoned factories and **poor** air quality.

**考研用法**：poor 引申义"差劲的/匮乏的"，如 poor performance（差劲表现），poor in resources（资源匮乏）

## 长难句解析

### 句1
> Cities once considered industrial wastelands have been transformed into vibrant communities, often through the push of ambitious policymakers who sought to place renewed emphasis on sustainable living.

**结构分析**：
- 主干：Cities have been transformed into vibrant communities
- 过去分词短语：once considered industrial wastelands（作后置定语修饰 cities）
- 方式状语：through the push of ambitious policymakers
- 定语从句：who sought to place renewed emphasis on sustainable living

**翻译**：曾经被视为工业废地的城市，已转变为充满活力的社区，这通常是通过那些力图重新强调可持续生活的雄心勃勃的决策者的推动实现的。

### 句2
> The problem highlights a central tension in urban planning: the desire to improve living conditions must be weighed against the rights of existing communities.

**结构分析**：
- 主干：The problem highlights a central tension
- 冒号后解释：the desire...must be weighed against the rights...
- 不定式短语：to improve living conditions（作 desire 的后置定语）

**翻译**：这个问题凸显了城市规划中的一个核心矛盾：改善生活条件的愿望必须与现有社区的权利相权衡。
