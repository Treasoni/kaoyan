# 📰 真题语境文章 - Day 047

**日期**: 2026-04-15
**主题**: Urban Heritage Conservation (城市遗产保护)
**使用词汇**: 25个本日单词

---

## English Text

In recent decades, the **defence** of cultural heritage has become a **costly** yet essential undertaking across the European **continent**. As cities expand, historic buildings face the **danger** of demolition, pushing preservationists into a **corner** where they must fight for every ancient **church** and **cathedral**.

The **chemistry** between urban development and heritage conservation has always been complex. When a **clerk** at the city planning office **dispatched** a notice to demolish a **castle** dating back to the 12th century, local residents **dared** to protest. They refused to **cave in** to pressure from developers who wanted to build modern **clinics** and **clubs** on the historic site.

Walking through the **dark** **corridors** of the old building, one could observe the delicate **china** displayed in **cupboards** and the **clay** sculptures that told stories of centuries past. The **deep** **cushions** in the main hall, though worn, still retained their charm. A **crane** stood idle outside, waiting for the order to tear down what had survived wars and revolutions.

The **collision** between progress and preservation is not new. In the **cold** December mornings, activists would gather at the **courtyard**, their breath visible in the **chilly** air, determined to **draw the curtains** on destructive urban planning. Some argued that **cheap** construction materials would only lead to another **death** of cultural identity.

The **custody** of historical artifacts requires specialized knowledge. **Chemists** analyze the **chalk** and **clay** compositions of ancient walls, while **carpenters** restore wooden structures using traditional techniques. Even the **cassette** recordings of oral histories from elderly residents have become valuable resources.

> After years of legal battles, the **corner** was finally turned. The city council established a heritage protection committee that took **custody** of endangered buildings, ensuring that the **coast** of cultural destruction would finally come to a halt.

## 中文译文

近几十年来，文化遗产的**保护**（defence）已成为欧洲**大陆**（continent）上一项**代价高昂的**（costly）但必不可少的工程。随着城市扩张，历史建筑面临被拆除的**危险**（danger），将保护主义者逼入**绝境**（corner），他们必须为每一座古老的**教堂**（church）和**大教堂**（cathedral）而战。

城市发展与遗产保护之间的**化学反应**（chemistry）一直很复杂。当城市规划办公室的一名**职员**（clerk）**发出**（dispatched）拆除一座可追溯到12世纪的**城堡**（castle）的通知时，当地居民**敢于**（dared）抗议。他们拒绝向开发商**认输**（cave in），后者想在历史遗址上建造现代**诊所**（clinics）和**俱乐部**（clubs）。

穿过古老建筑**昏暗的**（dark）**走廊**（corridors），人们可以观察到**橱柜**（cupboards）中展示的精美**瓷器**（china）和讲述几个世纪故事的**黏土**（clay）雕塑。大厅里**厚实的**（deep）**坐垫**（cushions）虽然磨损，但仍然保持着魅力。外面一台**起重机**（crane）闲置着，等待拆除在战争和革命中幸存下来的建筑。

进步与保护之间的**冲突**（collision）并不新鲜。在**寒冷的**（cold）十二月清晨，活动人士会聚集在**庭院**（courtyard），在寒气中呼出白雾，决心为破坏性的城市规划**拉上帷幕**（draw the curtains）。一些人认为，**廉价的**（cheap）建筑材料只会导致文化认同的又一次**消亡**（death）。

历史文物的**保管**（custody）需要专业知识。**化学家**（Chemists）分析古墙的**粉笔**（chalk）和**黏土**（clay）成分，而**木匠**（carpenters）使用传统技术修复木结构。甚至老年居民口述历史的**盒式磁带**（cassette）录音也已成为宝贵资源。

> 经过多年的法律斗争，**局势**终于**好转**（the corner was finally turned）。市议会成立了一个遗产保护委员会，**接管**（custody）了濒危建筑，确保文化破坏的**惯性**终于**停止**（coast...come to a halt）。
