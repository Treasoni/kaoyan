## 📖 语境文章

### The Hidden Costs of Corporate ==Expansion==

In the **absence** of ==proper== ==regulation,== large corporations often **eliminate** competition by **absorbing** smaller firms. This process, known as **mergers and acquisitions**, has become increasingly common in the **global** market.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **absorb** 在此处指"并购，吞并"，是考研僻义
> **in the absence of** 是固定搭配，意为"缺乏，不存在"

Last year, a major **financial** firm announced plans to **capture** 40% of the market share. Critics argue that such aggressive expansion could **bar** new ==entrants== from competing effectively. The **catch**, however, is that these deals often sound better than they actually are.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **firm** 此处指"公司"，是僻义
> **bar** 此处作动词，意为"阻止，禁止"
> **catch** 此处指"隐患，陷阱"，是僻义

The government has tried to **mediate** between consumer advocates and business leaders, but with limited success. **Elaborate** regulations have been ==proposed==, but lawmakers have failed to **figure out** how to ==enforce== them effectively.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **mediate** 意为"调解，斡旋"
> **elaborate** 此处作形容词，意为"复杂的，精心设计的"

In the **case** of the technology sector, the situation is even more concerning. Companies with nearly **infinite** **capacity** for growth are buying up startups at an unprecedented rate. **Capital** flows freely across borders, making it difficult for any single nation to ==regulate== these ==transactions.==

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **case** 此处指"情况，案例"
> **capacity** 此处指"能力，潜力"
> **capital** 此处指"资本"，是僻义

The **abstract** concept of "free market competition" often **bears** little ==resemblance== to reality. As one economist put it: "We need to look beyond the **bare** facts and understand the **fundamental** issues at stake."

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **abstract** 此处作形容词，但在真题中常考查名词"摘要"
> **bare** 意为"最基本的，仅仅的"

## 🧠 记忆方法体系

### 一、词根词缀法

| 词根/词缀 | 含义 | 文中示例 |
|----------|------|----------|
| **ex-pan-** | 向外扩张 | expansion |
| **reg-** | 规则、统治 | regulation, regulate |
| **trans-** | 跨越、转移 | transactions |
| **-en-** | 使... | enforce |
| **-ance** | 名词后缀（表状态） | resemblance |
| **-ant** | 名词后缀（表人） | entrants |

### 二、联想记忆法

- **expansion**：ex（向外）+ pan（平底锅）→ 像平底锅一样向外"扩张"
- **proper**：proper tea（适当的茶）→ "适当的"
- **regulation**：rule（规则）的正式说法 → "监管"
- **entrants**：entrance（入口）里的人 → "进入者"
- **enforce**：en（使）+ force（强迫）→ "强制执行"
- **regulate**：regular（规律的）+ -ate → 使变规律 → "监管"
- **transactions**：trans（转移）+ action（行动）→ 交易"行为"
- **resemblance**： resemble（像）→ 相"似"性

### 三、语境记忆法（推荐！）

**将单词放入文章句子中记忆**：

1. **expansion**：*The Hidden Costs of Corporate **Expansion***（企业**扩张**的隐性成本）

3. **enforce**：*...lawmakers have failed to **figure out** how to **enforce** them effectively.*（...立法者未能**弄明白**如何有效**执行**它们...）
