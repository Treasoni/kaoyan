## 原文

> [!quote] The Digital **Challenge** to Academic **Defintion**
>
> The rapid **acceleration** of digital technology has **defied** ==conventional== **definitions** of academic research. What we **characterize** as "scholarship" today is undergoing a **delicate** **transformation** (非今日词), one that **challenges** **definite** ==boundaries== between ==disciplines==.
>
> In the **field** of education, ==institutions== must **defend** their ability to **deliver** quality instruction while **benefiting** from technological advances. The **deliberate** integration of digital tools requires a **delicate** balance: too much **acceleration** risks ==compromising== the **character** of learning, while too much **delay** may leave students without **access** to essential resources.
>
> The **benefit** of this **transformation** is **definite**: increased **accessibility** (非今日词) and **incentive** for innovation. Yet, some **academics** **challenge** the ==notion== that technology is ==inherently== **beneficial**. They **characterize** the rush toward ==digitization== as a **threat** (非今日词) to **benign** **academic** environments, where **face-to-face** (非今日词) interaction fosters **belief** and **behaviour** essential to intellectual growth.
>
> To **withdraw** from this digital **incidence** would be to **deny** (非今日词) reality. Instead, we must **begin** by **defining** clear principles: that technology should **benefit** learners, not **defy** human connection; that **acceptance** of digital tools must be **deliberate**, not **incidental**; and that the **character** of education must remain **benign** and **inclusive** (非今日词).
>
> As we **inaugurate** this new era, let us remember: the **challenge** is not to **accept** technology blindly, but to **define** its role with **deliberate** intent, ensuring that **beneficial** outcomes **prevail** (非今日词).

## 句式分析

### 句1: The rapid **acceleration** of digital technology has **defied** conventional **definitions** of academic research.

- **结构**: 主语 + 谓语 + 宾语

- **结构**: 主语从句 + 系动词 + 分词短语 + 定语从句

### Q1: According to the passage, what is the author's attitude toward digital technology in education?

- [ ] A. Completely opposed to digitization
- [ ] B. Supportive but cautious about its implementation
- [ ] C. Believes it should be adopted without delay
- [ ] D. Indifferent to technological changes

> [!answer] 答案：B
> **解析**: 作者提到 "deliberate integration"（深思熟虑的整合）和 "delicate balance"（微妙平衡），表明支持但谨慎的态度。关键词：deliberate, delicate balance, not accept blindly。

### Q2: What does the author imply by "acceleration risks compromising the character of learning"?

- [ ] A. Technology makes learning faster and more efficient.
- [ ] B. Too rapid adoption may harm the essential qualities of education.
- [ ] C. Character building is no longer important in education.
- [ ] D. Digital tools should replace traditional learning methods.

> [!answer] 答案：B
> **解析**: compromise 意为"损害、危及"。作者警告过快的技术采用可能损害教育的本质特征。关键词：compromise, character of learning, delicate balance。
