## 📖 原文

The modern university **campus** is no longer just a place for academic pursuit; it has become a complex **ecology** of social interactions, cultural exchanges, and intellectual **facilities**. According to a recent survey, students today are **eager** to **carry out** various activities that **facilitate** their personal growth beyond the classroom.

One significant **barrier** to effective communication on campus is the **language barrier** faced by international students. To **ease** this problem, universities have established special **facilities** where students can **gather** to practice their language skills. These programs help **facilitate** cross-cultural understanding and **accord** students opportunities to build meaningful relationships.

The university **faculty** has also taken steps to **facilitate** student success. Professors are **in accordance with** new teaching methods that **hand** students more control over their learning. **Handy** digital tools and online resources **come in handy** when students need to **carry through** their research projects. However, some students still struggle to **halt** their procrastination habits and need to learn how to **gather** their thoughts effectively.

Financial concerns can also be a major **barrier**. Many students must **carry** heavy financial burdens while pursuing their education. The high **salary** expectations after graduation often drive their choices, but the reality of the job market can be like a **traffic jam**—slow and frustrating. Some students even face the risk of becoming **bankrupt** before they complete their degrees.

Despite these challenges, the university **campus** remains a place where **fabulous** opportunities exist. Students who are **eager** to learn will find that the **faculty** and various **facilities** are there to **facilitate** their journey. As one professor noted, "Education is not just about **carrying** knowledge from one generation to the next; it's about **gathering** wisdom and **facilitating** personal growth."

## 📝 译文

现代大学**校园**不再仅仅是学术追求的场所；它已成为一个包含社会交往、文化交流和智力**设施**的复杂**生态系统**。根据最近的一项调查，今天的学生**渴望**开展各种活动，以**促进**课堂之外的个人成长。

有效沟通的一个重大**障碍**是国际学生面临的**语言障碍**。为了**缓解**这个问题，大学建立了特殊的**设施**，学生可以**聚集**在那里练习语言技能。这些项目有助于**促进**跨文化理解，并**给予**学生建立有意义关系的机会。

大学**教职员工**也采取了措施来**促进**学生成功。教授们**按照**新的教学方法，把更多控制权**交给**学生。当学生需要**完成**研究项目时，便利的数字工具和在线资源**会派上用场**。然而，一些学生仍然难以**停止**拖延习惯，需要学习如何有效地**整理**他们的思路。

经济担忧也可能是一个主要**障碍**。许多学生在接受教育时必须**承担**沉重的经济负担。毕业后对高**薪水**的期望往往驱动着他们的选择，但就业市场的现实可能就像**交通拥堵**一样——缓慢而令人沮丧。有些学生甚至在完成学位之前就面临**破产**的风险。

尽管面临这些挑战，大学**校园**仍然是一个存在**极好**机会的地方。**渴望**学习的学生会发现，**教职员工**和各种**设施**都在那里**促进**他们的旅程。正如一位教授所说："教育不仅仅是将知识从一代**传递**到下一代；它是关于**收集**智慧和**促进**个人成长。"

## 🎯 重点词汇详解

### 1. accord ⭐⭐⭐ 🔴 Critical
**文中用法**: "...**accord** students opportunities to build..."
**释义**: vt. 给予，赠与
**常见义**: n. 一致，符合
**考频**: 50%
**例句**: The university should accord students more freedom. (大学应该给予学生更多自由)

### 2. faculty ⭐⭐⭐ 🔴 Critical
**文中用法**: "The university **faculty** has also taken steps..."
**释义**: n. 全体教员
**僻义**: n. 能力，天赋
**考频**: 45%
**例句**: She has a remarkable faculty for learning languages. (她有学习语言的天赋)

### 3. facilitate ⭐⭐⭐
**文中用法**: "...programs help **facilitate** cross-cultural understanding"
**释义**: vt. 促进，使便利
**词根**: facil(容易) + itate(使) → 使变得容易
**例句**: The new policy will facilitate trade between countries. (新政策将促进国家间的贸易)

### 4. barrier ⭐⭐⭐
**文中用法**: "One significant **barrier** to effective communication..."
**释义**: n. 障碍，障碍物
**搭配**: language barrier (语言障碍)
**例句**: Lack of confidence is a major barrier to success. (缺乏自信是成功的主要障碍)

### 5. carry out ⭐⭐⭐
**文中用法**: "...students today are **eager** to **carry out** various activities"
**释义**: 实施，施行
**搭配**: carry out a plan/survey/experiment
**例句**: The scientists are carrying out a series of experiments. (科学家们正在进行一系列实验)
