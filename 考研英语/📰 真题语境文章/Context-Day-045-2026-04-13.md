## Passage: The Environmental Aftermath of Industrial Prosperity

> The **prosperity** of the Industrial Revolution brought **furthermore** challenges that **surround** us to this day. While nations **prosper** economically, the **aftermath** of unchecked industrialization has left many regions in **ruins**, with devastating consequences for both human health and the natural environment.

> The **crude** methods of early manufacturing, **albeit** revolutionary for their time, **provoke**d significant environmental degradation. Factories would routinely **dump** waste into rivers and emit pollutants into the **air**, treating the ozone layer as an afterthought. The **irritat**ing smog that **surround**ed major cities was not merely a **fuss** — it was a public health crisis that would eventually **crush** communities and leave once-**prosperous** towns **in ruins**.

> Scientists have attempted to **isolate** the key factors contributing to environmental decline. Research teams have developed **prototype**s of cleaner technologies, from **supersonic** aircraft with reduced emissions to advanced **protein** synthesis methods that require fewer resources. **Furthermore**, international agreements have sought to **protect** vulnerable ecosystems, establishing legal **provision**s that **prosecute** companies violating environmental standards.

> However, progress has been slow. The **royalty** and political elite in many countries have been **afraid** to implement necessary changes. Some industries continue to **bypass** regulations **by accident** or deliberate evasion, **irrespective of** the consequences. The **dull** resistance to change is particularly evident in the **agriculture** sector, where traditional farming practices continue to degrade the **surroundings**.

> **Another** significant challenge lies in **administer**ing effective policies across vast **province**s. Local **agent**s responsible for enforcement often lack adequate resources. The **ownership** of land rights remains disputed, and indigenous communities — **accustom**ed to their traditional ways — find themselves **isolate**d from decision-making processes. This has **provoke**d widespread **protest**, with citizens demanding that their voices be **air**ed in public forums.

> **Almost** every major environmental treaty acknowledges these challenges. The **irony** is that while we possess the technology to address many of these issues, political will often proves **futile**. As one researcher noted, it would be a **cruel** joke if we allowed **superstition** and short-term thinking to **ruin** the only planet we have. We owe it to future generations — **owing to** our moral responsibility — to **further** the cause of environmental protection.

> **Already**, some positive changes are visible. **Musical** and cultural events raise awareness. **Crystal**-clear evidence from scientific research continues to **mute** skepticism. Innovative approaches — from sustainable **fur** and textile production to new methods of **irrigate**ing arid lands — demonstrate that **prosperous** development need not come at nature's expense.

### 🔑 重点句子翻译练习

1. > "The **crude** methods of early manufacturing, **albeit** revolutionary for their time, **provoke**d significant environmental degradation."
>
> 翻译：早期制造业的粗糙方法，尽管在当时具有革命性，却引发了严重的环境退化。

2. > "Some industries continue to **bypass** regulations **by accident** or deliberate evasion, **irrespective of** the consequences."
>
> 翻译：一些行业继续通过意外或故意逃避的方式绕过法规，不顾其后果。

3. > "The **irony** is that while we possess the technology to address many of these issues, political will often proves **futile**."
>
> 翻译：讽刺的是，虽然我们拥有解决许多这些问题的技术，但政治意愿往往被证明是徒劳的。

4. > "We **owe** it to future generations — **owing to** our moral responsibility — to **further** the cause of environmental protection."
>
> 翻译：出于道德责任，我们欠后代一个将环保事业继续推进的责任。
