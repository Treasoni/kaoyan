## 📖 原文

In the **decisive** **decade** ahead, achieving **economic** **efficiency** while protecting our environment has become a matter of **urgent** concern. Many scholars **contend** that we must **strike** a **balance** between development and sustainability.

**Though** some argue that **economical** growth should take priority, the **opposite** **opinion** gains more support. The **effective** use of resources and **efficient** production methods are no longer optional—they are **decisive** factors in our future.

We must **take measures** to **secure** a sustainable future. **By means of** innovation and **worthy** initiatives, we can **thrive** while preserving our planet. It is a **worthwhile** goal that deserves our **utmost** attention.

Furthermore, we must **abide by** the principles of environmental protection. Those who **oppose** **necessary** regulations fail to see the **scope** of the problem. The **threat** of climate change is **real**, and **a great deal of** evidence supports this claim.

**In conclusion**, achieving both **economic** prosperity and environmental sustainability is not just a possibility—it is a **decisive** necessity. We must act now, **through** collective effort and **decisive** action.

### Questions

**1. What is the passage mainly concerned with?**

B. The necessity of balancing economy and environment

**2. According to the passage, what is the author's stance on economic growth?**
A. Economic growth should always take priority
B. Economic growth is more important than sustainability
C. Economic growth and environmental protection must be balanced
D. Environmental protection is not necessary

**3. The phrase "a great deal of evidence" suggests that ______.**
A. The evidence is limited
B. There is substantial evidence supporting climate change
C. The evidence is questionable
D. More evidence is needed

**4. What can be inferred about those who oppose environmental regulations?**
A. They understand the scope of the problem
B. They prioritize economic efficiency
C. They fail to see the full scope of environmental issues
D. They support sustainable development

**3. 【答案】B**

**4. 【答案】C**
