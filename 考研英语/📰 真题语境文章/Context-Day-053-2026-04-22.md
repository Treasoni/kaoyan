# 📰 真题语境文章 - Day 053

## English Text

What truly **matters** in **life**? This is a question that has occupied the minds of **many** a **man** and **woman** through the ages. In **large** cities and **little** **villages** alike, people search for meaning in their daily routines. Some seek it through **love** and **marriage**; others find it in the **language** of art, music, or science. **Maybe** the answer is simpler than we think.

Consider the story of a young woman who **left** her **husband's** **house** on a **March** morning. She had **learned** that **impossible** as it seemed, she needed to **live** by her own principles. She packed a **map**, a **knife**, and **little** else, and **left** for an **island** she had **heard about** from a friend. She was **not** **lazy** — she had **learned** to work hard since childhood. Her **grandmother** had taught her that a **good** education **matters** more than a **large** **house** or a **highway** of wealth.

On the **island**, she discovered a **lot** about herself. She **lived** in a small **hotel** near a **lake**, where every morning she could **hear** the **horses** running along the **hill**. She **listened** to the **loud** **sound** of the waves and felt **happy** for the first time in **many** **years**. She would **laugh** with the **maid** who cleaned her room, and **learn** about the **local** **language** from a **kind** **lady** at the **library**. The **lady's** **handwriting** was beautiful — each **letter** formed with care, as if she was writing to a **lover**.

It **hit** her one afternoon that **knowledge** is the greatest treasure. She had **left** her **job** in the **market**, **hoping** for a **better** life. Now she knew that **hope** alone was not enough — one had to **learn**, to **listen**, to **matter** to others. She remembered the **lesson** her father had taught her: "**Last** **but** **not** **least**, be **kind** to everyone you **meet**." His **words** **lasted** in her mind like a **hit** song that refuses to fade.

She returned home in **June**, just in time for the **last** **days** of spring. Her **husband** **met** her at the door with a **kiss** and a **lot** of questions. She told him about the **island**, the **lake**, the **library**, and the **kind** **lady**. She said she had **learned** that **love** is not about **marrying** into wealth or **housing** expensive things — it is about **living** with purpose. "It is **impossible** to be **happy** without **helping** others," she said. He **listened**, and for the first time, truly understood.

In the months that followed, they **worked** as a **team**. He **manned** the **market** **stall** during the day, while she taught **language** classes in the afternoon. They would **eat** **lunch** together, share **juice** and **grapes** from the **market**, and **go** for walks along the **highway** in the evening. On **holiday**s, they would **jump** on a **horse** and **ride** into the **hills**, feeling **free** and **alive**.

## 中文译文

在**生命**（life）中，什么真正**重要**（matters）？这个问题占据了**许多**（many）**男人**（man）和女人的心灵，贯穿了各个时代。在**大**（large）城市和**小**（little）村庄，人们都在日常事务中寻找意义。**一些**人通过**爱**（love）和**婚姻**（marriage）来寻找；另一些人则在艺术、音乐或科学的**语言**（language）中找到它。**也许**（Maybe）答案比我们想的更简单。

想想一个年轻女人的故事，她在一个**三月**（March）的早晨**离开**（left）了她**丈夫**（husband）的**房子**（house）。她已经**学到**（learned），尽管看起来**不可能**（impossible），但她需要按照自己的原则**生活**（live）。她装了一张**地图**（map）、一把**刀**（knife）和**很少的**（little）其他东西，**前往**（left for）一个她从朋友那里**听说**（heard about）的**岛屿**（island）。她**并不****懒惰**（lazy）——她从小就已经**学会**（learned）了努力工作。她的**奶奶**（grandmother）曾教导她，良好的教育比大**房子**（house）或财富的**公路**（highway）更**重要**（matters）。

在**岛**（island）上，她对自己有了**很多**（a lot）认识。她住在**湖**（lake）边的一家小**旅馆**（hotel），每天早晨她都能**听到**（hear）**马**（horses）沿着**小山**（hill）奔跑的声音。她**倾听**（listened）着**响亮的**（loud）海浪声，**许多**（many）**年**来第一次感到**快乐**（happy）。她会和打扫房间的**女仆**（maid）一起**笑**（laugh），在**图书馆**（library）从一位**善良的**（kind）**女士**（lady）那里**学习**（learn）当地**语言**（language）。那位女士的**笔迹**（handwriting）很美——每一个**字母**（letter）都精心书写，仿佛在写给一个**爱人**（lover）。

一天下午，她突然**意识到**（It hit her），**知识**是最大的财富。她曾**离开**（left）了**市场**（market）的**工作**（job），**希望**（hoping）过上**更好的**生活。现在她知道，仅凭**希望**（hope）是不够的——一个人必须**学习**（learn）、**倾听**（listen）、对他人**有意义**（matter）。她想起了父亲教给她的**教训**（lesson）："**最后**（Last）**但**同样重要的是，对每个你**遇到**的人都要**友善**（kind）。"他的**话语**在她脑海中**持续**（lasted）回荡，就像一首**热门的**（hit）歌曲不愿消逝。

她于**六月**（June）回家，正好赶上春天的**最后**（last）几天。她的**丈夫**（husband）在门口**迎接**（met）她，给了她一个**吻**（kiss）和**许多**（a lot）问题。她告诉他关于**岛**（island）、**湖**（lake）、**图书馆**（library）和那位**善良的**（kind）**女士**（lady）的事。她说她已经**学到**（learned），**爱**（love）不是通过**结婚**（marrying）获得财富或**安放**（housing）昂贵的东西——而是有目标地**生活**（living）。"如果没有**帮助**（helping）他人，就**不可能**（impossible）**幸福**（happy），"她说。他**倾听**（listened），并且第一次真正理解了。

在接下来的几个月里，他们作为一个**团队**一起**工作**。他白天**负责**（manned）**市场**（market）摊位，她下午教**语言**（language）课。他们会一起吃**午餐**（lunch），分享从**市场**（market）买来的**果汁**（juice）和**葡萄**（grapes），傍晚沿着**公路**（highway）散步。在**假日**（holidays），他们会**跳**（jump）上一匹**马**（horse），**骑**进**山丘**（hills），感到自由和**充满生机**（alive）。

> [!tip] 学习建议
> 1. 先通读英文，尝试理解生词含义
> 2. 对照中文译文，标记不认识的单词
> 3. 重点关注 ⚠️ 僻义标注的词汇：
>    - **matter** 在文中用作动词"要紧"（What truly matters），这是考研高频用法
>    - **last** 在文中同时出现形容词"最后的"和动词"持续"（lasted in her mind），注意区分
>    - **left** 在文中是 leave 的过去式"离开"，不是形容词"左边的"
>    - **hit** 在文中出现"It hit her"（她突然意识到）和"hit song"（热门歌曲），两种僻义
>    - **lesson** 在文中引申为"教训"（a lesson her father taught），不限于"课程"
