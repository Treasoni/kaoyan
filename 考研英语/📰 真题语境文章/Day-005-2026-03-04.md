### The ==Decline== of Traditional Academia: A New ==Fashion== in Learning?

In the **absence** of ==proper== understanding, many **ignorant** critics **deem** traditional education to be in ==decline==. Yet, this ==harsh== judgment fails to recognize the **profound** transformation occurring in our ==generation==.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **decline** 此处指"衰退"，但考研常考僻义"婉言拒绝"
> **harsh** 此处指"严酷的、苛刻的"，修饰 criticism/reality

Companies like Google and Microsoft have **generated** enormous wealth, yet some **celebrities** argue they have **ignored** their social responsibilities. These **giants** of industry, with their **gigantic** influence, have **fashioned** a new economic reality.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **fashion** 此处作动词，僻义"塑造、形成"，非常见义"时尚"
> **giant** 此处指"大公司"，非常见义"巨人"

The **situation** calls for careful analysis. We must **deduce** the root causes from available evidence, not make decisions based on **ignorance**. Some scholars, with a **certain** **genius** for observation, have **certified** that the relationship between technology and society is not in **harmony**.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **certain** 此处指"某个"，僻义
> **certify** 意为"证实、证明"

**Skeptical** observers point out that what we **celebrate** as progress may actually **harm** social ==cohesion.== The **ceremony** of graduation no longer **guarantees** success; **certificates** alone cannot prove **competence**.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **skeptical** = sceptical，意为"怀疑的"

In a **favorable** environment, innovation should **flourish**. Yet when regulations are **dedicated** to protecting **incumbents**, the result is often **stifling**. We must **cease** this practice and **deduct** the costs from our expectations.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **cease** 意为"停止"，搭配 cease doing/to do 有区别

The **glamour** of tech startups has **faded**. What remains is the **skeleton** of a system that needs **reform**. Every **site** of innovation must be **situated** within a broader **context**. This is not a **sketch** but a **complete** picture.

> [!quote] 语境分析
> **skeleton** 此处指"框架、梗概"
> **site** 此处指"地点、场所"

We should not **favor** one approach over another without evidence. Instead, we must **generate** new ideas, **gifted** with critical thinking, and **celebrate** the **generous** exchange of knowledge. Only then can we **make certain** that progress serves all.

## 📖 阅读理解练习

### 问题 1
According to the passage, what is the author's attitude toward traditional education?

- [ ] A. Completely opposed to it
- [ ] B. Recognizes its transformation
- [ ] C. Believes it has ceased to exist
- [ ] D. Thinks it should be ignored

### 问题 2
The word "decline" (paragraph 1) is closest in meaning to:

- [ ] A. refuse politely
- [ ] B. deterioration
- [ ] C. slope downward
- [ ] D. rejection

### 问题 3
What does the author imply about "certificates"?

- [ ] A. They guarantee success
- [ ] B. They are no longer valuable
- [ ] C. They alone cannot prove competence
- [ ] D. They should be abolished

### 问题 4
The phrase "in favor of" (not mentioned directly but implied) means:

- [ ] A. in memory of
- [ ] B. in support of
- [ ] C. in place of
- [ ] D. in search of

---

## 答案与解析

**Q1 答案：B**
> 解析：作者提到 "fails to recognize the **profound transformation**"，表明传统教育正在转型而非消失。

**Q2 答案：B**
> 解析：**decline** 在此处的意思是"衰退、衰落"。⚠️ 注意：考研中常考僻义"婉言拒绝"（decline an invitation）。

**Q3 答案：C**
> 解析：文章明确提到 "**certificates** alone cannot prove **competence**"。

**Q4 答案：B**
> 解析：**in favor of** 意为"支持、赞同"。例：vote in favor of the proposal（投票支持提案）。
