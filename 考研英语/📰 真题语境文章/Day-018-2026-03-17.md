## The Pursuit of Corporate Transparency

In an era of heightened **surveillance** over corporate practices, regulators have become increasingly **suspicious** of companies that fail to **reveal** their true financial positions. The **pursuit** of transparency has led to stricter regulations, with governments **issuing** new guidelines that **suspend** trading privileges for non-compliant firms.

Recent investigations **revealed** that several major corporations had been **suppressing** critical information about their **revenue** streams. The **resultant** scandal has **overwhelmed** investor confidence, leading to a sharp decline in stock prices. Analysts suggest that the **cumulative** effect of such disclosures could **surpass** the impact of previous corporate crises.

To **survive** in this new regulatory environment, companies must adopt a more **systematic** approach to disclosure. The **average** firm now faces **extreme** pressure to demonstrate **mutual** respect for shareholder interests. Those that fail to **resume** transparent practices risk being **suspended** from major exchanges.

The **logic** behind these reforms is clear: without **sufficient** transparency, markets cannot function efficiently. **Curiosity** about corporate practices has driven public demand for accountability. As a **result**, even traditionally secretive industries are being forced to **investigate** their own practices and **avoid** the **awkward** position of public scrutiny.

## 📖 中文译文

### 追求企业透明度

在企业行为受到高度**监视**的时代，监管机构对未能**揭示**真实财务状况的公司变得越来越**怀疑**。对透明度的**追求**导致了更严格的法规出台，政府**颁布**了新准则，**暂停**不合规公司的交易特权。

最近的调查**显示**，几家大公司一直在**压制**关于其**收入**流的关键信息。**随之而来的**丑闻**压垮**了投资者信心，导致股价急剧下跌。分析师认为，此类披露的**累积**效应可能**超过**之前企业危机的影响。

为了在这个新的监管环境中**生存**，公司必须采用更**系统**的披露方法。**普通**公司现在面临**极端**压力，需要展示对股东利益的**相互**尊重。那些未能**恢复**透明做法的公司面临被主要交易所**暂停**交易的风险。

这些改革背后的**逻辑**很清楚：没有**足够的**透明度，市场无法有效运作。对企业做法的**好奇**推动了公众对问责的需求。**结果**，即使是传统上保密的行业也被迫**调查**自己的做法，**避免**处于公众审查的**尴尬**境地。

## 📖 阅读理解练习

### Questions

**1. According to the passage, why have regulators become more suspicious of corporations?**

A. Because corporations have revealed too much information
B. Because some corporations failed to disclose their true financial positions
C. Because corporations are making too much revenue
D. Because the average firm is too transparent

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>
**B. Because some corporations failed to disclose their true financial positions**

原文依据："regulators have become increasingly suspicious of companies that fail to reveal their true financial positions."
</details>

---

**2. What is the "cumulative effect" mentioned in the passage?**

A. The effect of suspending trading privileges
B. The combined impact of multiple disclosures of corporate wrongdoing
C. The result of government investigations
D. The pressure on average firms

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>
**B. The combined impact of multiple disclosures of corporate wrongdoing**

原文依据："the cumulative effect of such disclosures could surpass the impact of previous corporate crises."
</details>

---

**3. What does the passage suggest companies must do to "survive" in the new regulatory environment?**

A. Increase their revenue streams
B. Adopt a more systematic approach to disclosure
C. Suppress more information
D. Avoid public curiosity

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>
**B. Adopt a more systematic approach to disclosure**

原文依据："companies must adopt a more systematic approach to disclosure."
</details>

---

**4. The word "resume" in the passage is closest in meaning to:**

A. Write a summary
B. Continue after interruption
C. Investigate thoroughly
D. Suppress completely

<details>
<summary>答案</summary>
**B. Continue after interruption**

resume = 重新开始；恢复（非"简历"义）
</details>
