## 📖 原文

In the **whole** of human history, entrepreneurs have played a crucial role in shaping societies. However, the challenges they face today are being **compounded** by a rapidly changing global landscape. **Whoever** wishes to succeed in this competitive environment must be **equipped** with not only technical skills but also a **diplomatic** approach to business relationships.

Consider the case of a young **entrepreneur** named Sarah, who founded a technology company specializing in communication **equipment**. Her initial **enthusiasm** was evident as she **collaborated** with various partners to develop **compatible** software systems. The **combination** of innovative technology and strategic partnerships seemed promising.

However, the crisis soon **compounded**. The **apparatus** of international trade regulations created unexpected barriers. Sarah's company was **refused entry** into several key markets. The **appraisal** of her business model by investors revealed fundamental flaws that she had **concealed** from her partners.

**While** Sarah struggled with these challenges, her **appetite** for success remained strong. She refused to **concede** defeat, believing that the **dimension** of her vision extended beyond immediate profits. With **compassion** for her employees and **wholly** committed to her mission, she sought to **address** the root causes of her company's problems.

The solution required a **coherent** strategy. Sarah realized she needed to be more **diligent** in her financial planning and less **arbitrary** in her decision-making. She **applauded** the efforts of her team while implementing strict but fair policies. The **compulsory** measures she introduced were not popular, but they were necessary.

**Whereas** many companies in similar situations would have **tortured** their employees with endless meetings or **tormented** them with unrealistic expectations, Sarah took a different approach. She maintained a **compact** organization structure and ensured that every department worked **in concert** rather than in isolation.

The results were **bizarre** by conventional standards—employee satisfaction increased **than ever before**, and the company eventually became a **wholly owned subsidiary** of a larger corporation. Sarah's **entrance** into the executive board of the parent company was met with **thunderous applause**.

**Whatever** challenges emerged, Sarah faced them with a clear sense of **direction**. She had **beat** the odds through a **cohesive** team effort and a **wholesome** company culture. Her story **exemplifies** how modern entrepreneurs must to **collaborate** effectively, **address** problems diplomatically, and maintain **enthusiasm** even in the face of **torrent** criticism.

然而，危机很快**加剧**。国际贸易监管**体系**制造了意想不到的障碍。Sarah的公司被**拒绝进入**几个主要市场。投资者对她商业模式的**评估**揭示了她向合作伙伴**隐瞒**的根本性缺陷。

**当**Sarah与这些挑战抗争时，她对成功的**渴望**依然强烈。她拒绝**承认**失败，相信她的愿景**维度**超越了眼前的利润。出于对员工的**同情**和对使命的**完全**投入，她试图**处理**公司问题的根本原因。

解决方案需要一个**连贯**的战略。Sarah意识到她需要在财务规划上更加**勤奋**，在决策上不那么**武断**。她**赞赏**团队的努力，同时实施严格但公平的政策。她引入的**强制性**措施并不受欢迎，但是必要的。

**然而**，许多处于类似情况的公司会用无休止的会议**折磨**员工，或用不切实际的期望**使他们痛苦**，而Sarah采取了不同的方法。她保持**紧凑**的组织结构，确保每个部门**协同**工作而不是孤立运作。

按照传统标准，结果有些**离奇**——员工满意度**比以往任何时候都**高**，公司最终成为一家更大公司的**全资子公司**。Sarah**进入**母公司执行董事会时，赢得了**雷鸣般的掌声**。

**无论**出现什么挑战， Sarah都以清晰的**方向**感来面对。她通过**有凝聚力的**团队努力和**有益健康的**公司文化**战胜**了困难。她的故事**例证**了现代企业家如何需要有效**合作**、**圆滑地处理**问题，并在面对**急流般的**批评时保持**热情**。

A. critical
B. admiring
C. indifferent
D. skeptical

**4. Which of the following words is used in its less common meaning in this passage?**
A. entrance (used as a noun meaning "doorway")
B. address (used as a verb meaning "to deal with")
C. company (used as a noun meaning "corporation")
