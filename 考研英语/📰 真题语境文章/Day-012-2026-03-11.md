# 📰 真题语境文章 - Day 012 (2026-03-11)

## The Double-Edged Sword of Technological Progress

The ==rapid growth== of artificial intelligence has ==signaled== a new era in human history. Governments worldwide are racing to ==implement== policies that can ==harness== this technology while ==guarding== against its potential dangers. The ==outcome== of this race will ==signify== nothing less than the future of human civilization.

A ==significant== body of ==literature== suggests that AI could ==increase== global ==income== by up to 15% over the next decade. However, critics ==deserve== to be heard when they warn that such ==growth== might ==incur== heavy social costs. Jobs could be ==deprived== of human meaning, and communities might ==grow== ==slack== in their social bonds if we do not ==designate== ==desirable== boundaries for technological deployment.

The ==mental== health ==impairment== observed among workers in highly automated environments is a case in point. When workers ==become== mere cogs in a machine, they often ==literally== lose their sense of purpose. This ==flaw== in our current economic ==design== ==deserves== immediate attention.

To ==flourish== in this new era, we must ==incorporate== a ==simple== principle: technology should ==derive== its purpose from human wellbeing, not the other way ==around==. The ==goal== should be to ==simplify== human life, not to ==hinder== genuine human connection.

==Otherwise==, we ==risk== creating a society where the ==gap== between the ==guaranteed== income of the elite and the ==slack== resources of the masses becomes ==increasingly== ==significant==. Such an ==outcome== would ==signal== a ==failure== of our collective ==memory== and ==conscience==.

### 技术进步的双刃剑

人工智能的**快速增长**标志着人类历史进入了新时代。各国政府纷纷竞相**实施**各项政策，既能**利用**这项技术，又能**防范**其潜在危险。这场竞赛的**结果**将**预示**人类文明的未来。

在高度自动化环境中观察到的工人**精神**健康**损害**就是一个典型例子。当工人**成为**机器中微不足道的齿轮时，他们往往**确实**会失去目标感。我们当前经济**设计**中的这一**缺陷****值得**立即关注。

要在这个新时代**蓬勃发展**，我们必须**融入**一个**简单**的原则：技术应该从人类福祉中**获得**其目的，而不是**相反**。**目标**应该是**简化**人类生活，而不是**阻碍**真正的人际联系。

**否则**，我们可能会创造出这样的社会：精英**有保障的**收入与大众**匮乏的**资源之间的**差距**变得**日益****重大**。这样的**结果**将**发出**我们集体**记忆**和**良心****失败**的**信号**。

## 📖 长难句分析

### 句子1
> "A ==significant== body of ==literature== suggests that AI could ==increase== global ==income== by up to 15% over the next decade."

**结构分析**:
- 主句：A significant body of literature suggests
- 宾语从句：that AI could increase global income by up to 15% over the next decade

**翻译**: 大量文献表明，人工智能在未来十年内可能使全球收入增长高达15%。

### 句子2
> "When workers ==become== mere cogs in a machine, they often ==literally== lose their sense of purpose."

**结构分析**:
- 时间状语从句：When workers become mere cogs in a machine
- 主句：they often literally lose their sense of purpose

**翻译**: 当工人沦为机器中的微小齿轮时，他们往往会真的失去目标感。

### 句子3
> "Such an ==outcome== would ==signal== a ==failure== of our collective ==memory== and ==conscience==."

**结构分析**:
- 主语：Such an outcome
- 谓语：would signal
- 宾语：a failure of our collective memory and conscience

**翻译**: 这样的结果将标志着我们集体记忆和良知的失败。
