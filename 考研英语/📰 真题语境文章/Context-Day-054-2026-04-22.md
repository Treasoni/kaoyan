## Passage: The Last Summer

> The following passage incorporates all 90 target words from Day 054.

In **January**, when the **last** snow was still falling, a **lady** named Mary sat in the **hotel** **kitchen**, watching her **grandmother** write a **letter** in her own **handwriting**. The old woman, who had **married** into a farming family, had **learned** to **husband** every resource — a lesson that **lasted** a lifetime.

"**It** **matters** **how** we **live**," her grandmother would often say, adjusting her **hat** and smoothing her **hair**. "**Love** is not just about **marriage**; **it** is about caring for others, **kind** and **good** at heart."

Mary **knew** this was true. She **remembered** the **happy** days when she would **jump** into the **lake** near the **hill**, **listen** to the **lion**-like roar of the **horse** on the **island**, and **laugh** out **loud** with her friends. They would **go** **into** the **library** after **lunch**, **read** **many** books, and **learn** a **great** **deal** about **language** and **market** economics. Her **husband**, a **man** who worked **on the job** at a **large** **internet** company, would **kiss** her and say, "**Maybe** we should **leave** the **house** and **go** **into** the **highway** of opportunity."

But **half** a year later, by **March**, things had changed. The **hope** that **hit** them like a **big** success began to fade. The **market** went **up** and then down. **Less** money was **left**. Her **homework** from the university — a **lesson** about **impossible** dreams — sat on the **table** **here**, unfinished. She had a **hunger** for something **more**, but the **hour** grew **late**.

"**Hello**, **me**," she said to **herself** in the mirror. "**Goodbye**, **lazy** days." She picked up her **knife** and **fork** at **dinner**, drank **juice** and ate **meat** with **grape**s from the **hotel** **market**. Her **maid**, a **kind** young **woman**, brought her a **map** and a **jacket**. "**Here** you are, **madam**. **Have** a **good** **holiday**."

"**May**be I should fly a **kite** instead," Mary **laughed**.

By **June**, she had **heard** about a new **job** in **July**. It was a **live** broadcast position — something she had always wanted. With the **help** of her **husband** and her **grandmother**, she packed her **bag** with a **kilo** of **snacks** and **a hundred** **little** things. She **left** **his** and **her** doubts behind. **Man**y people, including **him**, **her** friends, and even **itself** — the fear that had held **them** back — could not stop her now.

She **headed** for the **line** where the road met the sky. With **every** **step**, she felt **like** she could **last** forever.

## 参考译文

一月，当最后一场雪仍在飘落时，一位名叫玛丽的女士坐在旅馆的厨房里，看着她的祖母用亲笔书写一封信。这位老人嫁入了一个农民家庭，学会了节约每一份资源——这是一堂持续了一辈子的课。

"我们如何生活很重要，"她的祖母常常这样说，一边整理帽子，一边抚平头发。"爱不仅仅是婚姻；它是关于关心他人，善良而内心美好。"

玛丽知道这是真的。她记得那些快乐的日子，她会在小山旁的湖里跳进去，听岛上马匹如狮子般的嘶鸣，和朋友们大声笑。午饭后，她们会去图书馆，读许多书，学到了很多关于语言和市场经济的知识。她的丈夫，一个在大型互联网公司上班的男人，会亲吻她并说："也许我们应该离开房子，走上机遇的公路。"

但半年后，到了三月，事情发生了变化。像巨大成功一样击中他们的希望开始消退。市场上涨又下跌。剩下的钱更少了。她在大学的家庭作业——一篇关于不可能梦想的论文——就放在这里的桌子上，未完成。她渴望更多的东西，但时间越来越晚。

"你好，我，"她对着镜子里的自己说。"再见，懒惰的日子。"晚餐时她拿起刀叉，喝果汁，吃着旅馆市场买来的葡萄和肉。她的女仆，一个善良的年轻女子，给她带来了地图和夹克。"给您，夫人。祝您假期愉快。"

"也许我应该去放风筝，"玛丽笑着说。

到了六月，她听说七月有一份新工作。那是一个直播职位——她一直想要的。在丈夫和祖母的帮助下，她收拾了一公斤零食和一百件小东西的行李。她把他的和她的疑虑都抛在了身后。许多人，包括他、她的朋友，甚至恐惧本身——那个一直阻碍他们的东西——现在都拦不住她了。

她朝道路与天空交汇的界线走去。每走一步，她都觉得自己可以永远持续下去。
