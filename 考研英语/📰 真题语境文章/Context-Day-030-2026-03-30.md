## 📝 写作模仿句型

### 高频句型

1. **X paves the way for Y**
   - Education paves the way for success.
   - Innovation paves the way for progress.

2. **The tension between X and Y**
   - The tension between tradition and modernity.
   - The tension between work and life balance.

3. **X must have faith in Y**
   - We must have faith in our ability.
   - Leaders must have faith in their teams.

4. **X permits Y to Z**
   - Technology permits us to work remotely.
   - Education permits students to reach their potential.

> - permeate 在抽象语境中表示"传播、扩散"
