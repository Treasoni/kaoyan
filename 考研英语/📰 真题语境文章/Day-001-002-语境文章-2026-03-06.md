### Digital Transformation: Balancing Efficiency and Risk

In the past **decade**, businesses across various sectors have sought to **upgrade** their operational capabilities through digital transformation. This **widespread** shift represents a **decisive** **factor** in determining whether companies can **thrive** in an increasingly competitive marketplace. However, the path to digital **efficiency** is fraught with challenges that require careful consideration.

**General** managers must **face** the reality that technological **updates** come with inherent risks. A **recent** **database** analysis revealed that nearly 60% of digital transformation projects **fail** to achieve their intended goals. This **alarming** **statistical** **data** suggests that companies often **overlook** critical aspects of implementation.

One **major** **threat** lies in the **gap** between technological potential and human **ability** to adapt. While new systems can dramatically improve work **efficiency**, employees may lack the necessary skills to **operate** them **effectively**. This **discrepancy** can lead to **widespread** resistance within the organization, potentially causing the entire initiative to **fade** into obscurity.

To **secure** successful outcomes, business leaders must take **effective** **measures** to **address** these challenges. First, they should **abandon** the "one-size-fits-all" approach and instead **embrace** tailored solutions that reflect their specific organizational culture. Second, they must ensure that all stakeholders have the **opportunity** to **participate** in the transformation process, fostering a sense of ownership and commitment.

**Meanwhile**, companies must also **shield** themselves from external **threats**. Cyber **security** has become a paramount concern, as digital systems are increasingly **vulnerable** to malicious attacks. A single breach can **oppress** an organization's financial stability and reputation. Therefore, investing in robust security protocols is not merely an option—it is an **urgent** necessity.

The **ultimate** goal should be to create a **balance** between innovation and stability. By **seeking** this equilibrium, organizations can **utilize** technology to enhance their operations while minimizing exposure to risk. After all, true progress is not about abandoning traditional principles, but about **evolving** them to meet contemporary demands.

## 📝 阅读理解练习

> [!example] 真题风格练习题

### Questions 1-3

**1. According to the passage, what percentage of digital transformation projects fail to achieve their goals?**

A. 40%
B. 50%
C. 60%
D. 70%

**2. The author identifies which of the following as a "major threat" to digital transformation?**

A. Financial instability
B. The gap between technology and human adaptability
C. Lack of management support
D. Outdated database systems

**3. What does the author suggest as the "ultimate goal" of digital transformation?**

A. To replace all traditional systems
B. To create a balance between innovation and stability
C. To achieve maximum efficiency at all costs
D. To eliminate all external threats

---

### 答案与解析

> [!tip] 答案: 1. C | 2. B | 3. B

**Question 1 解析**:
- 定位词: percentage, fail
- 定位句: "A recent database analysis revealed that nearly 60% of digital transformation projects fail..."
- 答案: C (60%)

**Question 2 解析**:
- 定位词: major threat
- 定位句: "One major threat lies in the gap between technological potential and human ability to adapt."
- 答案: B (The gap between technology and human adaptability)

**Question 3 解析**:
- 定位词: ultimate goal
- 定位句: "The ultimate goal should be to create a balance between innovation and stability."
- 答案: B (To create a balance between innovation and stability)
