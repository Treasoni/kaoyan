## 📖 原文

In the **decisive** **decade** ahead, the **destructive** impact of **pollution** on our environment has become a matter of **urgent** concern. Scientists **inquire** into the causes of environmental **destruction**, and their findings are **remarkable**.

> 📖 **译文**：在未来**决定性**的**十年**中，**污染**对环境的**破坏性**影响已成为一项**紧急**关切。科学家们**调查**环境**毁灭**的原因，他们的发现是**非凡的**。

The **legacy** of industrial development has left us with **innumerable** challenges. **A cluster of** cities now face **desperate** air quality issues, and many residents are driven to **despair**. **It is remarkable that** despite these challenges, we have failed to take **effective** **measures** to address the crisis.

> 📖 **译文**：工业发展的**遗产**给我们留下了**无数的**挑战。**一群**城市现在面临**严重的**空气质量问题，许多居民陷入**绝望**。**不同寻常的是**，尽管面临这些挑战，我们却未能采取**有效措施**来解决危机。

Environmental **engineering** offers hope. Engineers are working to develop cleaner **engines** and **sustainable** energy solutions. They **contend** that by **pooling** our resources and expertise, we can **sustain** economic growth while protecting the environment. The **destination** is clear: a cleaner, healthier future.

> 📖 **译文**：环境**工程**带来了希望。工程师们正在努力开发更清洁的**发动机**和**可持续的**能源解决方案。他们**主张**，通过**汇集**我们的资源和专业知识，我们可以在保护环境的同时**维持**经济增长。**目的地**很明确：一个更清洁、更健康的未来。

However, we must **beware of** those who **oppose** necessary regulations. Their arguments often **bewilder** the public and delay crucial action. **Lest** we forget, the **ancient** civilizations that ignored environmental warnings eventually collapsed.

> 📖 **译文**：然而，我们必须**小心**那些**反对**必要监管的人。他们的论点常常**使公众困惑**，延误关键行动。**以免**我们忘记，那些忽视环境警告的**古代**文明最终都崩溃了。

**In conclusion**, the **tide** of public opinion is **switching** toward environmental protection. We must **strike a balance** between development and sustainability, **enrich** our understanding of ecological systems, and **enroll** more young people in environmental sciences. The **destiny** of our planet depends on it.

> 📖 **译文**：**总之**，公众舆论的**趋势**正在**转向**环境保护。我们必须在发展和可持续性之间**达成平衡**，**丰富**我们对生态系统的理解，并**招收**更多年轻人学习环境科学。我们星球的**命运**取决于此。

## 🔑 词汇记忆技巧

### 词根词缀法

1. **destruction** = de-（向下、去除）+ struct（建造）+ -ion（名词后缀）→ 毁灭
2. **destructive** = destruct + -ive（形容词后缀）→ 破坏性的
3. **innumerable** = in-（不）+ numer（数）+ -able（能够）→ 数不清的
4. **bewilder** = be-（使）+ wilder（荒野）→ 使困惑（像在荒野中迷路）
5. **enrich** = en-（使）+ rich（丰富）→ 使丰富

### 联想记忆法

- **cluster** → 葡萄一串串 → 一群、一簇
- **tide** → 潮水涨落 → 趋势（像潮水一样变化）
- **destiny** → destination（目的地）→ 命运（人生的终点）
- **lest** → last（最后）→ 以免（最后才想到）
