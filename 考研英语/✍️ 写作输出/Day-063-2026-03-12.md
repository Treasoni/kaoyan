## 🚀 Part I: 写作词汇替换升级（5题）

> [!summary] 题目说明
> 将普通表达替换为高级词汇，提升作文档次。参考今日单词表完成替换。

**1. 原句**：The company has built a **complex** system to manage user data.
**替换**：The company has built a ________ system to manage user data.
**目标词**：sophisticated
**升级后**：The company has built a **sophisticated** system to manage user data.
**提升效果**：complex → sophisticated（复杂的→精密的、复杂的）

---

**2. 原句**：The government **forced** new regulations on the tech industry.
**替换**：The government ________ new regulations on the tech industry.
**目标词**：impose
**升级后**：The government **imposed** new regulations on the tech industry.
**提升效果**：forced → imposed（强制→强制实行、施加）

---

**3. 原句**：The project has made a **remarkable** achievement in environmental protection.
**替换**：The project has made an ________ achievement in environmental protection.
**目标词**：impressive
**升级后**：The project has made an **impressive** achievement in environmental protection.
**提升效果**：remarkable → impressive（引人注目的→令人印象深刻的）

---

**4. 原句**：This theory **began** from earlier research in the 1990s.
**替换**：This theory ________ from earlier research in the 1990s.
**目标词**：originate
**升级后**：This theory **originated** from earlier research in the 1990s.
**提升效果**：began → originated（开始→起源于、发端于）

---

**5. 原句**：The two parties finally reached a **sensible** agreement after long negotiations.
**替换**：The two parties finally reached a ________ agreement after long negotiations.
**目标词**：reasonable
**升级后**：The two parties finally reached a **reasonable** agreement after long negotiations.
**提升效果**：sensible → reasonable（明智的→合理的、明智的）

## 🌏 Part II: 汉译英练习（5题）

> [!summary] 题目说明
> 根据中文提示，使用今日单词表中的词汇完成翻译。注意动词时态和介词搭配。

**6. 这项新政策将对整个行业产生深远影响。**

参考译文：The new policy will have **far-reaching implications** for the entire industry.

**重点词汇**：
- implication (n. 可能的影响、含义)
- far-reaching (adj. 深远的)

---

**7. 政府决定对进口商品实施更严格的限制。**

参考译文：The government decided to **impose** stricter restrictions **on** imported goods.

**重点词汇**：
- impose (vt. 强制实行、施加)
- 搭配：impose sth on/upon sth

---

**8. 这个理论可以追溯到20世纪初的研究。**

参考译文：This theory **originated from** research in the early 20th century.

**重点词汇**：
- originate (v. 起源于、发端于)
- 搭配：originate from/in

---

**9. 经过长时间谈判，双方最终达成了和平协议。**

参考译文：After lengthy negotiations, the two parties finally reached a peaceful **settlement**.

**重点词汇**：
- settlement (n. 协议、解决)
- 搭配：reach a settlement

---

**10. 他试图给别人留下知识渊博的印象。**

参考译文：He tried to **give the impression of** being knowledgeable.

**重点词汇**：
- impression (n. 印象)
- 搭配：give the impression of

## ⚖️ Part III: 词义辨析（5题）

> [!summary] 题目说明
> 区分易混淆词汇，选择最恰当的单词完成句子。

---

### 11. impress vs. pressure

**impress** (v.)：使印象深刻，压印
**pressure** (n./v.)：压力，施压

**题目**：The professor ________ the importance of critical thinking on his students.

A. impressed
B. pressured
C. impressive
D. pressure

**答案**：**A. impressed**

**解析**：
- **impress sth on sb** = 使某人铭记某事（正确搭配）
- pressure 通常作名词使用，作动词时意为"对…施压"

**句子翻译**：教授使他的学生铭记批判性思维的重要性。

---

### 12. implicit vs. explicit

**implicit** (adj.)：含蓄的，不言明的，内含的
**explicit** (adj.)：明确的，清楚的，直率的

**题目**：The contract contains ________ terms that require careful reading.

A. implicit
B. explicit
C. impressed
D. implied

**答案**：**A. implicit**

**解析**：
- **implicit terms** = 隐含条款（需要仔细阅读才能发现）
- explicit terms = 明确条款（一目了然）
- 语境中"require careful reading"暗示是隐含的

**句子翻译**：合同包含需要仔细阅读的隐含条款。

---

### 13. impose vs. expose

**impose** (vt.)：强制实行，将…强加于
**expose** (vt.)：暴露，揭露，使接触

**题目**：The new regulations ________ additional costs on small businesses.

A. imposed
B. exposed
C. imposed on
D. exposed to

**答案**：**A. imposed**

**解析**：
- **impose sth on sb** = 将某事强加给某人/某事（正确用法）
- expose sth = 暴露某事（不合语境）
- 注意：impose后直接接宾语，on是介词

**句子翻译**：新规定给小企业强加了额外成本。

---

### 14. shelter vs. shield

**shelter** (n./v.)：庇护所；保护，掩蔽
**shield** (n./v.)：盾；保护，防御

**题目**：The UN provided ________ for thousands of refugees fleeing the conflict.

A. shelter
B. shield
C. sheltering
D. shielding

**答案**：**A. shelter**

**解析**：
- **provide shelter for** = 为…提供庇护所/住所
- shield 侧重于"防御、挡住"（如shield from harm）
- 语境中"refugees"（难民）需要住所

**句子翻译**：联合国为逃离冲突的数千名难民提供了庇护所。

---

### 15. sovereign vs. supreme

**sovereign** (adj./n.)：至高无上的，有主权的；君主
**supreme** (adj.)：最高的，至高无上的

**题目**：Each nation has the ________ right to determine its own economic policies.

A. sovereign
B. supreme
C. superior
D. superficial

**答案**：**A. sovereign**

**解析**：
- **sovereign right** = 主权权利（国家特有的权利）
- supreme 更多指"最高的"（如Supreme Court 最高法院）
- 语境中"nation"和"economic policies"暗示主权概念

**句子翻译**：每个国家都有决定自身经济政策的主权权利。

## 📝 写作应用建议

### 论证写作（大作文）

| 功能 | 推荐词汇 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **提出影响** | implication | This policy has far-reaching **implications**. |
| **实施措施** | impose | The government should **impose** restrictions on... |
| **描述复杂** | sophisticated | Modern technology is highly **sophisticated**. |
| **达成协议** | settlement | Reach a peaceful **settlement**. |
| **表示合理** | reasonable | A **reasonable** solution should be found. |

### 图表描述（小作文）

| 功能 | 推荐词汇 | 例句 |
|------|----------|------|
| **急剧变化** | sharp | There was a **sharp** increase in... |
| **阶段性变化** | phase | Enter a new **phase** of development. |
| **描述现象** | phenomenon | This is a common social **phenomenon**. |
| **令人印象深刻的** | impressive | The **impressive** growth rate... |
