## Part A：写作替换升级（4题）

**升级以下句子中的基础词汇，使用今日学习的高级词汇：**

### 1. 基础句：The new policy **helps** the development of small businesses.
> 目标词汇：facilitate

<details>
<summary>点击查看升级版本</summary>

**升级句**：The new policy **facilitates** the development of small businesses.

**解析**：
- help → facilitate（更正式，强调"使...更容易"）
- facilitate + development/growth/process 是高频搭配

</details>

---

### 2. 基础句：The government should **make** a plan to solve this problem.
> 目标词汇：formulate, scheme

<details>
<summary>点击查看升级版本</summary>

**升级句**：The government should **formulate** a comprehensive **scheme** to address this issue.

**解析**：
- make a plan → formulate a scheme（更专业、正式）
- address 比 solve 更适合用于问题解决类话题

</details>

---

### 3. 基础句：We need to take **good** actions to protect the environment.
> 目标词汇：effective, measures

<details>
<summary>点击查看升级版本</summary>

**升级句**：We need to **implement** **effective measures** to protect the environment.

**解析**：
- take good actions → implement effective measures
- implement（实施）+ measures（措施）是标准搭配

</details>

---

### 4. 基础句：The company's **inside** problems affected its performance.
> 目标词汇：internal, external

<details>
<summary>点击查看升级版本</summary>

**升级句**：The company's **internal** issues and **external** pressures significantly affected its performance.

**解析**：
- inside → internal（更正式）
- 可加入 external 形成对比，增加论述深度

</details>

## Part C：词义辨析（3题）

### 8. **facility vs faculty vs facilitate**

请选择正确的词填空：

a) The university's science ______ includes state-of-the-art laboratories.
b) She has a remarkable ______ for learning languages.
c) The new software will ______ the workflow.

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案**：
a) facility（设施）
b) faculty（能力/天赋）
c) facilitate（促进，动词）

**记忆口诀**：
- facility 以 -ity 结尾 → 名词"设施"
- faculty 以 -ty 结尾 → 名词"能力/教员"
- facilitate 以 -ate 结尾 → 动词"促进"

</details>

---

### 9. **general vs generalize vs generally**

请选择正确的词填空：

a) ______ speaking, the results were positive.
b) We should not ______ from a single case.
c) The ______ public supports the new policy.

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案**：
a) Generally（一般地，副词）
b) generalize（概括，动词）
c) general（一般的，形容词）

**词族关系**：
- general（adj. 一般的）→ generally（adv. 一般地）→ generalize（v. 概括）

</details>

---

### 10. **internal vs external vs eternal**

请选择正确的词填空：

a) The company faces both ______ and ______ challenges.
b) Some people believe in ______ life.

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案**：
a) internal, external（内部的，外部的）
b) eternal（永恒的）

**词根记忆**：
- inter-（内部）→ internal
- exter-（外部）→ external
- eter-（永远）→ eternal

</details>

## 📝 写作模板句型

### 1. 政策建议类

> **To address [issue], the government should implement effective measures to facilitate [goal].**

示例：To address urban congestion, the government should implement effective measures to facilitate public transportation.

---

### 2. 原因分析类

> **Both internal and external factors contribute to [phenomenon].**

示例：Both internal and external factors contribute to the company's declining performance.

---

### 3. 现状描述类

> **The [project/debate/reform] is still in embryo, requiring further [development/refinement].**

示例：The educational reform is still in embryo, requiring further refinement and stakeholder consultation.
