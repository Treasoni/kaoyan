## Part 2: 汉译英练习

> 使用本日词汇翻译以下句子。

### 基础翻译

1. 这个项目持续了三年。
   - The project **lasted** for three years.

2. 音乐会将在电视上现场直播。
   - The concert will be broadcast **live** on television.

3. 她正在休产假。
   - She is on maternity **leave**.

4. 这不重要。
   - It doesn't **matter**.

5. 这部电影获得了巨大的成功。
   - The movie was a huge **hit**.

6. 我们需要节约有限的资源。
   - We need to **husband** our limited resources.

7. 中国向欧洲出口各种商品。
   - China exports various **goods** to Europe.

8. 请严格按照说明操作。
   - Please follow the instructions to the **letter**.

### 进阶翻译

9. 市场经济中的语言障碍可能会影响国际贸易。
   - **Language** barriers in the **market** economy may affect international trade.

10. 她希望用自己的方式帮助他人，这是不可能实现的梦想吗？
    - She **hopes** to **help** others in her own way — is it an **impossible** dream?

11. 据说，很多人已经离开了大城市，因为他们付不起房价。
    - It is said that **many** people have **left** large cities because they cannot afford **house** prices.

12. 学习一门外语需要大量时间和精力，但这是值得的。
    - **Learning** a foreign **language** requires a **great deal** of time and effort, but it is **good** and **worthwhile**.

## Part 4: 词义辨析写作

> 用以下易混淆词组各造一个句子。

### 1. live (v.) vs live (adj.)
- v.: Many families **live** in this area.
- adj.: The **live** performance attracted a large audience.

### 2. leave (v.) vs leave (n.)
- v.: Please **leave** your bags at the door.
- n.: She took a two-week **leave** from work.

### 3. last (adj.) vs last (v.)
- adj.: This is my **last** chance.
- v.: The battery will **last** for two days.

### 4. good (adj.) vs goods (n.)
- adj.: The weather is **good** today.
- n.: The store sells various **goods**.

### 5. hit (v.) vs hit (n.)
- v.: The car **hit** the tree.
- n.: The song was a **hit** across the country.
