## 一、词汇替换练习

### 1. 用高级词汇替换划线词

**原文**：The problem is very *big*.
**替换**：The problem is **tremendous**.

---

**原文**：She was *unwilling* to accept the offer.
**替换**：She was **reluctant** to accept the offer.

---

**原文**：The plan is *workable*.
**替换**：The plan is **feasible**.

---

**原文**：He *announced* his intention to run for president.
**替换**：He **declared** his intention to run for president.

---

**原文**：This is a *natural* part of the process.
**替换**：This is an **inherent** part of the process.

---

**原文**：The situation *got worse* rapidly.
**替换**：The situation **degenerated** rapidly.

---

**原文**：I am *thankful* for your support.
**替换**：I am **grateful** for your support. / I express my **gratitude** for your support.

---

**原文**：The company *released* a new product.
**替换**：The company **launched/released** a new product.

---

**原文**：This helped to *reduce* the pressure.
**替换**：This helped to **relieve/alleviate** the pressure.

---

**原文**：The economy *dropped sharply* last year.
**替换**：The economy **plunged** last year.

## 二、汉译英练习

### 基础句型

1. **政府宣布将采取新措施来解决贸易逆差问题。**
   - The government **declared** that it would take new measures to address the **trade deficit**.

2. **这个计划在技术上是可行的，但需要巨大的努力。**
   - This plan is technically **feasible**, but it requires **tremendous** efforts.

3. **令我宽慰的是，考试比我预期的要简单。**
   - **To my relief**, the exam was easier than I had expected.

4. **这些缺陷是系统中固有的，很难修复。**
   - These **defects** are **inherent** in the system and difficult to fix.

5. **他对继承的财产表示感激。**
   - He expressed **gratitude** for the property he had **inherited**.

---

### 进阶句型

6. **尽管做出了巨大努力，情况还是恶化成了危机。**
   - Despite **tremendous** efforts, the situation has **degenerated** into a crisis.

7. **债券市场的暴跌使投资者感到不安。**
   - The **plunge** in the **bond** market has made investors uneasy.

8. **许多员工不愿意接受新的工作安排。**
   - Many employees are **reluctant** to accept the new work arrangements.

9. **财政大臣在宣言中承诺减少预算赤字。**
   - The **Chancellor** promised in his **declaration** to reduce the **budget deficit**.

10. **这种内在的联系使它们成为一个整体。**
    - This **inherent** **bond** makes them a whole.

---

### 高级句型（含从句）

11. **虽然这个计划看起来可行，但其固有的缺陷可能会导致它失败。**
    - While this plan appears **feasible**, its **inherent defects** may cause it to fail.

12. **令我感到宽慰的是，政府已经宣布了应对经济危机的措施。**
    - **To my relief**, the government has **declared** measures to address the economic crisis.

13. **这个巨大的项目需要我们投入大量资源，否则它可能会恶化。**
    - This **tremendous** project requires us to invest significant resources, otherwise it may **degenerate**.

14. **那些不愿意改变的人将很难适应新环境。**
    - Those who are **reluctant** to change will find it difficult to adapt to the new environment.

15. **他继承的不仅是财富，还有对慈善事业的热情。**
    - What he **inherited** was not only wealth but also a passion for **charity**.

## 三、写作常用句式

### 表达观点
- I am **reluctant** to conclude that... 我不情愿地得出结论...
- It is **feasible** to assume that... 可以合理地假设...
- The **gravity** of the situation suggests that... 情况的严重性表明...

### 表达因果关系
- The **deficit** has led to... 赤字导致了...
- This has **relieved** the pressure on... 这减轻了...的压力
- The situation has **degenerated** into... 情况已经恶化成...

### 表达情感
- **To my relief**,... 令我宽慰的是，...
- I would like to express my **gratitude** for... 我想表达我对...的感激
- I am **grateful** for... 我对...感到感激

### 表达程度
- **tremendous** efforts 巨大的努力
- **grave** consequences 严重的后果
- **inherent** risks 固有的风险

## 五、同义词/反义词拓展
