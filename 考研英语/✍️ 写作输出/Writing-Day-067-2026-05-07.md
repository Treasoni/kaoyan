## ✍️ Part 3: 写作应用场景

### 场景1: 环境保护类作文

**题目**: The Path to Sustainable Development

**范文段落**:

> With the **trajectory** of climate change becoming increasingly dangerous, **sustainable** development is no longer optional—it is essential. **Stringent** regulations on emissions must replace **unconventional** approaches that prioritize short-term gains. We must **transition** from fossil fuels to renewable energy, even if this requires economic **subsidies** to support affected industries. **Ultimately**, the question is not whether we can afford this **titanic** effort, but whether we can afford not to act.

**重点词汇**:
- trajectory (轨迹)
- sustainable (可持续的)
- stringent (严格的)
- unconventional (不墨守成规的)
- transition (转变)
- subsidies (补助)
- ultimately (最终)
- titanic (巨大的)

---

### 场景2: 科技与社会类作文

**题目**: Technology and Social Change

**范文段落**:

> We find ourselves in **uncharted** territory as artificial intelligence reshapes society. The **subconscious** impact of constant connectivity is still being studied. **Unconventional** thinking is needed to address the challenges posed by automation. **Transparent** policies and **unbiased** algorithms are essential to ensure fairness. As we **traverse** this new landscape, we must remember that technology should serve humanity, not the other way around.

**重点词汇**:
- uncharted (未知的)
- subconscious (潜意识)
- unconventional (不墨守成规的)
- transparent (透明的)
- unbiased (公正的)
- traverse (横越)

---

### 场景3: 政府与治理类作文

**题目**: Good Governance and Fair Society

**范文段落**:

> Good governance requires **transparent** institutions and **unbiased** decision-making. **Stringent** anti-corruption measures are necessary to maintain public trust. The path forward requires **strategic** planning and **structural** reforms. We must avoid **uneven** development that creates **unfairness** in society. With **ultimately** a commitment to equity, we can build institutions that serve all citizens fairly.

**重点词汇**:
- transparent (透明的)
- unbiased (公正的)
- stringent (严格的)
- strategic (战略性的)
- structural (结构上的)
- uneven (不平衡的)
- unfairness (不公平)
- ultimately (最重要地)

## 💡 写作提示

### 常用句型结构

1. **Ultimately, the question becomes...**
   > 最终，问题变成了......

2. **We find ourselves in uncharted territory...**
   > 我们发现自己处于未知领域......

3. **...stand in stark contrast**
   > ......形成鲜明对比

4. **...is no longer optional—it is essential**
   > ......不再是可选项——而是必不可少的

5. **With ... commitment, we can...**
   > 有了......的承诺，我们能够......
