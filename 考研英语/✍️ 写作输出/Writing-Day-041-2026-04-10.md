## 练习二：汉译英（5题）

将以下中文句子翻译为英文，使用今日所学词汇：

**1.** 经过深入思考，政府决定干预这场争端，以防止暴力冲突进一步升级。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> After ==contemplat==ing the situation, the government decided to ==intervene== in the ==strife== to prevent further ==explosive== ==friction== between the two parties.

**2.** 他坦诚地承认，在从自由职业者过渡到全职员工的过程中，经历了许多艰难的挣扎。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> He ==frank==ly admitted that ==transition==ing from a ==freelance== career to a full-time position involved considerable ==struggle==.

**3.** 这座透明的建筑保留了原有的框架结构，同时又巧妙地改造成了现代化的录音棚。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The ==transparent== building retained its original ==framework== ==intact== while being ingeniously ==convert==ed into a modern ==studio==.

**4.** 那个固执的囚犯被指控犯有欺诈罪，但他的律师试图说服陪审团相信他的清白。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The ==stubborn== ==convict== was charged with ==fraud==, but his lawyer tried to ==convince== the ==audience== of the jury of his innocence.

**5.** 频繁的干旱迫使许多家庭离开故土，踏上漫长的远征以寻找新的生计。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The ==frequent== ==drought==s forced many families to leave their homeland and undertake a long ==expedition== in search of a new means of survival.

## 练习三：写作段落（2题）

使用指定词汇写一段约80-100词的英文段落：

**题目1**：讨论在危机时期，政府是否应当介入社会事务
**要求使用词汇**：intervene, convince, transparent, authentic, framework

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> During times of social crisis, the question of whether a government should ==intervene== is highly debated. Critics argue that excessive involvement ==interfere==s with individual ==freedom==, while supporters maintain that a clear legal ==framework== is necessary to protect citizens. What matters most is that any intervention is ==transparent== and based on ==authentic== data rather than political motives. To ==convince== the public, governments must demonstrate that their actions genuinely serve the common good, not merely expand their own authority. (80 words)

**题目2**：描述一个人克服困难、追求梦想的经历
**要求使用词汇**：strive, stumble, sturdy, expedition, convert

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> Despite the ==frequent== ==obstacle==s she encountered, Maria continued to ==strive== toward her goal of becoming a marine biologist specialising in ==submarine== ecosystems. She would often ==stumble== upon setbacks — failed experiments, rejected proposals, and ==brutal== criticism — yet her ==sturdy== determination never wavered. After joining a research ==expedition== to the Pacific, she managed to ==convert== years of ==drift==ing uncertainty into focused purpose. Her journey proved that ==struggle==, when met with ==conviction==, can be ==transform==ed into the strongest foundation for success. (81 words)

## 练习四：近义词辨析

| 易混词组 | 含义区别 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| convince vs convict vs conversion | convince = v. 说服，使确信；convict = v. 定罪 / n. 罪犯；conversion = n. 转变，转化（名词形式） | I managed to **convince** him of the truth. / The jury **convict**ed the defendant. / The **conversion** of the warehouse into apartments took two years. |
| intervene vs interfere | intervene = v. 介入，调停（中性/褒义，为解决问题而介入）；interfere = v. 干涉，妨碍（贬义，插手他人的事） | The UN **intervene**d to restore peace. / Please don't **interfere** in my personal affairs. |
| transition vs transit vs translate | transition = n./v. 过渡，转变（强调过程）；transit = n. 运输，过境（强调移动）；translate = v. 翻译，转化 | The **transition** from school to work is challenging. / The goods are in **transit**. / Can you **translate** this document into English? |
| transparent vs explicit | transparent = adj. 透明的，坦率的（强调可见性）；explicit = adj. 明确的，清楚的（强调表达直接无歧义） | The company's financial reports should be **transparent**. / She gave **explicit** instructions about what to do. |
| convert vs converse vs conversely | convert = v. 转变，兑换；converse = v. 交谈 / adj. 相反的；conversely = adv. 相反地（连接词） | He **convert**ed to Buddhism. / They **converse**d for hours. / She is tall; **conversely**, her sister is short. |
| convenience vs convenient | convenience = n. 便利，方便（名词）；convenient = adj. 方便的，便利的（形容词） | The hotel offers every modern **convenience**. / Is this time **convenient** for you? |
| strive vs struggle | strive = v. 努力奋斗，力求（强调为目标积极努力）；struggle = v./n. 挣扎，斗争（强调过程艰难痛苦） | We should **strive** for excellence. / Many families **struggle** to make ends meet. |
| strip vs stripe | strip = v. 剥去 / n. 狭长条（动作/结果）；stripe = n. 条纹（图案样式） | He **strip**ped off his wet clothes. / The zebra has black and white **stripe**s. |
| draft vs drift | draft = n. 草稿 / v. 起草；drift = v. 漂流，随波逐流 / n. 漂流 | I'm still working on the first **draft**. / The boat **drift**ed out to sea. |
| crash vs crush vs craft | crash = v./n. 碰撞，坠毁（强调猛烈撞击）；crush = v. 压碎，镇压 / n. 迷恋；craft = n. 手艺，工艺 / v. 精心制作 | The car **crash**ed into the wall. / The revolution was **crush**ed. / She is skilled in the **craft** of pottery. |
| crack vs crash | crack = v./n. 裂开，断裂（强调出现裂缝但未必完全碎裂）；crash = v./n. 猛撞，坠毁（强调剧烈碰撞） | The mirror **crack**ed but didn't break. / The stock market **crash**ed overnight. |
| brutal vs brute | brutal = adj. 残忍的，野蛮的（强调性质）；brute = n. 野兽，残忍的人 / adj. 蛮横的（强调力量或本能） | The dictator was a **brutal** ruler. / He used **brute** force to open the door. |
| buffer vs buffet | buffer = n. 缓冲，缓冲区 / v. 缓冲；buffet = n. 自助餐 / v. 连续猛击，打击 | The forest acts as a **buffer** against floods. / The wind **buffet**ed the small boat. / We had dinner at the **buffet**. |
| authentic vs aural vs audio | authentic = adj. 真实的，可靠的；aural = adj. 听觉的（与耳朵相关）；audio = n./adj. 音频的，声音的（技术用语） | This is an **authentic** piece of ancient pottery. / The test measures **aural** comprehension. / The **audio** quality of the recording is excellent. |
| hum vs huge | hum = v./n. 嗡嗡声，哼唱（与声音有关）；huge = adj. 巨大的，庞大的（形容大小） | She began to **hum** a familiar tune. / The company made a **huge** profit last year. |
| expedition vs expenditure | expedition = n. 远征，探险队；expenditure = n. 支出，花费（财务用语） | They organised an **expedition** to the Arctic. / Military **expenditure** has increased sharply. |
| freeze vs frequent | freeze = v. 结冰，冻结；frequent = adj. 频繁的 / v. 常去（注意词义完全不同，但拼写容易混淆） | Water **freeze**s at zero degrees Celsius. / He is a **frequent** visitor to the museum. |
| drone vs drown | drone = n. 无人机 / v. 嗡嗡作响；drown = v. 淹死，淹没 | The **drone** captured aerial footage. / His voice was **drown**ed out by the noise. |
