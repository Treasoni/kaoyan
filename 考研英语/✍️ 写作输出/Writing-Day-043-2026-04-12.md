## 练习二：汉译英（8题）

将以下中文句子翻译为英文，使用今日所学词汇：

**1.** 科学家们提出了一个大胆的假设：人工智能有可能超越人类在推理方面的能力，这将对教育领域产生非凡的影响。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Scientists have formulated a bold ==hypothesis==: artificial intelligence may ==overtake== human reasoning capabilities, which would have an ==extraordinary== impact on the field of education.

**2.** 社交媒体不仅传播信息，还加剧了公众焦虑，人们比以往任何时候都更容易受到虚假宣传的影响。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Social media does not merely ==propagate== information; it also ==fuel==s public anxiety, making people more ==prone== to false ==propaganda== than ever before.

**3.** 研究表明，学生的创造力与标准化考试的频率成反比，这引发了关于教育自主性的广泛讨论。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Research indicates that students' creativity varies in ==inverse== proportion to the frequency of standardised testing, which has ==surface==d as a topic of widespread debate concerning educational ==autonomy==.

**4.** 政府试图通过外交手段避免一场潜在的贸易战争，同时从外部渠道提取关键的市场数据以评估风险。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The government attempted to ==avert== a potential trade war through diplomatic channels while ==extract==ing critical market data from ==external== sources to assess risks.

**5.** 这位科学家的发明推动了新能源技术的边界，她的研究在尖端领域取得了非凡的突破。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The scientist's ==invention== pushed the boundaries of new energy technology, and her research achieved ==extraordinary== breakthroughs at the scientific ==frontier==.

**6.** 过多的库存积压会给企业带来沉重的负担，而有效的库存管理则能够显著降低运营成本。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Excessive ==inventory== places a heavy burden on enterprises, whereas effective ==inventory== control can significantly reduce operational ==overhead==s.

**7.** 一夜之间推翻一个长期存在的制度几乎是不可能的，但持续的努力可以逐步实现变革。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> It is nearly impossible to ==overthrow== a long-established system ==overnight==, but sustained efforts can gradually bring about transformation.

**8.** 随着工业自动化的迅速发展，传统制造业正面临着前所未有的冲击，许多工人发现自己处于被取代的恐惧之中。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> With the rapid advancement of industrial ==automation==, traditional manufacturing is facing an unprecedented upheaval, and many workers find themselves ==prone== to the fear of being displaced.

## 练习三：词汇辨析

| 易混词组 | 含义区别 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| **awful** vs. **awesome** vs. **awkward** | awful = adj. 可怕的，糟糕的（消极评价）；awesome = adj. 令人惊叹的，极好的（积极评价，非正式）；awkward = adj. 尴尬的，笨拙的（社交/动作语境） | The weather was **awful** last weekend. / She gave an **awesome** presentation at the conference. / There was an **awkward** silence after his comment. |
| **external** vs. **exterior** vs. **extract** | external = adj. 外部的，外界的（强调来源或影响来自外部）；exterior = n./adj. 外部，外观（强调物理外表/外观）；extract = v./n. 提取，摘录（动作或产物） | ==External== factors such as policy changes can affect the market. / The ==exterior== of the building was painted white. / Scientists ==extract== valuable minerals from seawater. |
| **invent** vs. **invert** vs. **investigate** | invent = vt. 发明，创造（新事物）；invert = vt. 颠倒，倒置（顺序或位置）；investigate = v. 调查，研究（深入探究） | Edison ==invent==ed the light bulb. / You must ==invert== the equation to solve for x. / The committee will ==investigate== the cause of the accident. |
| **humor** vs. **humour** | humor = n. 幽默（美式拼写）；humour = n. 幽默（英式拼写）。含义完全相同，仅拼写差异。 | American: He has a great sense of **humor**. / British: She has a wonderful sense of **humour**. |
| **ax** vs. **axe** | ax = n. 斧子（美式拼写）；axe = n. 斧子（英式拼写）。含义相同，均为"斧"或"削减"之意，仅拼写差异。 | American: He chopped the wood with an **ax**. / British: The company decided to **axe** 200 jobs. |

## 练习四：写作段落

使用指定词汇写一段约80-100词的英文段落：

**题目**：讨论人工智能是否会超越人类智能，以及这一趋势可能带来的影响。

**要求使用词汇**：overtake, extraordinary, hypothesis, autonomy, fuel, avert, frontier, prone

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> The question of whether artificial intelligence will ==overtake== human intelligence has moved from science fiction to a legitimate scientific ==hypothesis==. Researchers at the ==frontier== of machine learning have achieved ==extraordinary== results in language processing and image recognition. However, these advances ==fuel== both excitement and anxiety. Critics warn that without sufficient ==autonomy== in decision-making safeguards, society may be ==prone== to unintended consequences. To ==avert== potential risks, governments and tech companies must collaborate on ethical guidelines that ensure AI serves — rather than supplants — human interests. (90 words)
