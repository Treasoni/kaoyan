# ✍️ 写作输出练习 - Day 012 (2026-03-11)

## 📝 Part A: 写作词汇替换升级

> **将普通表达升级为高级表达，提升作文档次**

### A1. 表达"重要的/有意义的"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| important | **significant / of great significance / crucial** |
| have meaning | **signify / carry significance** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ This issue is important for our future.
- ✅ This issue is **significant** for our future.
- ✅ This issue **carries great significance** for our future.

### A2. 表达"显示/表明"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| show | **signify / indicate / signal** |
| tell us | **reveal / demonstrate** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ The data shows that...
- ✅ The data **signifies** that...
- ✅ The data **signals** a trend toward...

### A3. 表达"导致/引起"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| cause | **incur / bring about / give rise to** |
| lead to | **result in / contribute to** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ This decision caused many problems.
- ✅ This decision **incurred** numerous problems.
- ✅ This decision **gave rise to** significant challenges.

### A4. 表达"增长/繁荣"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| grow well | **flourish / thrive / prosper** |
| increase a lot | **increase significantly / surge** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ The economy is growing well.
- ✅ The economy is **flourishing**.
- ✅ The economy is **thriving**.

### A5. 表达"获得/得到"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| get from | **derive from / obtain from** |
| gain | **acquire / secure** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ We get benefits from this policy.
- ✅ We **derive** benefits **from** this policy.
- ✅ We **acquire** substantial benefits from this policy.

### A6. 表达"阻碍/妨碍"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| stop | **hinder / impede / obstruct** |
| get in the way | **hamper / interfere with** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ Bad weather stopped our progress.
- ✅ Bad weather **hindered** our progress.
- ✅ Bad weather **impeded** our advancement.

### A7. 表达"结果/后果"
| 原级表达 | 升级表达 |
|----------|----------|
| result | **outcome / consequence** |
| ending | **conclusion / culmination** |

**例句对比**:
- ❌ The result was unexpected.
- ✅ The **outcome** was unexpected.
- ✅ The **consequence** took us by surprise.

## 🌏 Part B: 汉译英练习（5句）

> **使用今日词汇完成翻译，注意搭配和语域**

### B1. 政府正在执行新政策以减少失业率。
**目标词汇**: implement, policy, reduce
**参考答案**: The government is **implementing** new **policies** to **reduce** unemployment.
**升级版本**: The government is **implementing** new **policies** designed to **curb** unemployment rates.

### B2. 这项研究表明气候变化对全球经济有重大影响。
**目标词汇**: significant, impact, economy
**参考答案**: This study **signifies** that climate change has a **significant** **impact** on the global **economy**.
**搭配亮点**: **carries significant implications for**...

### B3. 过度依赖科技可能会损害我们的社交能力。
**目标词汇**: impair, social, ability, depend on
**参考答案**: Excessive **dependence on** technology may **impair** our **social** **abilities**.
**升级表达**: **Over-reliance on** technology can **impair** our interpersonal skills.

### B4. 经济发展不应以牺牲环境为代价。
**目标词汇**: growth, cost, environment, deserve
**参考答案**: Economic **growth** should not come at the **cost** of the **environment**, which **deserves** protection.
**高级表达**: Economic **growth** should not **incur** environmental **degradation**.

### B5. 这个组织的繁荣源于其创新文化。
**目标词汇**: flourish, derive from, innovate
**参考答案**: This organization **flourishes** as it **derives from** a culture of **innovation**.
**地道表达**: The organization's **flourishing** can be **attributed to** its innovative culture.

## 🔍 Part C: 词义辨析（5组）

### C1. sign vs signal vs signify

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 | 用法区别 |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **sign** | n./v. | 迹象、标志；签名 | 最通用，可指任何迹象 |
| **signal** | n./v./adj. | 信号；标志；重大的 | 强调明确的信号或标志 |
| **signify** | vt./vi. | 表示；预示；要紧 | 更正式，强调"意义" |

**例句对比**:
- There are **signs** of improvement. (有改善的迹象)
- The data **signals** a positive trend. (数据标志着积极趋势)
- What does this **signify**? (这意味着什么？)

**写作建议**:
- 图表作文用 **signal** (signal that...)
- 议论文用 **signify** (signify importance)
- 通用情况用 **sign**

---

### C2. describe vs derive vs deprive

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 | 常用搭配 |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **describe** | vt. | 描述、描写 | describe sth to sb |
| **derive** | vi./vt. | 起源；得到 | derive from |
| **deprive** | vt. | 剥夺、使丧失 | deprive sb of sth |

**例句对比**:
- Please **describe** the problem to me. (请描述问题)
- Many words **derive from** Latin. (很多词源自拉丁语)
- The law **deprives** citizens **of** their rights. (法律剥夺了公民权利)

**写作建议**:
- **derive** from = come from (起源、获得)
- **deprive** of = take away (剥夺)
- 注意不要混淆！

---

### C3. simple vs simplify vs simplicity

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 | 用法场景 |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **simple** | adj. | 简单的、简朴的 | 修饰名词 |
| **simplify** | vt. | 使简化 | 动词，表示动作 |
| **simplicity** | n. | 简单性、质朴 | 抽象名词 |

**例句对比**:
- The solution is **simple**. (解决方案很简单)
- We need to **simplify** the process. (我们需要简化流程)
- I admire the **simplicity** of her lifestyle. (我钦佩她生活的质朴)

**写作建议**:
- 论点表达：**simplicity** is often underrated
- 过程描述：**simplify** complex issues
- 评价用语：a **simple** yet effective approach

---

### C4. worth vs deserve vs desirable

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 | 用法区别 |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **worth** | adj./n. | 值得 | be worth doing |
| **deserve** | v. | 值得、应得 | deserve punishment/praise |
| **desirable** | adj. | 令人向往的 | a desirable outcome |

**例句对比**:
- This book is **worth** reading. (这本书值得读)
- He **deserves** the award. (他应得这个奖)
- A **desirable** outcome for all parties. (各方都期望的结果)

**写作建议**:
- **worth** + -ing (值得做某事)
- **deserve** + noun (应得某物)
- **desirable** + noun (令人向往的)

---

### C5. implement vs impair vs impart

| 单词 | 词性 | 核心含义 | 记忆技巧 |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| **implement** | vt./n. | 贯彻、执行；工具 | im-(进入) + ple-(填满) → 填满→实施 |
| **impair** | v. | 损害、削弱 | im-(加强) + pair(坏) → 使坏→损害 |
| **impart** | vt. | 告知、传授 | im-(给予) + part(部分) → 给予部分→告知 |

**例句对比**:
- **Implement** policies to reduce pollution. (执行政策减少污染)
- Smoking can **impair** lung function. (吸烟会损害肺功能)
- Teachers **impart** knowledge to students. (教师向学生传授知识)

**记忆口诀**:
- implement = 实施 (im-进入 + ple-充满)
- impair = 损害 (im-坏 + pair-对)
- impart = 传授 (im-给 + part-部分)

## 🎯 写作建议

### 1. 词汇升级策略
- 基础词 → 高级词（important → significant）
- 动词搭配要精准（describe vs derive vs deprive）
- 形容词要具体（simple vs desirable）

### 2. 搭配积累
- **implement** policies (执行政策)
- **derive** from (源自)
- **incur** costs (招致代价)
- **hinder** progress (阻碍进步)
- **flourish** in (在...中繁荣)

### 3. 常见错误避免
- ❌ deprive from → ✅ deprive of
- ❌ deserve doing → ✅ deserve to be done / deserve + noun
- ❌ simplify your work to be easier → ✅ simplify your work
