## 📋 词义辨析

### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. exceed / surpass**
The company's revenue ______ $10 million last year.
> The company's revenue **exceeded** $10 million last year.
（exceed = 超过数量/限度；surpass = 超越/胜过，强调优胜）

**17. cope with / deal with**
She struggled to ______ the heavy workload.
> She struggled to **cope with** the heavy workload.
（cope with = 应对困难，强调能力；deal with = 处理，更通用）

**18. interim / temporary**
The ______ government will rule until elections are held.
> The **interim** government will rule until elections are held.
（interim = 过渡期的，强调阶段性；temporary = 暂时的，强调时间短）

**19. coordinate / cooperate**
The two companies decided to ______ their marketing efforts.
> The two companies decided to **coordinate** their marketing efforts.
（coordinate = 协调整合；cooperate = 合作共事）

**20. exact / accurate**
We need the ______ figures for the report.
> We need the **exact** figures for the report.
（exact = 确切的，精确无误；accurate = 准确的，误差小）
