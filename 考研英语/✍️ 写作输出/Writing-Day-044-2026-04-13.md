## 练习一：词汇替换（10题）

将以下简单表达替换为更高级的词汇：

| 题号 | 基础表达 | 高级替换（你的答案） | 参考答案 |
|------|----------|---------------------|----------|
| 1 | give / provide | ___ | provision |
| 2 | make angry / annoy | ___ | provoke |
| 3 | keep safe | ___ | protect |
| 4 | successful / wealthy | ___ | prosperous |
| 5 | surround / enclose | ___ | surround (强调用法) |
| 6 | connect / blend | ___ | fuse |
| 7 | think about too much | ___ | dwell (on) |
| 8 | copy / reproduce | ___ | duplicate |
| 9 | raw / unrefined | ___ | crude |
| 10 | silent / unable to speak | ___ | mute |

> [!note]- 🔑 参考答案
> | 题号 | 基础表达 | 参考答案 |
> |------|----------|----------|
> | 1 | give / provide | **provision** — The government made ==provision== for emergency housing. |
> | 2 | make angry / annoy | **provoke** — His remarks ==provoke==d strong criticism from the public. |
> | 3 | keep safe | **protect** — We must ==protect== endangered species from extinction. |
> | 4 | successful / wealthy | **prosperous** — The country experienced a ==prosperous== era of economic growth. |
> | 5 | surround / enclose | **surround** (强调用法) — The village is ==surround==ed by lush green hills. |
> | 6 | connect / blend | **fuse** — The artist ==fuse==d traditional motifs with modern techniques. |
> | 7 | think about too much | **dwell (on)** — There is no point in ==dwelling== on past mistakes. |
> | 8 | copy / reproduce | **duplicate** — The experiment failed because they could not ==duplicate== the results. |
> | 9 | raw / unrefined | **crude** — The nation exports large quantities of ==crude== oil. |
> | 10 | silent / unable to speak | **mute** — She stood ==mute== with shock at the devastating news. |

## 练习二：汉译英（8题）

将以下中文句子翻译为英文，使用今日所学词汇：

**1.** 尽管政府采取了措施来保护臭氧层，但原油泄漏事件仍然对周围的海洋生态系统造成了毁灭性的破坏。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Although the government has taken measures to ==protect== the ozone layer, the ==crude== oil spill still caused devastating damage to the ==surround==ing marine ecosystem.

**2.** 这位考古学家的发现揭示了一个繁荣的朝代，其王室成员的陵墓中藏有精美的象牙雕刻和水晶器皿。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The archaeologist's discovery revealed a ==prosperous== dynasty, whose ==royal== members' tombs contained exquisite ==ivory== carvings and ==crystal== vessels.

**3.** 研究表明，不论年龄和背景，长期处于孤立状态的人更容易受到心理健康问题的困扰。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Research indicates that, regardless of age and background, individuals who remain ==isolate==d for extended periods are more likely to be troubled by mental health issues, and should not ==dwell== on negative thoughts.

**4.** 这座城市的经济增长进一步促进了文化产业的发展，音乐家和演员们纷纷将自己的作品制作成专辑出版。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The city's economic growth ==further==ed the development of the cultural industry, and ==musician==s and actors rushed to ==publish== their works as ==album==s.

**5.** 在自然灾害的后果中，救援人员不顾个人安危，勇敢地进入废墟中搜救幸存者。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> In the aftermath of the natural disaster, rescue workers disregarded their own safety and bravely entered the ruins to search for survivors, while the ==mute== victims waited silently for help.

**6.** 面对不断加剧的环境污染，科学家们敦促政府加强法规，防止企业将有害废物倾倒在农田附近。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Faced with worsening environmental pollution, scientists ==urge==d the government to ==further== strengthen regulations to prevent companies from ==dump==ing hazardous waste near farmland.

**7.** 这位女演员以她在历史剧中的出色表演而自豪，她在剧中扮演了一位为了民族繁荣而抗争的王室公主。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The actress was proud of her outstanding performance in the historical drama, where she played a ==royal== princess who fought for the nation's ==prosperity==.

**8.** 这项新法规的条款规定，任何企图绕过监管程序的企业都将面临起诉。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The ==provision==s of the new regulation stipulate that any enterprise attempting to bypass regulatory procedures will face ==prosecution==.

## 练习三：词汇辨析

| 易混词组 | 含义区别 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| **prosperous** vs. **thriving** vs. **flourishing** | prosperous = adj. 繁荣的（侧重经济富裕和成功）；thriving = adj. 蒸蒸日上的（侧重持续增长）；flourishing = adj. 茂盛的，兴旺的（侧重文化/艺术的繁荣） | The ==prosperous== nation invested heavily in infrastructure. / The startup became a ==thriving== business within two years. / The city has a ==flourishing== arts scene that attracts tourists worldwide. |
| **mute** vs. **dumb** vs. **silent** | mute = adj. 沉默的，无声的（可指选择不说话）；dumb = adj. 傻的，哑的（口语中常用"蠢"）；silent = adj. 安静的，沉默的（一般性描述） | She remained ==mute== throughout the interrogation, refusing to say a word. / That was a ==dumb== thing to say in front of the boss. / The room fell ==silent== when the professor entered. |
| **crude** vs. **rude** vs. **raw** | crude = adj. 粗糙的，天然的（侧重未加工）；rude = adj. 粗鲁的，无礼的（侧重行为）；raw = adj. 生的，原始的（侧重未处理） | The country relies heavily on ==crude== oil exports. / His ==rude== behaviour offended everyone at the dinner table. / ==Raw== vegetables are often more nutritious than cooked ones. |
| **further** vs. **farther** | further = adv. 更进一步（抽象程度，也可作vt.促进）；farther = adv. 更远（物理距离） | We need to ==further== investigate this issue. / The village is ==farther== down the road than I expected. |
| **crush** vs. **crash** vs. **smash** | crush = vt. 压碎，镇压（施加压力破坏）；crash = v. 碰撞，坠毁（突然撞击）；smash = vt. 粉碎，打碎（彻底破碎） | The rebellion was ==crush==ed by government forces. / The car ==crash==ed into a tree on the highway. / He ==smash==ed the window with a baseball bat. |
| **isolate** vs. **insulate** vs. **separate** | isolate = vt. 隔离，孤立（使脱离联系）；insulate = vt. 隔热/隔音，使绝缘（物理隔离）；separate = vt. 分开，分离（一般性分离） | Patients with infectious diseases must be ==isolate==d. / The walls are ==insulate==d to reduce noise. / Please ==separate== the recyclables from general waste. |
