## 📝 Part 1: 写作词汇替换升级（5题）

请用更高级的词汇替换句子中的划线部分：

### 题目

1. The government should **think** about the environmental impact of this policy.
   - 高级替换：__________

2. We need to **solve** the problem of income inequality.
   - 高级替换：__________

3. This factor **gets** more attention from researchers.
   - 高级替换：__________

4. The company took **steps** to improve workplace safety.
   - 高级替换：__________

5. Education plays an **important** role in social mobility.
   - 高级替换：__________

### 参考答案

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

1. **contend** - Many scholars **contend** that the government should consider...
   - 或 **argue** - The government should **argue for** the environmental impact...

2. **address** - We need to **address** the problem of income inequality.
   - 或 **tackle** - We need to **tackle** the issue of income inequality.

3. **secure** - This factor **secures** more attention from researchers.
   - 或 **garner** - This factor **garners** more attention from researchers.

4. **measures** - The company took **measures** to improve workplace safety.
   - 或 **initiatives** - The company took **initiatives** to improve workplace safety.

5. **decisive** - Education plays a **decisive** role in social mobility.
   - 或 **crucial** - Education plays a **crucial** role in social mobility.

</details>

## 🌐 Part 2: 汉译英练习（5题）

请将以下中文句子翻译成英文，使用 Day 002 学过的词汇：

### 题目

1. 许多学者**主张**，人工智能将对就业市场产生**决定性的**影响。

2. 政府需要采取紧急**措施**来**解决**气候变化问题。

3. 贫富差距是影响社会稳定的一个关键**因素**。

4. 有些批评者**反对**这种观点，认为它会**威胁**到个人隐私。

5. 公司应该**欣然接受**新技术，以提高**效率**。

### 参考答案

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

1. Many scholars **contend** that artificial intelligence will have a **decisive** impact on the job market.
   - 替换词：contend (主张), decisive (决定性的)

2. The government needs to take urgent **measures** to **address** climate change.
   - 替换词：measures (措施), address (解决)

3. The wealth gap is a critical **factor** affecting social stability.
   - 替换词：factor (因素)

4. Some critics **oppose** this view, arguing that it will **threaten** personal privacy.
   - 替换词：oppose (反对), threaten (威胁)

5. Companies should **embrace** new technologies to improve **efficiency**.
   - 替换词：embrace (欣然接受), efficiency (效率)

</details>

## 🔍 Part 3: 词义辨析（5题）

### 题目

**1. efficient vs effective**

选择正确的词填空：
- The new system proved to be highly \_\_\_\_\_\_ in reducing costs.
- The marketing campaign was \_\_\_\_\_\_ in increasing sales.

A. efficient / effective
B. effective / efficient
C. efficient / efficient
D. effective / effective

---

**2. economic vs economical**

选择正确的词填空：
- The \_\_\_\_\_\_ growth of the country has slowed down.
- This car is very \_\_\_\_\_\_ on fuel.

A. economic / economical
B. economical / economic
C. economic / economic
D. economical / economical

---

**3. fabricate vs manufacture**

选择正确的词填空：
- The company \_\_\_\_\_\_ electronic components.
- The suspect was accused of \_\_\_\_\_\_ evidence.

A. fabricates / manufacturing
B. manufactures / fabricating
C. fabricates / fabricating
D. manufactures / manufacturing

---

**4. threat vs threaten**

选择正确的词填空：
- Climate change poses a serious \_\_\_\_\_\_ to biodiversity.
- Rising sea levels \_\_\_\_\_\_ coastal cities.

A. threat / threaten
B. threaten / threat
C. threat / threat
D. threaten / threaten

---

**5. embrace vs hug**

选择正确的词填空：
- The CEO \_\_\_\_\_\_ the new digital transformation strategy.
- She \_\_\_\_\_\_ her mother warmly at the airport.

A. embraced / hugged
B. hugged / embraced
C. embraced / embraced
D. hugged / hugged

---

### 参考答案

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

1. **A. efficient / effective**
   - efficient: 强调"效率高，不浪费" → 减少成本
   - effective: 强调"有效果" → 增加销量

2. **A. economic / economical**
   - economic: 经济的（与经济相关的）
   - economical: 节俭的、省钱的

3. **B. manufactures / fabricating**
   - manufacture: 制造（实物产品）
   - fabricate: 捏造、编造（虚假事物）

4. **A. threat / threaten**
   - threat: 名词，威胁
   - threaten: 动词，威胁

5. **A. embraced / hugged**
   - embrace: 欣然接受（抽象意义）/ 拥抱
   - hug: 拥抱（具体动作）
   - 第一句是比喻义，第二句是具体动作

</details>

## 💡 重点词汇总结

### 写作替换黄金词汇

| 初级词 | 高级替换 | 用法示例 |
|--------|----------|----------|
| think | **contend** | Scholars contend that... |
| solve | **address** | address the issue |
| get | **secure** | secure support |
| steps | **measures** | take measures |
| important | **decisive** | decisive role |
| way | **means** | by means of |

- **efficient** (高效) vs **effective** (有效)
- **economic** (经济) vs **economical** (节俭)
- **manufacture** (制造) vs **fabricate** (捏造)
