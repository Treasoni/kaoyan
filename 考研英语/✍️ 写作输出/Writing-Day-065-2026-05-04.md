## Section A: 词汇替换练习

**说明**: 用今天学习的高级词汇替换句子中的简单表达。

### 1. think again -> reconsider

**原句**: The government was forced to **think again about** its decision after widespread criticism.

**替换**: The government was forced to **reconsider** its decision after widespread criticism.

**译文**: 在遭到广泛批评后，政府被迫重新考虑其决定。

---

### 2. get back -> regain

**原句**: It took her months to **get back** her strength after the illness.

**替换**: It took her months to **regain** her strength after the illness.

**译文**: 病后她花了几个月时间才恢复体力。

---

### 3. define again -> redefine

**原句**: The digital age has caused us to **define again** what it means to be connected.

**替换**: The digital age has caused us to **redefine** what it means to be connected.

**译文**: 数字时代使我们重新定义了"连接"的含义。

---

### 4. build again -> reconstruct

**原句**: After the earthquake, the community worked together to **build again** their homes.

**替换**: After the earthquake, the community worked together to **reconstruct** their homes.

**译文**: 地震后，社区共同努力重建家园。

---

### 5. skilled -> proficient

**原句**: She is highly **skilled** in three languages.

**替换**: She is highly **proficient** in three languages.

**译文**: 她精通三种语言。

---

### 6. say again -> reaffirm

**原句**: The CEO **said again** the company's commitment to sustainability.

**替换**: The CEO **reaffirmed** the company's commitment to sustainability.

**译文**: 首席执行官重申了公司对可持续发展的承诺。

---

### 7. look at again -> re-evaluate

**原句**: Economists are **looking at again** their growth forecasts due to the crisis.

**替换**: Economists are **re-evaluating** their growth forecasts due to the crisis.

**译文**: 由于危机，经济学家正在重新评估他们的增长预测。

---

### 8. unavoidable trend -> remorseless

**原句**: The **unavoidable trend** of globalization has transformed local economies.

**替换**: The **remorseless** march of globalization has transformed local economies.

**译文**: 全球化的无情进程已经改变了地方经济。

---

### 9. total focus on -> preoccupation with

**原句**: The public's **total focus on** celebrity culture has been criticized by intellectuals.

**替换**: The public's **preoccupation with** celebrity culture has been criticized by intellectuals.

**译文**: 公众对名人文化的过度关注受到了知识分子的批评。

---

### 10. necessary condition -> prerequisite

**原句**: A good education is a **necessary condition** for social mobility.

**替换**: A good education is a **prerequisite** for social mobility.

**译文**: 良好的教育是社会流动的先决条件。

---

### 11. go back on a promise -> renege on

**原句**: The government should not **go back on its promise** to increase education funding.

**替换**: The government should not **renege on its promise** to increase education funding.

**译文**: 政府不应该违背增加教育经费的承诺。

---

### 12. widely spread -> pervasive

**原句**: The **widely spread** use of smartphones has changed how we communicate.

**替换**: The **pervasive** use of smartphones has changed how we communicate.

**译文**: 智能手机的广泛使用改变了我们的交流方式。

---

### 13. gather courage -> pluck up courage

**原句**: She finally **gathered the courage** to speak out against injustice.

**替换**: She finally **plucked up the courage** to speak out against injustice.

**译文**: 她终于鼓起勇气公开反对不公正现象。

---

### 14. pointed out accurately -> pinpoint

**原句**: The analyst **pointed out accurately** the flaws in the proposal.

**替换**: The analyst **pinpointed** the flaws in the proposal.

**译文**: 分析人士准确指出了提案中的缺陷。

## Section B: 汉译英练习

**说明**: 将下列中文句子翻译成英文，要求使用括号中给出的目标单词。

### 1. 我们需要重新定义传统教育中"成功"的含义。（redefine）

**参考答案**: We need to **redefine** the meaning of "success" in traditional education.

### 2. 这道长城是世界闻名的文化遗产。（renowned, cultural relic）

**参考答案**: The Great Wall is a **world-renowned** **cultural relic**.

### 3. 公司承诺即使在困难时期也不会裁员。（redundancy）

**参考答案**: The company promised that there would be no **redundancy** even during difficult times.

### 4. 遗憾的是，很多学生高估了短时间备考的效果。（regrettably, overestimate）

**参考答案**: **Regrettably**, many students **overestimate** the effectiveness of last-minute exam preparation.

### 5. 经济增长的预测必须以现实数据为前提。（projection, presuppose）

**参考答案**: Economic growth **projections** **presuppose** realistic data.

### 6. 社交媒体对青少年的心理健康的普遍影响不容忽视。（pervasive, psychological）

**参考答案**: The **pervasive** influence of social media on teenagers' **psychological** well-being cannot be ignored.

### 7. 一个成功的谈判者必须具备熟练的沟通技巧和有说服力的论证能力。（proficient, persuasive）

**参考答案**: A successful negotiator must be **proficient** in communication skills and capable of **persuasive** argumentation.

### 8. 人口众多国家的生育率下降，使得政府重新评估其社会保障体系。（populous, procreation, re-evaluate）

**参考答案**: The decline in **procreation** rates in **populous** countries has forced governments to **re-evaluate** their social security systems.

### 9. 老师在全神贯注地批改试卷，没注意到有人进了教室。（preoccupation）

**参考答案**: The teacher was so deep in **preoccupation** with grading papers that she did not notice someone entering the classroom.

### 10. 战争结束后，社区开始重建工作，并重申了对和平的承诺。（rebuild, reaffirm）

**参考答案**: After the war ended, the community began to **rebuild** and **reaffirmed** its commitment to peace.

## Section C: 写作模板积累

**说明**: 以下句型适用于考研英语写作，使用今日高频词汇，可直接套用。

### 模板 1: "It is relatively easy to observe / argue that..."

> **It is relatively easy to observe** that the digital age has **redefined** the way we communicate, **regardless of** geographical boundaries.
>
> **It is relatively easy to argue** that **psychological** wellbeing is **primarily** determined by social connections **rather than** material wealth.

**适用场景**: 考研作文开头段（引出话题）
**译文**: 相对容易观察到数字时代重新定义了我们沟通的方式，不论地理界限。/ 相对容易论证心理健康主要由社会关系而非物质财富决定。

---

### 模板 2: "X requires more than just Y; it demands Z..."

> A successful career **requires more than just** technical **proficiency**; it demands **persuasive** communication skills and the ability to **reconstruct** problems from new perspectives.

**适用场景**: 论述某事物需要多方面因素（教育、职场、社会发展等话题）
**译文**: 成功的职业需要的不仅仅是技术熟练度；它还需要有说服力的沟通技巧以及从新角度重建问题的能力。

---

### 模板 3: "The pervasive influence of X has prompted Y to reconsider / re-evaluate..."

> **The pervasive influence** of social media on young people's **psychological** development **has prompted** educators and parents **to reconsider** the role of digital devices in daily life.

**适用场景**: 社会现象分析（科技影响、环境问题、生活方式变化等）
**译文**: 社交媒体对年轻人心理发展的普遍影响促使教育者和家长重新考虑数字设备在日常生活中的角色。

---

### 模板 4: "X is a prerequisite for Y, yet Z presupposes conditions that..."

> A basic education **is a prerequisite for** social mobility, **yet** current policies often **presuppose** conditions of equal opportunity **that** do not exist in reality.

**适用场景**: 指出理想与现实差距（教育、政策、社会发展等话题）
**译文**: 基础教育是社会流动的先决条件，然而当前政策往往以现实中不存在的平等机会为前提。

---

### 模板 5: "Regrettably, X has become a relic of the past, and Y has redefined..."

> **Regrettably**, traditional craftsmanship **has become a relic of the past**, and mass production **has redefined** our understanding of quality and value.

**适用场景**: 文化传承/变迁类作文（传统文化、价值观变化等）
**译文**: 遗憾的是，传统工艺已成为过去的遗迹，大规模生产已经重新定义了我们对质量和价值的理解。

> **学习总结**: 本次 Day 65 的训练重点是 re-（再次）、pro-（向前）、pre-（之前）三大前缀词的写作应用。re- 前缀的核心含义是"再次/返回"，是今日最大词族（22词），考研写作中出现频率最高的是 reconsider（重新考虑）和 redefine（重新定义）。pro- 前缀强调"向前"（promotion, proficient, progression），pre- 前缀强调"预先"（prerequisite, predictably, presuppose）。掌握这三大前缀系统后，可以快速扩展词汇量并准确理解陌生词汇。熟词僻义方面需要注意 poach（煮→挖人）、plum（李子→称心的）、pluck（摘→鼓起勇气）等词汇的抽象用法。
