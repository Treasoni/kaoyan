## Section A: 词汇替换练习

**说明**: 用今天学习的 over- 前缀及多词根高级词汇替换句子中的简单表达。

### 1. greatly exceed -> outweigh

**原句**: The benefits of a college education **greatly exceed** the financial costs.

**替换**: The benefits of a college education **far outweigh** the financial costs.

**译文**: 大学教育的收益远大于经济成本。

---

### 2. too strongly described -> overstate

**原句**: It is impossible to **too strongly describe** the importance of critical thinking in the modern workplace.

**替换**: It is impossible to **overstate** the importance of critical thinking in the modern workplace.

**译文**: 批判性思维在现代职场中的重要性怎么强调也不为过。

---

### 3. make shadow over -> overshadow

**原句**: The team's internal conflicts **made a shadow over** their previous accomplishments.

**替换**: The team's internal conflicts **overshadowed** their previous accomplishments.

**译文**: 团队内部的冲突使其先前的成就黯然失色。

---

### 4. think too highly -> overestimate

**原句**: Many students **think too highly of** their ability to finish tasks at the last minute.

**替换**: Many students **overestimate** their ability to finish tasks at the last minute.

**译文**: 许多学生高估了自己在最后一刻完成任务的能力。

---

### 5. too strong public complaint -> outcry

**原句**: There was a **too strong public complaint** against the new tax policy.

**替换**: There was a public **outcry** against the new tax policy.

**译文**: 公众对新税收政策强烈抗议。

---

### 6. always continue -> perpetuate

**原句**: Such stereotypes **always continue** harmful gender biases in the workplace.

**替换**: Such stereotypes **perpetuate** harmful gender biases in the workplace.

**译文**: 这种刻板印象使工作场所中有害的性别偏见持续存在。

---

### 7. go beyond the limit -> overstep

**原句**: The journalist **went beyond the limit** of professional ethics by publishing private information.

**替换**: The journalist **overstepped** the boundaries of professional ethics by publishing private information.

**译文**: 该记者通过发布私人信息逾越了职业道德的界限。

---

### 8. look down on -> patronise

**原句**: Some so-called experts **look down on** beginners instead of offering genuine help.

**替换**: Some so-called experts **patronise** beginners instead of offering genuine help.

**译文**: 一些所谓的专家非但没有提供真正的帮助，反而以高人一等的态度对待初学者。

---

### 9. harmful (in a hidden way) -> pernicious

**原句**: The **harmful** influence of fake news on public opinion should never be underestimated.

**替换**: The **pernicious** influence of fake news on public opinion should never be underestimated.

**译文**: 虚假新闻对公众舆论的有害影响绝不应被低估。

---

### 10. insist on doing -> obsess over

**原句**: Some people **insist on doing** every minor detail perfectly, which leads to inefficiency.

**替换**: Some people **obsess over** every minor detail, which leads to inefficiency.

**译文**: 有些人痴迷于每一个微小的细节，这导致了效率低下。

## Section B: 汉译英练习

**说明**: 将下列中文句子翻译成英文，要求使用括号中给出的目标单词。

### 1. 互联网的好处远大于其潜在的弊端，前提是我们合理使用它。（outweigh）

**参考答案**: The benefits of the Internet **far outweigh** its potential drawbacks, provided that we use it wisely.

### 2. 这项改革早就该进行了，公众对此反响热烈。（overdue, outcry）

**参考答案**: This reform is long **overdue**, and there has been a strong public **outcry** demanding it.

### 3. 人们不应高估短期努力的效果，而低估坚持的力量。（overestimate, persistence）

**参考答案**: People should not **overestimate** the effect of short-term efforts and underestimate the power of **persistence**.

### 4. 令人自相矛盾的是，过度的保护反而限制了孩子的成长空间。（paradoxically, overly）

**参考答案**: **Paradoxically**, **overly** protective parenting actually limits a child's room for growth.

### 5. 家长的教养方式对孩子的性格形成有着不可忽视的影响。（parenting, negligible / notable）

**参考答案**: **Parenting** style has a **noteworthy** impact on the formation of a child's personality.

### 6. 在面试中，非语言交流（如眼神接触和肢体语言）和语言表达同样重要。（nonverbal, necessarily）

**参考答案**: In an interview, **nonverbal** communication such as eye contact and body language is not **necessarily** less important than verbal expression.

### 7. 那些只关注短期利润的公司往往会忽视其行为的长期有害影响。（obsess, pernicious）

**参考答案**: Companies that **obsess over** short-term profits often ignore the **pernicious** long-term effects of their actions.

### 8. 公众的强烈抗议最终迫使立法机构否决了那项有争议的提案。（outcry, overrule）

**参考答案**: The public **outcry** eventually forced the legislature to **overrule** that controversial proposal.

## Section C: 写作模板积累

**说明**: 以下句型适用于考研英语写作，使用今日高频词汇，可直接套用。

### 模板 1: "The importance of X cannot be overstated..."

> **The importance of** environmental education **cannot be overstated**, as it lays the foundation for sustainable development and cultivates environmentally responsible citizens.

**适用场景**: 强调某事物的重要性（考研作文开头常用）
**译文**: 环境教育的重要性怎么强调也不为过，因为它为可持续发展奠定了基础，并培养了具有环境责任感的公民。

---

### 模板 2: "The benefits of X far outweigh the drawbacks..."

> **The benefits of** technological innovation **far outweigh** its **drawbacks**, although critics often **overstate** the negative impact of automation on employment.

**适用场景**: 利弊分析类作文（科技、全球化、社交媒体等话题）
**译文**: 技术创新的好处远大于其弊端，尽管批评者常常夸大自动化对就业的负面影响。

---

### 模板 3: "The pernicious effects of X have been largely overlooked / underestimated..."

> **The pernicious effects of** online misinformation on democratic processes **have been largely underestimated**, as it not only distorts public opinion but also **perpetuates** social division.

**适用场景**: 社会问题分析（信息时代、虚假新闻、偏见等）
**译文**: 在线虚假信息对民主进程的有害影响在很大程度上被低估了，因为它不仅扭曲了公众舆论，还固化了社会分裂。

---

### 模板 4: "There is a growing public outcry over X, which has prompted the authorities to..."

> **There has been a growing public outcry over** environmental pollution, **which has prompted** the government to introduce stricter regulations and **penalize** companies that violate emission standards.

**适用场景**: 社会热点类作文（环保、公共政策、维权等）
**译文**: 公众对环境污染的抗议日益强烈，这促使政府出台更严格的法规并对违反排放标准的公司进行处罚。

---

### 模板 5: "From an observer's perspective, the seemingly paradoxical nature of X lies in the fact that..."

> **From an observer's perspective, the seemingly paradoxical nature of** social media **lies in the fact that** it connects people across the globe while simultaneously fostering **nonverbal** isolation and **perpetuating** superficial relationships.

**适用场景**: 分析复杂现象（社交媒体、现代生活方式等）
**译文**: 从观察者的角度来看，社交媒体的看似矛盾之处在于它在连接全球用户的同时，也造成了非语言上的隔离，并固化了肤浅的人际关系。

> **学习总结**: 本次 Day 64 的训练重点是 over- 过度前缀族和多词根词汇的写作应用。over- 前缀的核心含义是"过度、超过、在上"，附加在动词或形容词前构成新词。考研写作中出现频率最高的是 outweigh（利弊分析）和 cannot be overstated（强调重要性），这两个表达均可作为万能句型使用。此外，需要特别注意 pernicious（潜伏性危害）、paradoxically（句首副词引出转折）、perpetuate（固化负面现象）等词汇的精准使用场景。词族记忆法方面，ob- 词根族（obsess, observer, opposition, objectionable）和 per- 词根族（persistent, perpetuate, pernicious, perceptive）值得重点回顾。通过本日训练，可以在写作中更精确地表达"程度"和"评价"，显著提升作文的语言档次。
