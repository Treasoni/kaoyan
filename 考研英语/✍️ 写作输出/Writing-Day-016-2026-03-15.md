## Part I: 写作词汇替换升级 (3题)

## Part III: 词义辨析与选用 (3题)

> **说明**：从括号中选择最恰当的词填空，注意词义细微差别

### 题目 8

The company's new strategy is expected to be highly ______, with projected revenue growth of 50%.

A. profitable
B. lucrative
C. benefit

> 答案：( )

**解析**：
- **profitable** (adj.)：有利可图的、有益的（强调盈利能力）
- **lucrative** (adj.)：获利多的、赚钱的（强调高额利润）
- **benefit** (n./v.)：利益、受益

**答案**：**A** (profitable 更强调整体盈利能力；lucrative 更强调高额回报，两者皆可)

**翻译**：该公司的新战略预计将非常有利可图， projected收入增长50%。

---

### 题目 9

The government plans to ______ stricter regulations on data privacy.

A. restrict
B. restrain
C. prohibit

> 答案：( )

**解析**：
- **restrict** (vt.)：限制、限定（数量、范围等）
- **restrain** (vt.)：克制、抑制（情感、行为）
- **prohibit** (vt.)：禁止（以法令等形式）

**答案**：**A** (restrict 强调限制范围；restrain 强调克制；prohibit 强调完全禁止)

**翻译**：政府计划对数据隐私实施更严格的限制。

---

### 题目 10

The scientist's ______ discovery changed our understanding of the universe.

A. prominent
B. profound
C. profitable

> 答案：( )

**解析**：
- **prominent** (adj.)：杰出的、突出的（强调显眼、知名）
- **profound** (adj.)：深刻的、深远的（强调影响深度）
- **profitable** (adj.)：有利可图的（强调经济收益）

**答案**：**B** (profound 强调影响深远；prominent 强调显眼；profitable 强调经济利益)

**翻译**：这位科学家深刻的发现改变了我们对宇宙的理解。

## 🎯 写作句型模板

### 开头段句型

1. **Promote...**
   - The government should **promote** the development of...
   - Education **promotes** social mobility and economic growth.

2. **Have a profound impact on...**
   - The Internet has had a **profound impact on** our daily lives.
   - This policy will have a **profound impact on** future generations.

3. **It is integral to...**
   - Education is **integral to** personal development.
   - Trust is **integral to** building strong relationships.

### 论证段句型

1. **Intend to do...**
   - The policy is **intended to** address inequality.
   - These measures **intend to** give individuals more control.

2. **Attempt to do...**
   - We should **attempt to** solve this problem through dialogue.
   - The government **attempted to** reduce pollution.

3. **Attain one's goal...**
   - Only through hard work can we **attain our goals**.
   - Education helps people **attain their potential**.

### 结尾段句型

1. **Restore balance...**
   - We must **restore balance** between work and life.
   - Regulations should **restore balance** between innovation and protection.

2. **Take proper measures...**
   - We should **take proper measures** to address climate change.
   - The government must **take proper action** to protect consumers.

3. **Integral to...**
   - Cooperation is **integral to** solving global problems.
   - Innovation is **integral to** economic development.
