## 📝 写作替换练习（10题）

### 说明
将括号中的简单表达替换为更高级的考研词汇。

---

**1. The government must (solve/deal with) this issue.**

> The government must **address** this issue.

---

**2. This software (works well with/goes with) most systems.**

> This software is **compatible with** most systems.

---

**3. We should (have/possess) problem-solving skills.**

> We should be **equipped with** problem-solving skills.

---

**4. She (really likes/is into) learning new things.**

> She has great **enthusiasm for** learning new things.

---

**5. These two views (fit/match) well together.**

> These two views are **compatible**.

---

**6. We need to (make/create) a coherent plan.**

> We need to develop a **coherent** plan.

---

**7. the team (worked together/joined forces) to complete the project.**

> the team **collaborated** to complete the project.

---

**8. The problem was (made worse/increased) by poor management.**

> The problem was **compounded** by poor management.

---

**9. He (gave/kept) a diplomatic response.**

> He gave a **diplomatic** response.

---

**10. The company is (fully/completely) owned by the government.**

> The company is a **wholly** owned subsidiary of the government.

## 📋 词义辨析（5题）
### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. coherent / cohesive**
The story is not **____**; it jumps from one topic to another without clear connections.

> The story is not **coherent**; it jumps from one topic to another without clear connections.

（coherent = 连贯的，指逻辑； cohesive = 有凝聚力的，指团队）

---

**17. torture / torment**
After days of interrogation, the prisoner was in mental **______**.

> after days of interrogation, the prisoner is in mental **torment**.

（torture = 肉体折磨； torment = 精神折磨）

---

**18. conceal / hide**
The spy tried to **______** his true identity from the enemy.

> The spy tried to **conceal** his true identity from the enemy.

（conceal = 隐瞒，更正式； hide = 藏，更口语）

---

**19. compulsory / voluntary**
In this country, primary education is **______** for all children.

> In this country, primary education is **compulsory** for all children.

（compulsory = 强制的； voluntary = 自愿的）

---

**20. diligent / hardworking**
The **______** student spent hours preparing for the exam.

> The **diligent** student spent hours preparing for the exam.

（diligent = 勤奋的，更正式； hardworking = 努力工作的，更口语）
