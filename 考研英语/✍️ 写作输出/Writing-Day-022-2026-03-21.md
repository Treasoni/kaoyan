## Part 1: 写作词汇替换（3题）

**1. 用更高级的表达替换画线部分：**
> The success of this project **depends on** everyone's cooperation.

💡 **替换词**：hinge on/upon

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The success of this project hinges on everyone's cooperation.**

- **考点**：hinge on = 取决于（比 depend on 更正式）
- **搭配**：hinge on/upon sth
</details>

---

**2. 用更高级的表达替换画线部分：**
> We must **destroy** poverty completely.

💡 **替换词**：eradicate

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**We must eradicate poverty completely.**

- **考点**：eradicate = 根除、消灭（比 destroy 更精准）
- **搭配**：eradicate poverty/disease/crime
</details>

---

**3. 用更高级的表达替换画线部分：**
> **Although** there were many difficulties, they succeeded.

💡 **替换词**：in spite of

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**In spite of many difficulties, they succeeded.**

- **考点**：in spite of = 尽管（比 although 更正式）
- **搭配**：in spite of + 名词/名词短语
</details>

## Part 2: 汉译英（4题）

## Part 3: 词义辨析（3题）

**8. 选择正确的词填空：**
> The two countries have shown great _____ in fighting climate change.
> A. solidarity    B. solitude    C. similarity    D. simplicity

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
**答案：A. solidarity**

- **考点**：solidarity = 团结、齐心协力
- **辨析**：
  - solidarity = 团结（正面）
  - solitude = 孤独（负面）
  - similarity = 相似性
  - simplicity = 简单
</details>

---

**9. 选择正确的词填空：**
> We need to _____ between good and bad advice.
> A. discern    B. concern    C. dismiss    D. discuss

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
**答案：A. discern**

- **考点**：discern = 辨别、觉察
- **辨析**：
  - discern = 辨别（区分好坏）
  - concern = 关心；涉及
  - dismiss = 解雇；驳回
  - discuss = 讨论
</details>

---

**10. 选择正确的词填空：**
> The conflict has _____ into a full-blown crisis.
> A. escalated    B. descended    C. decreased    D. delayed

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
**答案：A. escalated**

- **考点**：escalate into = 升级为、逐步恶化为
- **辨析**：
  - escalate = 升级、恶化（向上）
  - descend = 下降（向下）
  - decrease = 减少
  - delay = 延迟
- **搭配**：escalate into sth
</details>

## 💡 写作技巧总结

### 1. 使用更正式的连接词
- instead of → rather than
- but → however / nevertheless
- so → therefore / thus / consequently

### 2. 使用更精准的动词
- get → obtain / acquire / gain
- make → generate / produce / create
- give → provide / offer / grant

### 3. 使用更丰富的形容词
- good → beneficial / favorable / advantageous
- bad → detrimental / harmful / adverse
- big → significant / substantial / considerable
