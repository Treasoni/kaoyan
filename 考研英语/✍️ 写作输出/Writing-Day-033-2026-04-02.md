## Part1: 词汇升级替换

> [!tip] 将简单词汇替换为高级词汇， 提升写作水平

### 替换练习

> 将以下句子中的基础词汇替换为本日学习的高级词汇：

| 原句 | 替换后 |
|-----|-------|
| The company's profits went up quickly. | The company's profits **climbed** significantly. |
| He refused to accept the offer. | He **refrained from** accepting the offer. |
| The city has a very thick population. | The city has a **dense** population. |
| She has good skills in negotiation. | She is **skilled at** negotiation. |
| The economy stopped growing. | The economy **reached a plateau**. |
| They chose to invest in renewable energy. | They **opted for** renewable energy investment. |
| Please don't leave out any details. | Please don't **omit** any details. |
| The report improved their strategy. | The report **refined** their strategy. |
| She showed grace under pressure. | She displayed **grace** under pressure. |
| Blood moves around the body. | Blood **circulates** through the body. |
| We need a long period of hard work. | We need **endurance** over time. |

## Part2: 句子改写
> [!warning] 使用本日学习的词汇重写以下句子

### 娡板1: opt for
> **原句**： Many students are choosing online courses.
>
> **升级**： An increasing number of students are **opting for** online courses.
>
> **考研应用**： In recent years, many graduates have been **opting for** further education rather than entering the job market directly.

### 模板2: refrain from
> **原句**： You should avoid making hasty decisions.
>
> **升级**： You should **refrain from** making hasty decisions.
>
> **考研应用**： Journalists should **refrain from** expressing personal opinions in their reporting.

### 模板3: reach a plateau
> **原句**： The housing market has stopped growing.
>
> **升级**： The housing market has **reached a plateau**.
>
> **考研应用**： After years of rapid development, the company's innovation seems to have **reached a plateau**.

### 模板4: dense → density
> **原句**: The area has a high population density.
>
> **升级**: The area is characterized by its high population **density**.
>
> **考研应用**: Urban planners must to consider population **density** when designing public transportation systems.

## Part4: 写作模板句型

> [!example] 高分写作常用句型

### 句型1: opt for + n. (选择...)
- More companies are **opting for** sustainable development strategies.
-（越来越多的公司选择可持续发展战略。）
- Many students **opt for** taking a gap year before university.
-（许多学生选择在上大学前休学一年。）

### 句型2: refrain from + doing sth (克制不做某事)
- Please **refrain from** using mobile phones during the meeting.
-（请会议期间克制使用手机。）
- The government should **refrain from** interfering in the market.
-（政府应克制干预市场。）

### 句型3: reach a plateau (进入停滞期)
- After initial success, many startups **reach a plateau** and struggle to grow further.
-（初期成功后，许多初创企业进入停滞期，难以进一步增长。）

### 句型4: be skilled at/in + n./doing (擅长...)
- She is highly **skilled at** managing complex projects.
-（她非常擅长管理复杂项目。）
- Workers need to be **skilled in** using new technologies.
-（工人需要熟练使用新技术。）

### 句型5: circulate through/among (在...中流通/传播)
- Rumors began to **circulate through** the office.
-（谣言开始在办公室里传播。）
- Information **circulates among** team members instantly.
-（信息在团队成员之间即时流通。）

### 句型6: descend from/into (起源于/陷入)
- This tradition **descends from** ancient customs.
-（这一传统起源于古代习俗。）
- The company **descended into** chaos after the scandal.
-（丑闻发生后，公司陷入混乱。）

## Part5: 僻义词汇运用
> [!warning] 运用僻义词汇完成句子

### 僻义1: climate (n. 氛围，局势)
> **句子**： The current political **climate** is not favorable for foreign investment.
> （当前的政治**氛围**不利于外国投资。）

### 僻义2: slight (vt. 冷落，轻视)
> **句子**： She felt **slighted** by her colleagues' lack of attention.
> （她感到被同事们**冷落**了，因为他们没有注意到她。）

### 僻义3: plateau (n. 停滞期)
> **句子**： After years of progress, his career seems to have hit a **plateau**.
> （经过多年的进步，他的事业似乎进入了**停滞期**。）

### 僻义4: induce (vt. 引起，导致)
> **句子**： The medication may **induce** side effects in some patients.
> （这种药物可能在某些患者身上**引起**副作用。）

## Part6: 话题写作
> [!quote] 使用本日词汇完成以下话题写作

### 话题1: 科技与社会 (Technology and Society)
> **关键词**: platform, network, circulate, opt for, skilled

With the rise of digital **platforms**, social **networks** have become powerful tools for communication. Information **circulates** rapidly among users, and more people **opt for** online services. As a result, there is a growing demand for **skilled** workers in the tech industry.

（随着数字**平台**的兴起，社交**网络**已成为强大的沟通工具。信息在用户之间快速**流通**，越来越多的人**选择**在线服务。因此，科技行业对**熟练**工人的需求不断增长。）

### 话题2: 城市化与人口 (Urbanization and Population)
> **关键词**: dense, density, citizen, civil, civilization

As cities become more **dense**, population **density** continues to increase. **Citizens** face challenges such as housing shortages and traffic congestion. **Civil** society organizations are working to address these issues and improve the quality of urban life. Throughout **civilization**, humans have adapted to changing environments.
（随着城市变得更加**密集**，人口**密度**持续增加。**公民**面临住房短缺和交通拥堵等挑战。**公民**社会组织正在努力解决这些问题，提高城市生活质量。在整个**文明**进程中，人类一直在适应不断变化的环境。）
