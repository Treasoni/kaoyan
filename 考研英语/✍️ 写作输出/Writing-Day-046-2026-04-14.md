## Part B: 汉译英练习（5题）

> [!note] 使用本单元词汇翻译以下句子

### 1. 除了高昂的学费之外，许多大学生还面临着住宿问题。

> **参考翻译**: **Aside from** high tuition fees, many college students also face accommodation problems.
>
> **重点词**: aside from(除...之外)

### 2. 过去十年间，城市房价猛增，普通家庭难以承受。

> **参考翻译**: Over the past decade, urban housing prices have **ballooned**, making them unaffordable for ordinary families.
>
> **重点词**: balloon(vi.猛增)

### 3. 政府应该对工业排放加以限制，以保护环境。

> **参考翻译**: The government should **put a cap on** industrial emissions to protect the environment.
>
> **重点词**: put a cap on(对...加以限制)

### 4. 社会有责任照顾弱势群体，包括老人和残疾人。

> **参考翻译**: Society has a responsibility to **care for** vulnerable groups, including the elderly and the disabled.
>
> **重点词**: care for(关心，照顾)

### 5. 调查人员在寻找解决方案时碰壁了，因为数据严重不足。

> **参考翻译**: The investigators **hit a brick wall** in their search for a solution because of a severe lack of data.
>
> **重点词**: hit a brick wall(碰壁)

## Part C: 高级写作句型（5个模板）

> [!example] 直接可用于考研写作的句型

### 句型1: 补充说明
```
Aside from + n. phrase, + clause
```
**示例**: **Aside from** technological innovation, educational reform is equally important for national development.

### 句型2: 数据描述（猛增）
```
...has/have ballooned to + 数量/程度
```
**示例**: The number of online shoppers **has ballooned to** over 800 million in China.

### 句型3: 政策限制
```
impose/put a cap on + n. phrase
```
**示例**: It is imperative that the government **put a cap on** greenhouse gas emissions.

### 句型4: 责任关怀
```
It is our responsibility to care for + n. phrase
```
**示例**: **It is our responsibility to care for** the environment for future generations.

### 句型5: 困境转折
```
...hit a brick wall when/because + clause
```
**示例**: The research team **hit a brick wall** when they realized the existing data was unreliable.

## Part D: 易混淆词辨析（3组）

### 辨析1: care for vs care about vs take care of

| 词汇 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **care for** | 照顾/照料/喜欢 | She spent years caring for her sick mother. |
| **care about** | 在意/关心/担忧 | He doesn't care about what others think. |
| **take care of** | 照顾/处理 | Please take care of this issue immediately. |

### 辨析2: bank (银行) vs bank (河岸)

| 含义 | 词性 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **bank** (银行) | n. | She deposited money in the bank. |
| **bank** (河岸/堤) | n. | They had a picnic on the river bank. |
| **bank** (存入银行) | vt. | He banked the cheque right away. |

**注意**: 写作中需根据上下文判断 bank 的含义。"bank loan"(银行贷款) vs "river bank"(河岸)。

### 辨析3: bull market vs bear market

| 词汇 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **bull market** | 牛市（行情上涨） | Investors made profits during the bull market. |
| **bear market** | 熊市（行情下跌） | Many lost their savings in the bear market. |

**记忆技巧**: bull（公牛）用角向上顶 = 牛市上涨；bear（熊）用爪向下拍 = 熊市下跌。
