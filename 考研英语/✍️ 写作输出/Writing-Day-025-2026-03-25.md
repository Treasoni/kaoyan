> Her argument was **lame** and unconvincing.

> This **minute** improvement suggests the treatment is working.

## 📋 词义辨析
### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. sacred / holy**
The temple is considered a ______ place by believers.
> The temple is considered a **sacred** place by believers.
（sacred = 神圣的，强调尊严； holy = 神圣的，强调宗教性）
**17. pale / faint)
The colors in the painting were too ______ to be seen clearly.
> the colors in the painting were too **faint** to be seen clearly.
（pale = 苍白的，强调颜色浅； faint = 微弱的，强调看不清）
**18. lack / shortage**
The There is a ______ of clean water in this region.
> There is a **shortage** of clean water in this region.
（lack = 缺乏，更通用； shortage = 短缺，更强调供应不足）
**19. panic / fear**
> She felt a sudden ______ when she saw the snake.
> She felt a sudden **panic** when she saw the snake.
（panic = 恐慌， 更强调突然恐惧； fear = 害怕， 更强调持续状态）
**20. minute / tiny)
> the **____** details in the contract are often overlooked.
> The **minute** details in the contract are often overlooked.
（minute = 微小的， 强调细节； tiny = 极小的， 强调大小）
