## 题型1: 写作替换升级 (1-5)

### 1. 表达"强迫某人做某事"

> 原句: The new law **makes** companies reduce pollution.
>
> 任务: 使用 **compel** 改写此句

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: The new law **compels** companies to reduce pollution.

**评注**:
- compel 比 make 更正式，常用于法律、政策语境
- 搭配: compel sb to do sth
- 写作加分点: 使用 compel 体现用词的精准性

</details>

---

### 2. 表达"承认问题并弥补"

> 原句: The company **said sorry** for the mistake and **paid** customers back.
>
> 任务: 使用 **acknowledge** 和 **compensate** 改写

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: The company **acknowledged** the mistake and **compensated** customers for their losses.

**评注**:
- acknowledge 比 say sorry 更正式，体现企业责任
- compensate sb for sth 就…对某人进行补偿
- 写作加分点: 双动词搭配，逻辑清晰

</details>

---

### 3. 表达"明智的决定"

> 原句: It was a **smart** decision to save money.
>
> 任务: 使用 **sensible** 改写

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: It was a **sensible** decision to save money for emergencies.

**评注**:
- sensible = 明智的，理智的
- 区别: sensitive = 敏感的
- 写作加分点: sensible 比 smart 更正式

</details>

---

### 4. 表达"促进经济增长"

> 原句: The new policy will **help** the economy **grow**.
>
> 任务: 使用 **boost** 改写

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: The new policy will **boost** the economy.

**评注**:
- boost = 使增长，使兴旺
- 搭配: boost the economy, boost employment
- 写作加分点: 单个动词替代动词短语，简洁有力

</details>

---

### 5. 表达"理性的选择"

> 原句: It is **reasonable** to choose a stable job.
>
> 任务: 使用 **rational** 改写

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: It is **rational** to choose a stable job in uncertain times.

**评注**:
- rational = 合理的；理性的
- 与 reasonable 近义，但 rational 更强调"基于理性的"
- 写作加分点: rational 体现逻辑思维能力

</details>

## 题型2: 汉译英练习 (6-10)

### 6. 分配资源

> 中文: 政府决定把更多资源分配给教育部门。
>
> 必用词: **allocate**

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: The government decided to **allocate** more resources **to** the education sector.

**评注**:
- allocate sth to sb/sth 把某物分配给某人/某部门
- 区别: distribute 更强调"分发"，allocate 更强调"按计划分配"

</details>

---

### 7. 积累经验

> 中文: 年轻人需要积累工作经验才能胜任这份工作。
>
> 必用词: **accumulate**, **competent**

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: Young people need to **accumulate** work experience to be **competent** for this job.

**评注**:
- accumulate = 积累，积聚
- be competent for/to do sth = 能胜任…
- 写作加分点: 双词搭配，逻辑递进

</details>

---

### 8. 敏感问题

> 中文: 他对别人对他工作的批评很敏感。
>
> 必用词: **sensitive**

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: He is very **sensitive to** criticism of his work.

**评注**:
- ⚠️ 易混淆: sensitive (敏感的) vs sensible (明智的)
- 搭配: be sensitive to/about sth 对…敏感
- 记忆: sensitive 有 "tive" = "active" = 倾向于感知

</details>

---

### 9. 比较分析

> 中文: 与去年相比，今年的销售额增长了20%。
>
> 必用词: **comparison**

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**答案**: **In comparison with** last year, this year's sales increased by 20%.

**评注**:
- in comparison with = 与…相比
- 也可用: by comparison / compared with
- 写作加分点: 介词短语开头，句式变化

</details>

### 10. 实现梦想

> 中文: 经过多年努力，她终于实现了成为医生的梦想。
>
> 必用词: **realize**

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

## 题型3: 词义辨析 (11-15)

### 11. sensible vs sensitive

> 题目: 想表达"她对这个问题很敏感"，应选择：
> - [ ] A. She is very **sensible** about this issue.
> - [ ] B. She is very **sensitive** about this issue.

<details>
<summary>解析</summary>

**答案**: B. sensitive

**辨析**:
- **sensible** = 明智的，理智的
  - 例: a sensible decision (明智的决定)
- **sensitive** = 敏感的
  - 例: be sensitive about criticism (对批评敏感)

**记忆技巧**:
- sensible → "能感知到正确的事" → 明智的
- sensitive → "倾向于感知的" → 敏感的

</details>

---

### 12. compare...with vs compare...to

> 题目: 想表达"教授把人脑比作电脑"，应选择：
> - [ ] A. The professor **compared** the human brain **with** a computer.
> - [ ] B. The professor **compared** the human brain **to** a computer.

<details>
<summary>解析</summary>

**答案**: B. compare...to

**辨析**:
- **compare ... with ...** = 与…相比较（强调对比差异）
  - 例: Compare this car with that one. (比较这两辆车的差异)
- **compare ... to ...** = 把... 比作...（强调比喻相似性）
  - 例: The poet compared love to a rose. (诗人把爱比作玫瑰)

</details>

---

### 13. compete vs compensate

> 题目: 想表达"公司必须为损失赔偿客户"，应选择：
> - [ ] A. The company must **compete** customers for the loss.
> - [ ] B. The company must **compensate** customers for the loss.

<details>
<summary>解析</summary>

**答案**: B. compensate

**辨析**:
- **compete** = 竞争
  - 搭配: compete for sth / compete with sb
- **compensate** = 补偿，赔偿
  - 搭配: compensate sb for sth 就…对某人进行补偿

**记忆技巧**:
- compete → competition (竞争)
- compensate → compensation (补偿)

</details>

---

### 14. rate vs ratio

> 题目: 想表达"失业率持续上升"，应选择：
> - [ ] A. The unemployment **rate** continues to rise.
> - [ ] B. The unemployment **ratio** continues to rise.

<details>
<summary>解析</summary>

**答案**: A. rate

**辨析**:
- **rate** = 比率；速度；率（强调变化的速率）
  - 例: unemployment rate (失业率), birth rate (出生率), interest rate (利率)
- **ratio** = 比例（强调两个数量之间的关系）
  - 例: the ratio of A to B (A与B的比例), student-teacher ratio (师生比)

</details>

### 15. board 的僻义

> 题目: "All passengers must board the plane before 9 PM." 中 board 的意思是：
> - [ ] A. 木板
> - [ ] B. 董事会
> - [ ] C. 登上
> - [ ] D. 提供膳食

<details>
<summary>解析</summary>

**答案**: C. 登上

**辨析**: ⚠️ 僻义预警！
- board 的常见义:
  - n. 木板
  - n. 董事会
  - n. 膳食
- **board 的考研僻义**:
  - v. 登上（交通工具）
  - vi. 寄宿

**真题例句**:
> Passengers were waiting to **board** the flight.
> 乘客们正在等待登机。

</details>

## 📌 写作建议

1. **动词替换**: 用 compel, acknowledge, compensate, boost 替代基础动词
2. **形容词升级**: 用 sensible, rational, competitive 替代 smart, reasonable
3. **搭配记忆**: be sensitive to, allocate...to, compensate sb for
4. **僻义应用**: board (登上), choice (精选的), brief (给指示)
