## Part 1: 写作替换升级（3题）

> [!tip] 技巧说明
> 用更高级的词汇替换划线部分，提升表达档次

### 1. 原句改写

**原句**：The effects of the policy ~~cover~~ a wide ~~range~~ of social groups.

**升级后**： The effects of the policy ~~extend across~~ a broad **spectrum** of social groups.

**解析**：
- cover a wide range → extend across a broad spectrum
- spectrum 比 range 更正式，常用于学术或正式写作

---

### 2. 请用更高级的表达替换

**原句**：The company's success ~~shows~~ that hard work ~~leads to~~ good results.

**提示**：
- shows → demonstrates / illustrates
- leads to → translates into / yields
- good results → positive outcomes / desirable results

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- The company's success **demonstrates** that hard work **translates into** desirable results.
- The company's success **illustrates** that dedication **yields** positive outcomes.

**高级表达**：
- demonstrate: 论证，证明
- translate into: 转化为
- yield: 产出，产生
</details>

---

### 3. 请用更高级的表达替换

**原句**：The new project will ~~start~~ next month and is expected to ~~grow~~ quickly.

**提示**：
- start → commence / initiate
- grow → blossom / flourish / expand

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- The new project will **commence** next month and is expected to **blossom** quickly.
- The new initiative will **initiate** next month and is expected to **flourish** rapidly.

**高级表达**：
- commence: 正式开始（比 start 更正式）
- blossom: 蓬勃发展（比喻用法）
- flourish: 繁荣，兴旺
</details>

## Part 2: 汉译英练习（4题）

### 4. 将以下句子翻译成英文

**中文**：这一政策的受益者不仅限于城市居民，还延伸到了农村地区。

**关键词提示**：
- 不仅限于： extend beyond / not be limited to
- 城市居民： urban residents
- 农村地区： rural areas

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
1. The benefits of this policy **extend beyond** urban **residents** to rural areas.
2. The beneficiaries of this policy are not **limited to** city **residents** but also include those in rural regions.
3. This policy benefits not only urban **residents** but also extends to rural communities.

**亮点表达**：
- extend beyond: 延伸到...之外
- resident: 居民（比 people 更正式）
</details>

### 5. 将以下句子翻译成英文

**中文**：随着人工智能的发展，许多传统工作面临被自动化取代的风险，这引发了广泛的担忧。

**关键词提示**：
- 随着...的发展： with the development of / as ... evolves
- 面临风险： face the risk of
- 引发担忧： spark concern / raise concerns

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
1. With the development of artificial intelligence, many traditional jobs **face the risk of** being replaced by automation, which has **sparked widespread concern**.
2. As AI technology **evolves**, numerous conventional occupations are at risk of automation, **raising concerns** across society.
3. The advancement of AI has **fluctuated** the job market, **triggering** anxiety about employment security.

**亮点表达**：
- spark concern: 引发担忧
- face the risk of: 面临...的风险
- widespread concern: 广泛的担忧
</details>

**中文**：为了维护国家尊严，政府决定采取更强硬的措施来应对这一危机。

**关键词提示**：
- 维护尊严： maintain/preserve dignity
- 采取强硬措施： take tough measures / adopt resolute actions
- 应对危机： address the crisis / tackle the crisis

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：

2. In order to **preserve the nation's dignity**, the administration resolved to take tougher measures to **tackle** the emergency.
3. For the sake of **national dignity**, the government is **resolved** to implement stronger policies in response to this crisis.

**亮点表达**：
- maintain dignity: 维护尊严
- resolute actions: 坚决的行动
- address the crisis: 应对危机

**中文**：这家公司因忽视员工福利而声名狼藉，导致大量人才流失。

## Part 3: 词义辨析（3题）

### 8. 辨析题：fluctuate vs. float

**请选择正确的词填空**：

1. Oil prices ______ according to global demand. (fluctuate / float)
2. The boat ______ gently on the calm water. (fluctuates / floats)
3. His attention ______ between the two tasks. (fluctuated / floated)

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**答案**：
1. **fluctuate** - 油价根据全球需求波动（指价格变化）
2. **floats** - 船在平静的水面上漂浮（物理上的漂浮）
3. **fluctuated** - 他的注意力在两个任务之间波动（比喻用法，指注意力变化）

**辨析要点**：
- **fluctuate**: 强调"波动、起伏"，常用于价格、情绪、数据等抽象概念
- **float**: 强调"漂浮"，既可是物理上的漂浮，也可以是抽象的"浮动"

**记忆口诀**：价格波动用 fluctuate，物理漂浮用 float
</details>

---

### 9. 辨析题：represent vs. reproduce

**请选择正确的词填空**：

1. The lawyer will ______ the client in court. (represent / reproduce)
2. The experiment failed to ______ the expected results. (represent / reproduce)
3. This painting ______ the artist's unique style. (represents / reproduces)

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**答案**：
1. **represent** - 律师将在法庭上代表客户（代表）
2. **reproduce** - 实验未能复现预期结果（复制/再现）
3. **represents** - 这幅画代表了艺术家独特的风格（代表/体现）

**辨析要点**：
- **represent**: 代表，表现，相当于
- **reproduce**: 复制，再生产，繁殖，再现

**记忆口诀**：人代表用 represent，物复制用 reproduce
</details>

### 10. 辨析题：commence vs. commence

**请判断以下句子中 commence 的含义**：

1. The ceremony will commence at 9 a.m.
2. The governor commuted the prisoner's sentence.

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案</summary>

**答案**：
1. **开始** - 仪式将于上午9点开始。（基本义）
2. **减刑** - 州长给囚犯减刑了。（僻义）

**辨析要点**：
- **commence (基本义)**: 开始，着手
- **commute (僻义)**: 减刑（commute a sentence）

**注意**：第2句中的词是 **commute**（不是 commence），这里展示了形近词辨析。

**易混词**：
- commence: 开始
- commute: 通勤 / 减刑
- command: 命令
- comment: 评论
</details>

## 📝 写作模板积累

### 模板1： 描述趋势变化

```
The [phenomenon/trend] has blossomed into a significant force that [effect].
[Topic] has fluctuated significantly in recent years, sparking concerns about [issue].
```

**应用示例**：
- The gig economy has blossomed into a significant force that reshapes employment.
- Housing prices have fluctuated significantly in recent years, sparking concerns about affordability.

---

### 模板2： 表达观点评价

```
[Subject] is notorious for [negative aspect], which has led to [consequence].
To be blunt, [direct statement]. However, this perspective fails to consider [counterargument].
```

**应用示例**：
- The company is notorious for its environmental violations, which has led to widespread boycotts.
- To be blunt, the policy is flawed. However, this perspective fails to consider the economic constraints involved.

---

### 模板3： 表达未来展望

```
Experts envisage a future where [prediction].
The resolution of this issue requires [action], as outlined in the blueprint for [plan].
```

**应用示例**：
- Experts envisage a future where renewable energy dominates global power generation.
- The resolution of this crisis requires international cooperation, as outlined in the blueprint for sustainable development.

## 🎯 今日写作任务

> [!challenge] 挑战任务
> 请使用以下至少5个词汇写一段100-150词的议论文段落：
> - spectrum, represent, fluctuate, equivalent, envisage

<details>
<summary>点击查看参考范文</summary>

**范文**：

The debate over universal basic income (UBI) has intensified across the political **spectrum**. Proponents **envisage** a future where financial security is guaranteed for all citizens, arguing that UBI would **represent** a fundamental shift in social policy. Critics, however, worry that labor market participation might **fluctuate** significantly if work is no longer necessary for survival. Some economists suggest that UBI could be **equivalent** to a negative income tax, while others argue the costs would be prohibitive. As pilot programs commence worldwide, the evidence remains inconclusive, leaving policymakers to balance idealism with fiscal reality.

**亮点分析**：
- spectrum: 用于描述政治立场的范围
- envisage: 用于表达对未来的设想
- represent: 用于说明某事物代表某种变化
- fluctuate: 用于描述劳动力市场的波动
- equivalent: 用于比较两种政策的等价性

</details>
