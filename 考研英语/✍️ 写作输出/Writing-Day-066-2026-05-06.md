## ✍️ 词组搭配练习

### 动词搭配

1. **sift through** = 仔细审查
   - 例句: Journalists must **sift through** vast amounts of data daily.

2. **scoop up** = 抢先获得
   - 例句: The website **scooped up** the exclusive story.

3. **rustle up** = 凑集
   - 例句: Let me **rustle up** some support for the proposal.

4. **silt up** = 淤塞
   - 例句: The harbor has **silted up** over the years.

5. **simmer down** = 平静下来
   - 例句: After the argument, she needed time to **simmer down**.

### 介词搭配

1. **in the spotlight** = 处于关注中心
   - 例句: The celebrity finds herself constantly **in the spotlight**.

2. **in a rut** = 墨守成规
   - 例句: Being **in a rut** can stifle personal growth.

3. **out of sight** = 看不见
   - 例句: The mountains were **out of sight** from our location.

4. **at first sight** = 乍一看
   - 例句: **At first sight**, the problem seemed simple.

5. **in a stew** = 焦虑不安
   - 例句: He was **in a stew** about the upcoming exam.

## ✍️ 汉译英练习

### 句子翻译（使用本单元单词）

1. 经济已经停滞不前。
   → The economy has reached a **standstill**.

2. 这个发现引起了轰动效应。
   → The discovery created a **sensational effect**.

3. 新政策旨在重振当地经济。
   → New policies aim to **revitalize** the local economy.

4. 短期主义思维会损害长期发展。
   → **Short-termism** thinking can harm long-term development.

5. 媒体需要恢复其社会责任。
   → Media needs to restore its social **restoration**.

6. 社交媒体改变了我们消费新闻的空间方式。
   → Social media has changed the **spatial** way we consume news.

7. 敏感性问题需要仔细审查。
   → **Sensitivity** issues require careful **sift**ing through.

8. 自我提升是个人成长的重要部分。
   → **Self-help** is an important part of personal growth.

9. 这位智者因睿智而受到尊敬。
   → The **sage** was respected for being **sagacious**.

10. 博物馆正在进行修复工作。
    → The museum is under **restoration**.
