## 📝 写作替换练习（10题）
### 说明
将括号中的简单表达替换为更高级的考研词汇。

---

**1. The teacher (explained/made clear) the difficult concept.**
> The teacher **clarified** the difficult concept.

---

**2. We need to (make better/improve) our team's performance.**
> We need to **elevate** our team's performance.

---

**3. The two companies (joined/combined) to form a larger corporation.**
> The two companies **united** to form a larger corporation.

---

**4. Please (make sure/check) that all documents are classified correctly.**
> Please **classify** all documents correctly.

---

**5. The story (revealed/showed) the truth about the incident.**
> The story **unfolded** the truth about the incident.

---

**6. She is (qualified/able) to apply for this scholarship.**
> She is **eligible** to apply for this scholarship.

---

**7. The army (placed/positioned) troops along the border.**
> The army **deployed** troops along the border.

---

**8. The crisis (used up/exhausted) most of their resources.**
> The crisis **depleted** most of their resources.

---

**9. He is about to (start/begin) a new career journey.**
> He is about to **embark on** a new career journey.

---

**10. The government (expressed/said) its concern about the issue.**
> The government **voiced** its concern about the issue.

## 📋 词义辨析（5题）
### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. classic / classical**
Beethoven's symphonies are considered ______ music masterpieces.

> Beethoven's symphonies are considered **classical** music masterpieces.
（classical = 古典的；classic = 经典的，一流的）

---

**17. clarify / classify**
The librarian needs to ______ these books by genre.

> The librarian needs to **classify** these books by genre.
（classify = 分类；clarify = 澄清，阐明）

---

**18. depart / deploy**
The military decided to ______ additional forces to the region.

> The military decided to **deploy** additional forces to the region.
（deploy = 部署；depart = 离开）

---

**19. unite / unfold**
The two nations ______ against the common threat.

> The two nations **united** against the common threat.
（unite = 联合；unfold = 展开）

---

**20. elevate / eligible**
Only candidates with a master's degree are ______ for this position.

> Only candidates with a master's degree are **eligible** for this position.
（eligible = 有资格的；elevate = 提高，提拔）
