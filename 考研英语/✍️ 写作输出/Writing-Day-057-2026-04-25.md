## 一、高级词汇替换练习

考研写作中，使用高级词汇替换基础词汇是提分关键。以下从今日词汇中提取可用于写作替换的词汇。

### 1.1 基础词 → 高级替换

| 基础表达 | 高级替换 | 例句 |
|---------|---------|------|
| want | **wish**（希望） | We **wish** to emphasize that... |
| overcome | **weather**（渡过） | The nation must **weather** this crisis. |
| control | **under**（受控） | The situation is now **under** control. |
| give up | **abdicate**（放弃） | Leaders must not **abdicate** their responsibilities. |
| desire | **thirsty for**（渴望） | Students are **thirsty for** knowledge. |
| use | **make use of**（利用） | We should **make use of** available resources. |
| good | **admirable**（令人钦佩的） | Her dedication to research is **admirable**. |
| responsibility | **accountability**（问责） | There must be **accountability** in governance. |
| gather | **accrue**（积累） | Benefits **accrue** over time. |
| news | **word**（消息） | **Word** spread that the policy had changed. |

### 1.2 写作替换练习

请用高级替换词改写以下句子：

**题目1**：The company needs to overcome the economic crisis.
> **参考答案**：The company must **weather** the economic crisis.

**题目2**：People should use new technology to improve efficiency.
> **参考答案**：People should **make use of** new technology to improve efficiency.

> **参考答案**：The government must not **abdicate** its responsibility to protect citizens.

**题目4**：Young people really want to get more knowledge.
> **参考答案**：Young people are **thirsty for** more knowledge.

**题目5**：Heard the news that the policy has been revised.
> **参考答案**：**Word** came that the policy had been revised.

## 三、词义辨析练习

### 3.1 used to do vs be used to doing vs be used to do

**题目1**：He ______ (过去常常) play basketball after school. (used to / is used to)
> **答案**：used to（过去常常打篮球，现在不打了）

**题目2**：She ______ (习惯��) getting up early. (used to / is used to)
> **答案**：is used to（习惯于早起）

**题目3**：Wood ______ (被用来) make furniture. (used to / is used to)
> **答案**：is used to（木材被用来制作家具）

**记忆口诀**：
- used to + do = 过去常常（现已不这样）
- be used to + doing = 习惯于（现在仍然这样）
- be used to + do = 被用来做（被动语态）

### 3.2 have a word with vs have words with

**题目4**：I need to ______ him about the project. (have a word with / have words with)
> **答案**：have a word with（需要和他谈谈项目）

**题目5**：They ______ over money. (had a word / had words)
> **答案**：had words（他们因为钱吵了一架）

### 3.3 wish vs hope

**题目6**：I ______ I were a bird. (wish / hope)
> **答案**：wish（虚拟语气，不可能实现的愿望）

**题目7**：I ______ you pass the exam. (wish / hope)
> **答案**：hope（可能实现的期望）

### 3.4 be tired of vs be tired from

**题目8**：I am ______ studying all day. (tired of / tired from)
> **答案**：tired from（因为整天学习而疲劳 — 生理原因）

**题目9**：She is ______ doing the same work every day. (tired of / tired from)
> **答案**：tired of（厌倦每天做同样的工作 — 心理厌倦）

### 4.1 议论文开头段

> In today's **world**, the issue of **accountability** has attracted widespread attention. **Usually**, when institutions **abdicate** their responsibilities, the consequences are far-reaching. **Word** has spread that [topic], raising serious concerns among the public.

### 4.2 议论文论述段

> The **trouble** with [problem] is that benefits fail to **accrue** to those who need them most. **Under** the current system, leaders operate **without** meaningful oversight. **Unless** we **make use of** effective regulatory mechanisms, the situation will not improve. We should be **thirsty for** reform rather than complacent.

### 4.3 议论文结尾段

> It is the **very** **will** of the people that drives meaningful change. **Once** citizens refuse to **stay** silent, institutions must **weather** the storm of public scrutiny. The **wish** for a better society can only be realized through collective **accountability**.
