## Part B: 汉译英练习（5题）

> [!note] 使用本单元词汇翻译以下句子

### 1. 这项政策旨在促进经济发展，尽管面临诸多挑战。

> **参考翻译**: This policy aims to **further** economic development, **albeit** facing numerous challenges.
>
> **重点词**: further(v.促进), albeit(conj.尽管)

### 2. 不论年龄和背景，每个人都有平等的机会。

> **参考翻译**: **Irrespective of** age and background, everyone has equal opportunities.
>
> **重点词**: irrespective of(不论)

### 3. 由于恶劣天气，航班被取消了。

> **参考翻译**: **Owing to** the severe weather, flights were cancelled.
>
> **重点词**: owing to(由于)

### 4. 政府应该保护公民免受环境污染的危害。

> **参考翻译**: The government should **protect** citizens **from** environmental pollution.
>
> **重点词**: protect...from...(保护…免受…)

### 5. 这家公司因违反安全法规而被起诉。

> **参考翻译**: The company was **prosecuted** **for** violating safety regulations.
>
> **重点词**: prosecute for(因…被起诉)

## Part C: 高级写作句型（5个模板）

> [!example] 直接可用于考研写作的句型

### 句型1: 让步转折
```
..., albeit + adj./adv./prep. phrase
```
**示例**: The proposal was implemented, **albeit** with significant modifications.

### 句型2: 原因解释
```
...owing to + n. phrase
```
**示例**: The economic downturn, **owing to** global inflation, has affected millions.

### 句型3: 补充论证
```
Furthermore, + clause
```
**示例**: **Furthermore**, the environmental impact cannot be overlooked.

### 句型4: 条件包容
```
Irrespective of + n. phrase, + clause
```
**示例**: **Irrespective of** political affiliation, citizens must unite against the crisis.

### 句型5: 归因表达
```
...owe A to B = A is owed to B
```
**示例**: We **owe** our technological progress **to** decades of scientific research.

## Part D: 易混淆词辨析（3组）

### 辨析1: crush vs crash vs clash

| 词汇 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **crush** | 压碎/镇压 | The rebellion was crushed. |
| **crash** | 碰撞/崩溃 | The stock market crashed. |
| **clash** | 冲突/不协调 | Their opinions clashed. |

| 词汇 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| **dye** | 染色 (vt./n.) | She dyed her hair red. |
| **die** | 死亡 (vi.) | The old tree died in winter. |
| **dying** | dye的现在分词 OR die的现在分词 | Context needed! |
