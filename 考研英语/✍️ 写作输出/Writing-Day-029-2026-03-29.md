## 🎯 Part 2: 句型仿写

### 模仿真题语境文章中的高级句型

### 句型1: not just about A; it's about B
**原句**: Education is not just about carrying knowledge from one generation to the next; it's about gathering wisdom and facilitating personal growth.

**仿写练习**:
1. Success is not just about achieving goals; it's about enjoying the process and learning from failures.
2. Friendship is not just about having fun together; it's about supporting each other through difficult times.
3. Innovation is not just about creating new products; it's about solving real-world problems and improving people's lives.

### 句型2: in accordance with (按照，根据)
**原句**: Professors are in accordance with new teaching methods that hand students more control.

**仿写练习**:
1. All students must act in accordance with the university regulations.
2. The project was carried out in accordance with the original plan.
3. We should make decisions in accordance with the principles of fairness and justice.

### 句型3: come in handy (迟早有用)
**原句**: Handy digital tools and online resources come in handy when students need to carry through their research projects.

**仿写练习**:
1. Your language skills will come in handy when you travel abroad.
2. Don't throw away those old boxes—they might come in handy someday.
3. This basic knowledge of computers comes in handy in almost every job nowadays.

### 使用今日重点词汇进行翻译

**1. 大学应该给予学生更多的学术自由。**
> Universities should **accord** students more academic freedom.
>

**2. 新政策将促进两国之间的贸易发展。**

>
> 词汇：facilitate (促进)

>
> 词汇：language barrier (语言障碍)

## 📊 Part 4: 写作词汇积累

### 论述文常用表达

#### 表达"促进、帮助"
- **facilitate** (v.) 促进，使便利
- **promote** (v.) 促进，推动
- **advance** (v.) 推进，促进
- **assist** (v.) 协助，帮助

#### 表达"障碍、困难"
- **barrier** (n.) 障碍，阻碍
- **obstacle** (n.) 障碍，困难
- **hurdle** (n.) 障碍，难关
- **impediment** (n.) 妨碍，阻碍

#### 表达"实施、执行"
- **carry out** (phr.) 实施，执行
- **implement** (v.) 实施，执行
- **execute** (v.) 执行，实施
- **perform** (v.) 执行，履行

#### 表达"按照、根据"
- **in accordance with** (phr.) 按照，根据
- **according to** (phr.) 根据，按照
- **in line with** (phr.) 与...一致
- **based on** (phr.) 基于，根据

#### 表达"渴望的"
- **eager** (adj.) 渴望的，热切的
- **keen** (adj.) 渴望的，热心的
- **anxious** (adj.) 渴望的，急切的
- **enthusiastic** (adj.) 热情的，热心的

## ✨ Part 5: 高级句型升级

### 从简单句到高级句的升级

#### 升级1: 使用非谓语动词
**简单句**: Students who are eager to learn will succeed.
**高级句**: Students **eager to learn** will succeed.

#### 升级2: 使用倒装句
**简单句**: We seldom see such fabulous opportunities.
**高级句**: **Seldom do we see** such fabulous opportunities.

#### 升级3: 使用强调句
**简单句**: The university facilities facilitate student success.
**高级句**: **It is the university facilities that facilitate** student success.

#### 升级4: 使用分词结构
**简单句**: Because the language barrier exists, communication becomes difficult.
**高级句**: **The language barrier existing**, communication becomes difficult.

## 🎓 Part 6: 写作实战练习

### 主题：大学教育的价值

**题目**: Some people believe that university education should focus on academic knowledge, while others argue that it should also develop students' practical skills. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

**参考范文使用今日词汇**:

> University education serves multiple purposes in modern society. According to recent surveys, students are **eager** to develop both theoretical knowledge and practical skills during their college years.
>
> On one hand, academic knowledge forms the foundation of higher education. Universities **accord** students opportunities to explore various disciplines and **carry out** research projects. The **faculty** members, **in accordance with** academic standards, **facilitate** intellectual growth and critical thinking.
>
> On the other hand, practical skills are equally important. The **language barrier** that many students face highlights the need for communication skills. Internships and practical training **come in handy** when students enter the job market. Moreover, the ability to **gather** and analyze information is a **fabulous** asset in any career.
>
> In conclusion, university education should not create **barriers** between theory and practice. Instead, it should **facilitate** the integration of both, **halting** the traditional separation and **carrying forward** a more holistic approach to education.
