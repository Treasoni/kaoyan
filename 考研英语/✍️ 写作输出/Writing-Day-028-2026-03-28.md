# ✍️ Day 028 写作输出（2026-03-28）

## 📝 写作词汇替换练习

### Task 1: 基础替换

将下列句子中的普通词汇替换为今日学习的高级词汇：

| 原句 | 替换后 |
|------|--------|
| We need to **deal with** this problem. | we need to **tackle** this problem. |
| The crisis **weakened** public trust. | The crisis **undermined** public trust. |
| Water is **rare** in deserts. | Water is **scarce** in deserts. |
| We need **real** evidence. | We need **tangible** evidence. |
| They have a **mutual** agreement. | They have a **reciprocal** agreement. |
| This is **fake** information. | This is **false** information. |
| The economy is **failing badly**. | The economy is **tanking**. |
| He **didn't listen to** my advice. | He **turned a deaf ear to** my advice. |

---

### Task 2: 句子改写

使用指定词汇改写下列句子：

#### 2.1 使用 "recipe" 的僻义

**原句**：Too much confidence can cause disaster.

**改写**：Overconfidence is **a recipe for disaster**.

---

#### 2.2 使用 "vehicle" 的僻义

**原句**：Music is a way to express emotions.

**改写**：Music is **a vehicle for** expressing emotions.

---

#### 2.3 使用 "catalogue" 的僻义
**原句**：He has made many mistakes in his career.

**改写**：He has a **catalogue of** failures in his career.

---

#### 2.4 使用 "scarce" 作副词
**原句**：I hardly ever see him these days.

**改写**：I **scarce** see him these days. (文学用法)
**改写**：I **scarcely** see him these days. (常用用法)

### Task 3: 汉译英练习

将下列中文翻译成英文，使用今日学习的词汇：

#### 3.1 基础翻译

1. 政府正在努力**解决**失业问题。
   The government is struggling to **tackle** the unemployment problem.

2. 贸易战**削弱**了两国关系。
   The trade war **undermined** the relationship between the two countries.

3. 稀缺资源的分配是一个重要问题。
   The distribution of **scarce resources** is an important issue.

4. 我们需要**有力证据**来支持这个论点。
   We need **tangible evidence** to support this argument.

---

#### 3.2 进阶翻译（使用僻义）

5. 互联网已成为**传播**知识的重要**工具**。
   The Internet has become an important **vehicle for** spreading knowledge.

6. 忽视这些警告是**导致灾难的因素**。
   Ignoring these warnings is **a recipe for disaster**.

7. 他对父亲的建议**充耳不闻**。
   He **turned a deaf ear to** his father's advice.

8. 这家公司去年**倒闭了**。
   The company **tanked** last year.

### Task 4: 写作段落练习

使用至少 **5个** 今日学习的词汇，- 词汇需加粗

**练习：关于经济危机的写作**

---

**The Impact of Economic Crisis**

The Great **Recession** of 2008 was not merely an economic event; it **undermined** public confidence in financial institutions. As **scarce resources** became even more limited, governments struggled to **tackle** the mounting challenges.

The **abuse** of financial instruments, combined with **false** optimism, created **a recipe for disaster**. When housing prices began to recede, the entire system **tanked**. The crisis left **tangible** scars on millions of families.

To prevent another **catastrophe**, policymakers need **tangible evidence** and effective **tactics**. They must not **turn a deaf ear to** warning signs. Only through **reciprocal** cooperation can we build a sustainable future.

---
