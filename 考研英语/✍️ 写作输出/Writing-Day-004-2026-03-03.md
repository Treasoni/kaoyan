## 题型1：写作替换升级

### 练习 1
> **原句**：Many people think that the government should do something about pollution.
>
> **任务**：使用 `elaborate` 或 `contend` 改写此句

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案</summary>

**升级版 1**：Many experts **contend** that the government must take immediate action to address environmental issues.

**升级版 2**：Environmental advocates have **elaborated on** the urgent need for stricter pollution regulations.

**评注**：
- `contend` 比 `think` 更正式，用于学术观点表达
- `elaborate on` 将简单观点扩展为详细论述
</details>

---

### 练习 2
> **原句**：Big companies are buying small companies.
>
> **任务**：使用 `absorb` 或 `eliminate` 改写此句

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案</summary>

**升级版 1**：Large corporations are **absorbing** smaller competitors at an unprecedented rate.

**升级版 2**：Market consolidation has led dominant firms to **eliminate** smaller players through acquisitions.

**评注**：
- `absorb` 在商业语境中指"并购"，比 `buy` 更专业
- `eliminate` 强调竞争被消除的结果
</details>

>
> **必用词**：absence, capital, barrier

<details>
<summary>📝 参考答案</summary>

## 题型3：词义辨析（为写作选词）

### 练习 6
> **题目**：想表达"这个问题很复杂"，应选择：
> - [ ] A. This problem is complex.
> - [ ] B. This issue is **elaborate**.
> - [ ] C. This matter involves **elaborate** mechanisms.

<details>
<summary>📝 解析</summary>

**最佳答案**：C

**分析**：
- A 选项语法正确但用词基础
- B 选项错误：`elaborate` 形容"精心设计的"，不能直接形容"问题"
- C 选项正确：`elaborate mechanisms` = 复杂的机制

**写作建议**：
- 形容问题复杂 → complex, intricate, sophisticated
- 形容设计精细 → elaborate, detailed
</details>

---

### 练习 7
> **题目**：在写作中想表达"禁止某人做某事"，最正式的表达是：
> - [ ] A. stop sb from doing sth
> - [ ] B. **bar** sb from doing sth
> - [ ] C. **ban** sb from doing sth

<details>
<summary>📝 解析</summary>

**答案**：B 或 C 均可，但有细微差别

**辨析**：
- `bar from`：法律或制度上的禁止，更正式
- `ban from`：官方明令禁止，常用于政策
- `stop from`：日常用语，不够正式

**写作建议**：
- 议论文中优先使用 `bar from` 或 `prohibit from`
</details>

## ✅ 今日写作任务

> [!task] 任务
> 使用今日词汇写一段关于"企业并购影响"的议论文（5-6句），必须包含：
> - absorb 或 eliminate
> - capital
> - mediate 或 elaborate
> - figure out

<details>
<summary>📝 范文参考</summary>

The rapid pace of corporate mergers has raised significant concerns about market competition. Large corporations are **absorbing** smaller competitors at an unprecedented rate, which may eventually **eliminate** consumer choices. While companies **contend** that such consolidation leads to greater efficiency, critics argue that it merely concentrates **capital** in fewer hands. Regulators must **figure out** effective ways to monitor these transactions. Government agencies should **mediate between** corporate interests and consumer protection to ensure fair competition.

（企业并购的快速发展引发了对市场竞争的重大担忧。大公司正以前所未有的速度**吞并**较小的竞争对手，这可能最终**消除**消费者的选择。虽然公司**声称**这种整合能带来更高的效率，但批评者认为这只会将**资本**集中在更少的人手中。监管机构必须**弄清楚**监控这些交易的有效方法。政府机构应在企业利益和消费者保护之间进行**调解**，以确保公平竞争。）
</details>
