## Section A: 词汇替换练习

**说明**: 用今天学习的 in-/im-/ir- 前缀高级词汇替换句子中的简单表达。

### 1. not enough -> insufficient

**原句**: The funding provided for the research project was **not enough** to cover all the necessary expenses.

**替换**: The funding provided for the research project was **insufficient** to cover all the necessary expenses.

**译文**: 提供给该研究项目的资金不足以支付所有必要的开支。

---

### 2. not caring -> indifference

**原句**: The government's **lack of caring** toward the plight of the rural poor has drawn widespread criticism.

**替换**: The government's **indifference** toward the plight of the rural poor has drawn widespread criticism.

**译文**: 政府对农村贫困人口困境的漠不关心引起了广泛批评。

---

### 3. not important -> insignificant

**原句**: The difference in the two sets of data is **not important** and can be ignored.

**替换**: The difference in the two sets of data is **insignificant** and can be ignored.

**译文**: 两组数据之间的差异微不足道，可以忽略不计。

---

### 4. not compatible -> incompatible

**原句**: His personal beliefs are **not compatible with** the company's corporate culture.

**替换**: His personal beliefs are **incompatible with** the company's corporate culture.

**译文**: 他的个人信仰与公司的企业文化格格不入。

---

### 5. not directly -> indirectly

**原句**: The new policy will **not directly** affect small businesses, but it will change the market environment.

**替换**: The new policy will **indirectly** affect small businesses by altering the market environment.

**译文**: 新政策将通过改变市场环境间接影响小企业。

---

### 6. not effective -> ineffective

**原句**: Traditional teaching methods have proven **not effective** in engaging digital-native students.

**替换**: Traditional teaching methods have proven **ineffective** in engaging digital-native students.

**译文**: 传统教学方法在吸引数字原生代学生方面已被证明无效。

---

### 7. not appropriate -> inappropriate

**原句**: The manager's comments were **not appropriate** for a professional workplace setting.

**替换**: The manager's comments were **inappropriate** for a professional workplace setting.

**译文**: 经理的评论不适用于专业职场环境。

---

### 8. not complete -> incomplete

**原句**: The historical records from that period are **not complete**, leaving many questions unanswered.

**替换**: The historical records from that period remain **incomplete**, leaving many questions unanswered.

**译文**: 该时期的历史记录仍然不完整，留下了许多未解之谜。

---

### 9. not sensitive -> insensitive

**原句**: The journalist's remarks about the tragedy were **not sensitive** to the victims' families.

**替换**: The journalist's remarks about the tragedy were **insensitive** to the victims' families.

**译文**: 记者关于这场悲剧的言论对受害者家属麻木不仁。

---

### 10. not competent -> incompetent

**原句**: The board dismissed the CEO, claiming he was **not competent** to handle the financial crisis.

**替换**: The board dismissed the CEO, claiming he was **incompetent** to handle the financial crisis.

**译文**: 董事会解雇了CEO，称他无法胜任处理金融危机的工作。

## Section B: 汉译英练习

**说明**: 将下列中文句子翻译成英文，要求使用括号中给出的目标单词。

### 1. 全球化使各国经济相互依赖日益加深。（interdependence）

**参考答案**: Globalization has deepened the **interdependence** of national economies.

### 2. 具有讽刺意味的是，过度追求效率反而导致了整个系统的低效。（ironically, inefficient）

**参考答案**: **Ironically**, the excessive pursuit of efficiency has rendered the entire system **inefficient**.

### 3. 一个公司的创新能力是衡量其长期竞争力的重要指标。（innovative, indicator）

**参考答案**: A company's **innovative** capacity is a crucial **indicator** of its long-term competitiveness.

### 4. 体制性障碍阻碍了发展中国家工业化的进程。（institutional, industrialization）

**参考答案**: **Institutional** barriers have hindered the process of **industrialization** in developing countries.

### 5. 缺乏确凿的证据使他的论点站不住脚。（inconclusive, indefensible）

**参考答案**: The **inconclusive** evidence rendered his argument **indefensible**.

### 6. 考生全神贯注地阅读题目，试图凭借直觉找出正确答案。（intently, intuitively）

**参考答案**: The examinees read the questions **intently**, trying **intuitively** to identify the correct answers.

### 7. 他天生具有敏锐的才智和深刻的洞察力，这些品质继承自他的导师。（innate, intellect, insight, inherited）

**参考答案**: He possesses an **innate** sharp **intellect** and profound **insight** — qualities **inherited** from his mentor.

### 8. 人们有意或无意地受到社交媒体的影响，这已对社会人际交往产生了深远影响。（intentionally, unintentionally, interpersonal）

**参考答案**: People are **intentionally** or **unintentionally** influenced by social media, which has profoundly affected **interpersonal** communication.

## Section C: 写作模板积累

**说明**: 以下句型适用于考研英语写作，使用今日高频词汇，可直接套用。

### 模板 1: "It is ironic that..."

> **It is ironic that** the very technology designed to connect people has, in many ways, weakened our **interpersonal** relationships and fostered a culture of **indifference**.

**适用场景**: 社会现象类作文（科技、媒体、人际关系）
**译文**: 具有讽刺意味的是，原本旨在连接人们的技术，在许多方面反而削弱了我们的人际关系，并助长了冷漠的文化。

---

### 模板 2: "...is an inevitable consequence of..."

> The widening achievement gap **is an inevitable consequence of** **insufficient** educational resources and **inadequate** institutional support for underprivileged communities.

**适用场景**: 社会问题分析类作文（教育、不平等）
**译文**: 日益扩大的成就差距是对贫困社区教育资源不足和制度支持不够的必然结果。

---

### 模板 3: "The interplay between...and..."

> **The interplay between** technological **innovation** and **institutional** reform will **inevitably** shape the trajectory of modern **industrialization**.

**适用场景**: 科技发展、社会进步类作文
**译文**: 技术创新与制度改革之间的相互作用将不可避免地塑造现代工业化的轨迹。

---

### 模板 4: "There is an inexorable trend towards..."

> **There is an inexorable trend towards** global economic **interdependence**, and no nation can develop **independently** of the international community.

**适用场景**: 全球化、国际合作类作文
**译文**: 全球经济相互依存是一种不可阻挡的趋势，没有任何国家可以独立于国际社会而发展。

---

### 模板 5: "This serves as an indicator of..."

> The rapid increase in **intake** of fast food among young people **serves as an indicator of** the **inadequate** public awareness about healthy eating habits.

**适用场景**: 社会现象分析类作文（健康、生活方式）
**译文**: 年轻人快餐摄入量的快速增长表明公众对健康饮食习惯的认识不足。
