## 练习二：汉译英（5题）

将以下中文句子翻译为英文，使用今日所学词汇：

**1.** 政府必须采取严厉措施来遏制空气污染的加剧，因为呼吸系统疾病的激增已经暴露出环境治理的严重不足。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The government must take strict measures to ==curb== the worsening air pollution, as the ==surge== in respiratory diseases has ==surface==d as a glaring ==symptom== of inadequate environmental governance.

**2.** 在农村地区，许多学校仍然缺乏完善的课程体系，这给教师和学生都带来了沉重的负担。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> In ==rural== areas, many schools still lack a well-developed ==curriculum==, which places a heavy ==burden== on both teachers and students and fails to serve the true ==purpose== of education.

**3.** 这位外科医生因为准时到达手术室而受到赞扬，他迅速而果断地为一名遭遇突发心脏骤停的患者实施了手术。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The ==surgeon== was praised for being ==punctual== in arriving at the operating room, where he ==swift==ly and decisively performed ==surgery== on a patient who had suffered a ==sudden== cardiac arrest.

**4.** 随着网络犯罪日益增多，执法部门正试图通过多个法律标准来打击犯罪行为，以重建公众对数字空间的信任。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> With ==cyberspace== ==crime==s ==multiply==ing, law enforcement is attempting to combat ==criminal== behaviour through ==multiple== legal ==criterion|criteria== in order to rebuild public ==trust== in the digital domain.

**5.** 研究表明，合成材料的大量使用会引发一系列环境综合征，而用天然替代品替代这些材料则可以从根本上治愈这一顽疾。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译

## 练习三：写作段落（2题）

使用指定词汇写一段约80-100词的英文段落：

**题目1**：讨论如何控制日益严重的空气污染问题
**要求使用词汇**：curb, symptom, multiply, submerge, surface

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> Air pollution has become one of the most pressing challenges of our time. As industrial emissions ==multiply== year by year, the harmful effects ==surface== in the form of respiratory diseases and ecological degradation — clear ==symptom==s of an ailing environment. If left unchecked, entire communities could be ==submerge==d under layers of toxic smog. Governments must therefore take bold steps to ==curb== emissions through stricter regulations and cleaner technologies. Only coordinated global action can reverse this dangerous trend and safeguard public health for future generations. (86 words)

**题目2**：讨论农村教育的发展与挑战
**要求使用词汇**：rural, curriculum, punctual, burden, multiply

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> Despite recent progress, ==rural== education in many developing regions remains severely under-resourced. An outdated ==curriculum== fails to equip students with the skills needed in a modern economy, while a shortage of teachers forces existing staff to ==multiply== their efforts, creating an unsustainable workload. This heavy ==burden== often leads to burnout and high turnover. Moreover, poor infrastructure makes it difficult for both teachers and students to be ==punctual==, further disrupting the learning process. Addressing these systemic issues requires sustained investment in teacher training, digital tools, and community engagement. (86 words)

## 练习四：近义词辨析

| 易混词组 | 含义区别 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| curb vs curve vs curl | curb = v. 控制，抑制 / n. 路缘（控制/限制）；curve = n. 曲线，弯曲（形状）；curl = v./n. 卷曲，蜷缩（卷的动作或形态） | The government must **curb** inflation. / The road has a sharp **curve**. / Her hair **curl**s naturally. |
| cure vs curve | cure = v./n. 治愈，疗法（医疗语境）；curve = n. 曲线，弧线（几何/形状语境） | Scientists are searching for a **cure** for cancer. / Draw a **curve** connecting these two points. |
| sympathy vs sympathetic | sympathy = n. 同情，同情心（名词）；sympathetic = adj. 同情的，赞成的（形容词，注意词义延伸为"赞同的"） | I have great **sympathy** for the victims. / She was very **sympathetic** to our cause. |
| synthetic vs symbol | synthetic = adj. 合成的，人造的（强调非天然）；symbol = n. 符号，象征（强调代表意义） | **Synthetic** fibres are widely used in clothing. / The dove is a **symbol** of peace. |
| purpose vs purse | purpose = n. 目的，意图；purse = n. 钱包，手提包 / v. 撅嘴（注意拼写相似但词义完全不同） | What is the **purpose** of this meeting? / She reached into her **purse** for her keys. |
| rural vs urban | rural = adj. 农村的，乡下的；urban = adj. 城市的（反义词对，考研阅读常对比使用） | **Rural** communities often lack access to advanced healthcare. / **Urban** populations continue to grow rapidly. |
| surface vs surge | surface = n. 表面 / v. 浮出水面，显露；surge = v./n. 激增，汹涌（强调突然大幅增加） | New evidence has **surface**d. / There was a **surge** in demand for electric vehicles. |
| custom vs curriculum | custom = n. 习俗，海关 / adj. 定制的；curriculum = n. 课程体系，教学计划（教育专用词） | Each culture has its own **custom**s. / The school updated its **curriculum** to include computer science. |
| attract vs attendant | attract = v. 吸引，引起；attendant = n. 服务员，随从 / adj. 伴随的（注意：attendant 不是"吸引"的派生词） | The exhibition **attract**ed thousands of visitors. / The flight **attendant** ensured passenger safety. |
| symptom vs syndrome | symptom = n. 症状（单一表现）；syndrome = n. 综合征（一组症状的集合，医学用语） | Fever is a common **symptom** of flu. / Down **syndrome** is a genetic condition. |
| bunch vs bundle | bunch = n. 束，串（自然聚在一起，如花、钥匙）；bundle = n. 捆，包（人为捆绑在一起，也可指抽象的"一揽子"） | She bought a **bunch** of flowers. / He tied the newspapers into a **bundle**. |
| punish vs punch | punish = v. 惩罚，处罚（正式/法律语境）；punch = v. 用拳猛击 / n. 一拳（肢体动作） | The court **punish**ed the offender with a fine. / He **punch**ed the wall in frustration. |
| swift vs sweep | swift = adj. 迅速的，敏捷的（强调速度快）；sweep = v. 扫，席卷 / n. 扫除（强调覆盖范围广） | The government took **swift** action. / The flood **sweep**t through the village. |
| subtract vs submerge | subtract = v. 减去，扣除（数学运算）；submerge = v. 淹没，浸没（物理动作，也比喻被淹没） | **Subtract** 5 from 12 and you get 7. / The entire neighbourhood was **submerge**d by floodwater. |
| trust vs true | trust = v./n. 信任，信赖（关系层面）；true = adj. 真实的，正确的（事实层面） | You should never **trust** a stranger blindly. / Is it **true** that he resigned? |
| burst vs bury | burst = v. 爆裂，突然爆发（向外扩散）；bury = v. 埋葬，掩埋（向下隐藏） | The pipe **burst** under pressure. / They **burie**d the treasure underground. |
| bureau vs bureaucracy | bureau = n. 局，办事处（具体机构）；bureaucracy = n. 官僚主义，官僚体制（抽象制度，常含贬义） | The Federal Bureau of Investigation handles major crimes. / Excessive **bureaucracy** slows down public services. |
