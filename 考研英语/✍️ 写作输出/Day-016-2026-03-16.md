## Part 1: 写作替换升级

> [!tip] 使用更高级的词汇替换划线部分

**1. Environmental protection is <u>very important</u> to sustainable development.**
- 替换词：**crucial / critical**
- 升级后：Environmental protection is **crucial** to sustainable development.

**2. The new policy caused a <u>big</u> change in consumer behavior.**
- 替换词：**dramatic**
- 升级后：The new policy caused a **dramatic** change in consumer behavior.

**3. Some animals are <u>easily hurt</u> by habitat destruction.**
- 替换词：**vulnerable**
- 升级后：Some animals are **vulnerable** to habitat destruction.

**4. The company finally <u>used</u> legal action to protect its interests.**
- 替换词：**resorted to**
- 升级后：The company finally **resorted to** legal action to protect its interests.

**5. The economic crisis <u>caused</u> many small businesses to close.**
- 替换词：**triggered / prompted**
- 升级后：The economic crisis **triggered** many small businesses to close.

**6. We need <u>enough</u> evidence to support this claim.**
- 替换词：**sufficient**
- 升级后：We need **sufficient** evidence to support this claim.

**7. His <u>success</u> over adversity inspired many people.**
- 替换词：**triumph**
- 升级后：His **triumph** over adversity inspired many people.

**8. The government should <u>suggest</u> new policies to address climate change.**
- 替换词：**propose / recommend**
- 升级后：The government should **propose** new policies to address climate change.

## Part 3: 词义辨析

> [!warning] 选择正确的词填空

**1. The two companies will go their \_\_\_\_\_\_ ways after the merger.**
- A. respectful
- B. respective
- C. respected
- D. respecting

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：B. respective**

**解析**：
- **respective** = 各自的（形容词）
- **respectful** = 恭敬的
- **respected** = 受尊敬的
- **respecting** = 关于（介词）

句意：合并后，两家公司将各走各的路。
</details>

---

**2. The team has won three \_\_\_\_\_\_ championships.**
- A. successful
- B. successive
- C. succeeding
- D. succeeded

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：B. successive**

**解析**：
- **successive** = 连续的（形容词）
- **successful** = 成功的
- **succeeding** = 随后的
- **succeeded** = 继任的（过去分词）

句意：该队连续赢得了三次冠军。
</details>

---

**3. She sought legal \_\_\_\_\_\_ before filing the lawsuit.**
- A. council
- B. counsel
- C. counseling
- D. counselor

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：B. counsel**

**解析**：
- **counsel** = 法律顾问、建议（名词）
- **council** = 委员会、议会
- **counseling** = 咨询服务
- **counselor** = 顾问（人）

句意：她在提起诉讼前寻求了法律建议。
</details>

---

**4. The company plans to \_\_\_\_\_\_ new markets in Asia.**
- A. exploit
- B. explore
- C. explode
- D. explain

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：B. explore**

**解析**：
- **explore** = 探索、考察
- **exploit** = 剥削、利用
- **explode** = 爆炸
- **explain** = 解释

</details>

**5. The \_\_\_\_\_\_ of the dam caused widespread flooding.**
- A. collapse
- B. collision
- C. collusion
- D. collection

<details>
<summary>点击查看答案与解析</summary>

**答案：A. collapse**

**解析**：
- **collapse** = 倒塌、崩溃
- **collision** = 碰撞
- **collusion** = 勾结
- **collection** = 收集

句意：大坝的倒塌造成了大范围洪水。

注：虽然 collapse 不在今日单词表中，但 tumble（跌倒、倒塌）有类似含义。
</details>

## Part 4: 写作句型积累

> [!success] 高分句型模板

### 1. 表达"至关重要"

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| It is **crucial** that... | It is crucial that we address climate change immediately. |
| ...is **critical** to... | Education is critical to social mobility. |
| ...plays a **vital** role in... | Technology plays a vital role in modern healthcare. |

### 2. 表达"导致/引起"

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| ...**triggered** a series of... | The policy triggered a series of protests. |
| ...**prompted** sb to do sth | The crisis prompted the government to take action. |
| ...**served as a trigger for**... | The incident served as a trigger for social change. |

### 3. 表达"回应"

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| In **response** to... | In response to public pressure, the company changed its policy. |
| ...**responded** by doing sth | The government responded by increasing funding. |

### 4. 表达"易受影响"

| 句型 | 例句 |
|------|------|
| ...is **vulnerable** to... | Small businesses are vulnerable to economic downturns. |
| ...is **susceptible** to... | Elderly people are susceptible to this disease. |

## 📝 今日写作任务

> [!task] 写作练习
> 请使用以下至少5个词汇，写一段100-150词的短文，主题为"科技对就业的影响"：
>
> **词汇要求**：critical, vulnerable, sufficient, prompt, respond, dramatic, cultivate, triumph

<details>
<summary>点击查看范文</summary>

The impact of technology on employment has become a **critical** issue in modern society. While technological advancement has brought **dramatic** changes to various industries, many workers remain **vulnerable** to job displacement. It is **sufficient** to say that simply resisting change is not the answer.

Instead, governments and educational institutions should **prompt** workers to adapt by **cultivating** new skills. Companies must **respond** to this challenge by providing adequate training programs. If managed properly, the workforce can **triumph** over the challenges posed by automation, creating a more prosperous future for all.
