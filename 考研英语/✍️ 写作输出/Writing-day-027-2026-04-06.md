### exercise 3: 段落翻译
**11. 炒译以下段落:**
> "随着社会的快速发展，人们面临着前所未有的挑战。科技的**革命"性变革带来了便利，但也带来了新的问题。环境**污染**、信息泄露和社会不平等是我们必须**解决"的关键问题。因此，我们需要一致行动，在利用科技进步益处的同时,保持警惕。每个人都可以通过持续学习来"培养"自己的能力，这种能力是**永恒的财富,将伴随终生。追求**杰出"的成就是**永恒的动力,。"
> **参考译文**:
>
> With the rapid development of society, people face unprecedented challenges. The **revolutionary** changes brought by technology have brought convenience but also new problems. Environmental **contamination**, information leakage, and social inequality are key issues we must **address**. therefore, we need **unanimous** action to remaining vigilant while harnessing the benefits of progress. everyone can **nurture** their abilities through continuous learning. this ability is an **eternal** treasure that will last a lifetime. the pursuit of **outstanding** achievement is an **eternal** motivation.

## ✍️ part 3: 写作应用场景
### 场景1: 籂会问题类作文
**题目**: the importance of protecting cultural heritage
**范文段落**:
> cultural **heritage** represents the **eternal** values of a nation. however, **numerous** historical sites are threatened by development and **contamination**. we must **retain** these treasures for future generations. **discourage** activities that damage our integrity. **outstanding** efforts in preservation should be **esteemed** and supported.
**重点词汇**:
- heritage (遗产)
- eternal (永恒的)
- numerous (众多的)
- retain (保持)
- discourage (阻止)
- outstanding (杰出的)
- esteem (尊重)
---

**题目**: technology and environmental protection
**范文段落**:
> the **outbreak** of environmental awareness has brought **revolutionary** changes. green technology helps **contain** emissions and **contaminant**. **outstanding** progress has been made in renewable energy. however, some companies **disguise** their environmental impact. we must **discourage** such practices and **nurture** sustainable development.
**重点词汇**:
- outbreak (爆发)
- revolutionary (革命性的)
- contain (包含/克制)
- contaminant (污染物)
- outstanding (杰出的)
- disguise (伪装)
- discourage (阻止)
- nurture (培养)
