## 练习二：汉译英（5题）

将以下中文句子翻译为英文，使用今日所学词汇：

**1.** 尽管面临严峻的挑战，该公司还是采取了严格的措施来稳定局势。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Despite facing formidable challenges, the company took ==rigorous== measures to stabilize the situation.

**2.** 这种社会偏见在老年人中很普遍，但我们应该摆脱这些刻板印象。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> This social bias is ==prevalent== among the elderly, but we should ==rid== ourselves of these ==stereotype==s.

**3.** 内在的动机往往比外在的奖励更能激励人们取得卓越的成就。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> ==Intrinsic== ==motive==s often ==motivate== people to achieve excellence more effectively than external rewards.

**4.** 该国以调查为借口入侵了邻国，这一行为最终扰乱了整个地区的稳定。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> The country ==invade==d its neighbour under the ==pretext== of an investigation, an act that ==eventually== ==disrupt==ed the stability of the entire region.

**5.** 她凭借直觉和丰富的专业经验，清楚地阐述了自己的观点，这证明她的建议是极其宝贵的。

> [!note]- 🔑 参考翻译
> Drawing on her ==intuition== and rich professional experience, she ==articulate==d her views clearly, which proved her advice to be ==invaluable==.

## 练习三：写作段落（2题）

使用指定词汇写一段约80-100词的英文段落：

**题目1**：描述一种普遍存在的社会现象并表达你的看法
**要求使用词汇**：prevalent, stereotype, rigid, artificial, articulate

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> A ==prevalent== ==stereotype== in modern society is the belief that success is solely determined by academic credentials. This ==rigid== view creates an ==artificial== hierarchy that undervalues practical skills and creative talent. Those who cannot ==articulate== their ideas in formal academic language are often dismissed, regardless of their actual competence. In my view, society must move beyond such narrow definitions of ability and recognise that human potential is far more diverse than any single metric can capture. (75 words)

**题目2**：讨论技术发展对传统行业的冲击
**要求使用词汇**：disrupt, intrinsic, robust, prevail, mobilize

> [!note]- 🔑 参考范文
> The rapid rise of automation continues to ==disrupt== traditional manufacturing industries, threatening the livelihoods of workers whose ==intrinsic== value lies in decades of hands-on experience. While some argue that technological progress is inevitable and that ==robust== economies will simply absorb the shock, the reality is more complex. Governments must ==mobilize== resources to retrain displaced workers and ensure that the benefits of innovation do not ==prevail== only among the wealthy few. Without deliberate intervention, the gap between the technologically empowered and the rest will only widen. (81 words)

## 练习四：近义词辨析

| 易混词组 | 含义区别 | 例句 |
|----------|----------|------|
| stationary vs stationery | stationary = 静止的（adj.）；stationery = 文具（n.） | The car was **stationary** at the traffic light. / I bought some **stationery** for the new semester. |
| mold vs mould | 美式/英式拼写差异，含义相同（模具/塑造） | The US factory uses a **mold** to shape the parts. / The UK factory uses a **mould** to shape the parts. |
| mobilise vs mobilize | 英式/美式拼写差异，均表示"动员" | The British Army **mobilise**d quickly. / The US Army **mobilize**d quickly. |
| prevail vs prevalent | prevail = v. 流行，获胜；prevalent = adj. 流行的 | Optimism **prevails** in the community. / Flu is highly **prevalent** in winter. |
| rigid vs rigorous | rigid = 僵硬的，固执的；rigorous = 严格细致的，严谨的 | He held **rigid** views on discipline. / The research methods were **rigorous**. |
| intrinsic vs intuition | intrinsic = 内在的，固有的；intuition = 直觉（能力） | The painting has **intrinsic** value. / She had an **intuition** that something was wrong. |
| disturb vs disrupt | disturb = 打扰，弄乱；disrupt = 使中断，瓦解 | Please don't **disturb** me while I'm working. / The strike **disrupt**ed public transport. |
| still vs stationary | still = adv.仍然 / adj.静止的（可指短暂）；stationary = adj.固定不动的（强调不变） | The lake was perfectly **still** at dawn. / The population has remained **stationary** for a decade. |
| distant vs distance | distant = adj.遥远的；distance = n.距离 / v.疏远 | She gave me a **distant** smile. / The **distance** between the two cities is 200 km. |
| invalid vs invaluable | invalid = 无效的 / 残疾的；invaluable = 极有价值的（非"无价值的"） | The ticket is **invalid** after today. / Her contribution was **invaluable** to the project. |
| rob vs rip | rob = 抢劫，剥夺；rip = 撕裂，扯开 | He was **rob**bed of his wallet. / She **rip**ped the envelope open. |
| motive vs motivate | motive = n.动机；motivate = v.激励，激发 | What was the **motive** behind the crime? / A good teacher **motivate**s students to learn. |
| distill vs distil | 美式/英式拼写差异，均表示"蒸馏/提炼" | The American lab **distill**ed the compound. / The British lab **distil**led the compound. |
| loose vs loosen | loose = adj.松的 / v.释放；loosen = v.使变松，放松 | The handle is **loose**. / Can you **loosen** the lid for me? |
| forbid vs forbid | forbid = v.禁止（过去式forbad/forbade，过去分词forbidden） | Smoking is **forbidden** in public places. / Her parents **forbade** her from going out. |
