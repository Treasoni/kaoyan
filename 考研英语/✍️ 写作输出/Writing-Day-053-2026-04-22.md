## Part I: 词汇替换（升级表达）

将下列简单表达替换为本日学习的更高级词汇：

| 序号 | 原始表达 | 替换词 | 升级表达 |
|------|----------|--------|----------|
| 1 | important / significant | **matter** | What truly **matters** is not the outcome but the effort. |
| 2 | continue / endure | **last** | The effects of the policy will **last** for decades. |
| 3 | broadcast in real time | **live** | The ceremony was broadcast **live** to millions of viewers. |
| 4 | vacation / time off | **leave** | She requested a three-month **leave** to care for her family. |
| 5 | exactly / precisely | **to the letter** | The instructions must be followed **to the letter**. |
| 6 | impossible / can't be done | **impossible** | It is **impossible** to overestimate the importance of education. |
| 7 | lesson learned | **lesson** | The financial crisis taught us a valuable **lesson** about risk. |
| 8 | save / use sparingly | **husband** | We must **husband** our natural resources for future generations. |
| 9 | popular / successful | **hit** | The new policy was an unexpected **hit** with the public. |
| 10 | provide shelter for | **house** | The new building will **house** the university's research center. |

<details>

What **matters** is that we should not let this opportunity slip away, because it may not **last** long.

## Part III: 词汇辨析

### 1. matter: 事情 vs 要紧

| 词义 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| n. | 事情，问题 | The **matter** has been resolved. |
| v. | 要紧，有关系 | It doesn't **matter** what others think. |

**考研注意**: matter 作动词常用于否定句和疑问句。What matters is...（重要的是...）是考研写作高级句式。a matter of（关于...的问题）也是固定搭配。

---

### 2. last: 最后的 vs 持续

| 词义 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| adj. | 最后的 | The **last** chapter of the book is the most important. |
| v. | 持续 | The meeting **lasted** for three hours. |
| adv. | 最后 | He arrived **last**. |

**考研注意**: last 作动词"持续"是考研完形填空和阅读理解的高频考点。搭配：last for + 时间段。the last straw（最后一根稻草）是常见习语。

---

### 3. live: 居住 vs 现场直播的

| 词义 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| v. | 居住 | She **lives** in a small apartment. |
| adj. | 现场直播的 | The game was broadcast **live**. |
| adj. | 活的 | **Live** animals are not allowed on the bus. |

**考研注意**: live 的形容词发音 /laɪv/，与动词发音 /lɪv/ 不同。live broadcast（现场直播）是固定搭配。

---

### 4. leave: 离开 vs 休假 vs 留下

| 词义 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| v. | 离开 | She **left** early this morning. |
| n. | 休假 | He is on **leave** this week. |
| v. | 留下 | **Leave** the door open. |

**考研注意**: leave 的三种含义都是考研高频考点。on leave（在休假中）是固定搭配。leave sb doing sth（让某人一直做某事）是重要语法结构。

---

### 5. hit: 打 vs 热门 vs 意识到

| 词义 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| v. | 打，击中 | The ball **hit** the window. |
| n. | 热门，成功 | The movie was a huge **hit**. |
| v. | 突然意识到 | It **hit** me that I had forgotten the keys. |

**考研注意**: It hit sb that...（某人突然意识到...）是考研阅读理解中常见的表达方式，不要理解为"打了某人"。hit the market（上市）、hit the headlines（成为头条）也是固定搭配。

> [!tip] 写作建议
> 1. **matter** 替换 be important：What matters is...（重要的是...），是考研写作开头/结尾的高级句式
> 2. **last** 作动词"持续"：可用于描述现象/趋势的持续时间，数据图表作文中常用
> 3. **live** 作形容词"直播的"：live broadcast/coverage，可用于描述媒体相关话题
> 4. **leave** 多义灵活：on leave（休假）、leave sb doing（让某人一直做）、leave alone（不管）
> 5. **husband** 作动词"节约使用"：husband resources/energy，可用于环保/可持续发展话题
> 6. 注意 **hit** 的三种含义（打/热门/突然意识到），在阅读理解中根据上下文判断
