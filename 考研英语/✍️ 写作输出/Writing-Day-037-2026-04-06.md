### exercise 3: 段落翻译
**21. 翻译以下段落：**
> "随着数字时代的到来,信息传播变得更加迅速。然而,这也带来了污染公共舆论的风险。我们必须保持批判思维,不要轻易被伪装的信息所欺骗。在回顾历史时,我们可以看到许多这样的教训。因此,培养媒体素养成为当代社会的迫切需求。此问题的核心是如何在获取信息的同时保持清醒的头脑。"
 > **参考译文**:
> with the **outbreak** of the digital age, information spreads more rapidly. however, this also brings the risk of **contaminating** public discourse. we must **retain** critical thinking and not be easily **deceived** by **disguised** information. in **retrospect**, we can see **numerous** such lessons from history. therefore, **nurturing** media literacy has become an **outstanding** need of contemporary society. the **central** question is how to **contain** the spread of misinformation while accessing information.
**22. 翻译以下段落:**
> "文化遗产是一个国家的灵魂。我们必须保护这些永恒的财富,让后代能够继承我们的历史。杰出的遗产不仅包括建筑和还包括非物质文化遗产。我们应该培养年轻一代对传统文化的热爱,而不是让他们对历史麻木。因此,教育在这个问题上起着关键作用。" > **参考译文**:
> cultural **heritage** is the soul of a nation. we must protect these **eternal** treasures so that future generations can **retain** our history. **outstanding** heritage includes not only architecture but also intangible cultural heritage. we should **nurture** the younger generation's love for traditional culture, rather than let them become **numb** to history. therefore, education plays a crucial role in this issue.
**23. 翻译以下段落:**
> "环境危机已经爆发。大量工厂污染了水源,排放有害气体。这场危机的利害攸关方是人类文明的未来。我们必须疏散受影响地区的居民,采取紧急措施阻止污染扩散。这不是一个可以通过临时方案解决的问题,而需要永恒的解决方案。杰出领袖应该站出来,领导这场斗争。" > **参考译文**:
> an environmental crisis has broken out. **numerous** factories have **contaminated** water sources and emitted harmful gases. **at stake** is the future of human civilization. we must **evacuate** residents in affected areas and take urgent measures to **discourage** the spread of pollution. this is not a problem that can be solved by temporary solutions, but requires **eternal** solutions. **outstanding** leaders should step forward to lead this fight.

## ✍️ part 3: 写作应用场景
### 场景1: 环境保护类作文
**题目**: the role of technology in environmental protection
**范文段落**:
> with the **outbreak** of environmental awareness, technology plays an increasingly important role. **satellite** imagery allows us to monitor deforestation and **contaminate**d areas in real time. however, we must not **deceive** ourselves—technology alone cannot solve these problems. **unanimous** action from governments, businesses, and individuals is required. we must **adhere to** sustainable practices and not settle for **faulty** solutions that create more problems than they solve.**重点词汇**:
- outbreak (爆发)
- contaminate (污染)
- deceive (欺骗)
- unanimous (一致的)
- adhere to (坚持)
- faulty (有缺陷的)
---
### 场景2: 社会问题类作文
**题目**: inequality in modern society
**范文段落**:
> economic inequality has **adverse** effects on social stability. while some enjoy **decent** lives, others struggle in **agony**. the **central** question is how to create a more just society. we cannot **deceive** ourselves into thinking this problem will disappear. it requires **unanimous** effort from all sectors. governments must **address** the root causes rather than merely treating symptoms.**重点词汇**:
- adverse (不利的)
- decent (体面的)
- agony (痛苦)
- central (核心的)
- deceive (欺骗)
- unanimous (一致的)
- address (解决)
---
### 场景3: 科技发展类作文

**范文段落**:
> with the **outbreak** of artificial intelligence, the job market is undergoing transformation. while some positions remain **vacant** due to skills mismatch, others are being automated. this creates **agony** for workers in affected industries. we must **address** this challenge through education and training. **adolescents** today need to prepare for jobs that don't yet exist. **unanimous** action from educators and policymakers is essential.**重点词汇**:
- outbreak (爆发)
- vacant (空缺的)
- agony (痛苦)
- address (解决)
- adolescent (青少年)
- unanimous (一致的)

### 常用句型结构

2. **the central question is...**

3. **have adverse effects on...**
   > ......对......有不利影响
4. **we must address the problem of...**
   > 我们必须解决......的问题
5. **despite..., we adhere to...**
