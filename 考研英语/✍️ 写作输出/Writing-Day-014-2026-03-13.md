## Part A：高级词汇替换（3题）

> [!task] 任务说明
> 将句子中的简单词汇替换为今日学习的更高级表达

**1. 请用更高级的表达替换句中的 "support"：**

> 原句：Many experts **support** the idea that education reform is necessary.

替换后：Many experts \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ the idea that education reform is necessary.

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：advocate**

> Many experts **advocate** the idea that education reform is necessary.

**解析**：advocate（vt. 拥护，提倡）比 support 更正式，常用于议论文中表达观点。

</details>

---

**2. 请用更高级的表达替换句中的 "make sure"：**

> 原句：The government should **make sure** that all citizens have access to healthcare.

替换后：The government should \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ that all citizens have access to healthcare.

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：ensure**

> The government should **ensure** that all citizens have access to healthcare.

**解析**：ensure（v. 确保）是 ensure 的正式表达，在写作中更加正式。

</details>

---

**3. 请用更高级的表达替换句中的 "use"：**

> 原句：Companies should **use** new technologies to improve efficiency.

替换后：Companies should \_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_ new technologies to improve efficiency.

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：take advantage of / combine ... with**

> Companies should **take advantage of** new technologies to improve efficiency.
> 或：Companies should **combine** traditional methods **with** new technologies.

**解析**：take advantage of（利用）和 combine...with（将...与...结合）都是比 use 更高级的表达。

</details>

## Part B：汉译英练习（4题）

> [!task] 任务说明
> 将中文句子翻译成英文，使用今日学习的词汇

**4. 这项政策不仅需要资金投入，还需要长期的承诺。**

> 提示词：policy, fund, commit

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**参考译文：**

> This policy **entails** not only financial investment but also long-term **commitment**.
>
> 或：Implementing this policy **entails** both financial input and sustained **commitment**.

**关键词**：entail（vt. 需要，使必要）

</details>

**5. 专家建议企业在扩张前应确保有充足的资金。**

> 提示词：advise, ensure, sufficient, expand

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**参考译文：**

> Experts **advise** that companies should **ensure** sufficient funds before expanding.
>
> 或：It is **advisable** for companies to **ensure** adequate funding before expansion.

**关键词**：advise（v. 建议），ensure（v. 确保），advisable（adj. 可取的）

</details>

## Part C：词义辨析（3题）

> [!task] 任务说明
> 辨析形近词或近义词的区别

**8. 选择最合适的词填空：**

> The two events ______ at the same time, which was purely coincidental.

A) collided
B) coincided
C) combined
D) collected

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：B) coincided**

**辨析**：
- **coincide** = 巧合，同时发生，相符（强调时间上的重合或巧合）
- **collide** = 碰撞，冲突（强调物理碰撞或意见不合）
- **combine** = 结合（强调合二为一）
- **collect** = 收集（强调聚集）

**记忆技巧**：coincide 中的 "in" 可联想为 "inside"，两者在时间上"在里面"重合。

</details>

---

**9. 选择最合适的词填空：**

> The company was ______ for its outstanding contribution to environmental protection.

A) commanded
B) commended
C) commented
D) commissioned

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：B) commended**

**辨析**：
- **commend** = 表扬，称赞（正式用语，常与 for 搭配）
- **command** = 命令；掌握（与 of 搭配表示"精通"）
- **comment** = 评论（与 on 搭配）
- **commission** = 委托；任命

**记忆技巧**：commend 中的 "men" 可联想为 "men praise men"（人们互相表扬）。

</details>

---

**10. 选择最合适的词填空：**

> The contract was declared void due to a ______ of its terms.

A) break
B) breach
C) breakdown
D) breed

<details>
<summary>查看答案</summary>

**答案：B) breach**

**辨析**：
- **breach** = 违反，违背（合同、协议、法律等），正式用语
- **break** = 打破（通用词，口语化）
- **breakdown** = 故障；崩溃（名词）
- **breed** = 繁殖；品种

**记忆技巧**：breach 专门用于法律语境，a breach of contract（违约）。

</details>

## 📝 写作句型积累

### 1. 表达"导致/需要"（entail）

| 简单表达 | 高级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| need / require | entail |
| cause / lead to | entail / breed |

**例句**：
- Success **entails** perseverance and dedication.
- （成功需要坚持不懈和全身心投入。）

### 2. 表达"提倡/建议"（advocate, advise）

| 简单表达 | 高级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| suggest / recommend | advise / advocate |
| think it's good | advisable |

**例句**：
- Many scholars **advocate** a balanced approach to economic development.
- It is **advisable** to consider multiple factors before making a decision.

### 3. 表达"确保"（ensure）

| 简单表达 | 高级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| make sure | ensure |
| guarantee | ensure / affirm |

**例句**：
- Strict regulations **ensure** consumer safety.
- The government must **ensure** equal access to education.

### 4. 表达"影响"（affect）

| 简单表达 | 高级表达 |
|---------|---------|
| change | affect |
| have an effect on | affect |

**例句**：
- Technological advancements **affect** every aspect of modern life.
- Climate change significantly **affects** agricultural production.
