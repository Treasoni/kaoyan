## Part A：写作替换升级（3题）

### 1. 基础词汇替换

**原句**：Many experts **think** that artificial intelligence will change the job market significantly.

**请用本课词汇替换 think，升级句子表达：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- Many experts **speculate** that artificial intelligence will change the job market significantly.
- Many experts **forecast** that artificial intelligence will change the job market significantly.
- Many experts **foresee** that artificial intelligence will change the job market significantly.

**解析**：
- speculate：推测（基于不充分的证据）
- forecast：预测（基于数据分析）
- foresee：预见（提前看到）

</details>

---

### 2. 形容词替换

**原句**：The **bad** situation of the economy has caused serious problems for workers.

**请用本课词汇替换 bad，升级句子表达：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- The **grim** situation of the economy has caused serious problems for workers.
- The **acute** situation of the economy has caused serious problems for workers.
- The **complicated** situation of the economy has caused serious problems for workers.

**解析**：
- grim：严酷的、令人沮丧的（强调悲观）
- acute：严重的、急性的（强调紧迫性）
- complicated：复杂的（强调多因素）

</details>

---

### 3. 动词短语替换

**原句**：The government should **pay attention to** environmental protection.

**请用本课词汇替换 pay attention to，升级句子表达：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- The government should **place emphasis on** environmental protection.
- The government should **emphasize** environmental protection.
- The government should **forge ahead with** environmental protection.

**解析**：
- place emphasis on / emphasize：强调、重视
- forge ahead with：稳步推进

</details>

## Part B：汉译英练习（4题）

### 4. 翻译句子

**中文**：从项目一开始，专家们就强调团队合作的重要性。

**请翻译成英文（使用 outset, emphasize）：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- **From the outset** of the project, experts **emphasized** the importance of teamwork.
- Experts **have emphasized** the importance of teamwork **from the outset** of the project.

**解析**：
- from the outset：从一开始
- emphasize：强调（可替换为 place emphasis on）

</details>

---

### 5. 翻译句子

**中文**：公司必须遵守新的环保法规，否则将面临严重后果。

**请翻译成英文（使用 comply with, enforce）：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- Companies must **comply with** the new environmental regulations, or they will face severe consequences.
- The government will **enforce** the new environmental regulations, and companies must **comply with** them.

**解析**：
- comply with：遵守、服从
- enforce：强制执行

</details>

### 6. 翻译句子

**中文**：数据表明，那些努力适应变化的人更容易获得成功。

**请翻译成英文（使用 indicate, endeavor, acquire）：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

**参考答案**：
- The data **indicates** that those who **endeavor** to adapt to change are more likely to **acquire** success.
- Statistics **indicate** that individuals who **endeavor** to embrace change tend to **acquire** greater success.

**解析**：
- indicate：表明、显示
- endeavor to：努力做某事
- acquire：获得

</details>

### 7. 翻译句子

**中文**：我们应该称赞员工的努力，并鼓励他们继续提升专业技能。

**请翻译成英文（使用 compliment, encourage, specialize）：**

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看参考答案</summary>

## Part C：词义辨析（3题）

### 8. 选词填空

**语境**：The two concepts ______ each other perfectly, creating a comprehensive framework.

**选项**：
A. complement
B. compliment
C. complete
D. complicate

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看答案</summary>

**答案：A. complement**

**解析**：
- complement：补充、使完美（相辅相成）
- compliment：称赞
- complete：完成
- complicate：使复杂

**语境**：这两个概念相互补充，形成了完整的框架。

**记忆技巧**：comple**ment** → comple**te**（补充使其完整）

</details>

---

### 9. 词义辨析

**语境**：The police need to ______ the law strictly to maintain social order.

**选项**：
A. force
B. enforce
C. forge
D. fold

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看答案</summary>

**答案：B. enforce**

**解析**：
- force：强迫（force sb to do sth）
- enforce：强制执行（enforce the law）
- forge：锻造、形成
- fold：折叠

**语境**：警察需要严格执行法律以维护社会秩序。

**记忆技巧**：en- (使) + force (力量) → enforce (用力量去执行)

</details>

---

### 10. 词义辨析

**语境**：His strange behavior is ______ of a deeper psychological problem.

**选项**：
A. indication
B. indicative
C. indicator
D. indicated

<details>
<summary>🔍 查看答案</summary>

**答案：B. indicative**

**解析**：
- indication：迹象（名词）
- indicative：表明的、指示的（形容词）
- indicator：指标（名词）
- indicated：表明的（过去分词）

**语境**：他奇怪的行为表明他有更深层的心理问题。

**固定搭配**：be indicative of... 表明...、显示...

</details>

## 📝 写作模板应用

### 模板1：议论文开头

```
From the outset, it is important to emphasize that [主题]. Recent studies indicate that [现象]. Experts speculate that [原因], and we must endeavor to [解决方案].
```

**示例**：
> From the outset, it is important to emphasize that **climate change** poses a serious threat. Recent studies indicate that **global temperatures are rising**. Experts speculate that **human activities** are the primary cause, and we must endeavor to **reduce carbon emissions**.

---

### 模板2：图表作文描述

```
The data clearly indicates that [趋势]. The gross [总量] has increased/decreased by [百分比]. This is indicative of [原因/含义]. Specialists forecast that [未来预测].
```

**示例**：
> The data clearly indicates that **online shopping** is becoming increasingly popular. The **gross sales** have increased by **30%**. This is indicative of **changing consumer behavior**. Specialists forecast that **this trend will continue**.

---

### 模板3：建议信结尾

```
I hope these suggestions complement your efforts to [目的]. Please do not bother to contact me if you need further assistance. I encourage you to [行动建议].
```

**示例**：
> I hope these suggestions **complement** your efforts to **improve the project**. Please do not **bother to** contact me if you need further assistance. I **encourage** you to **implement these changes as soon as possible**.
