## 📋 词义辨析

### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. fragile / frail**
The old vase is extremely ______ and must be handled with care.
> The old vase is extremely **fragile** and must be handled with care.
（fragile = 易碎的，强调物理上容易损坏；frail = 虚弱的，强调身体衰弱）

**17. hostile / hospitable**
Despite the ______ reception from some critics, the film became a box office success.
> Despite the **hostile** reception from some critics, the film became a box office success.
（hostile = 敌对的/不利的；hospitable = 好客的。注意：两者词源相同但意思相反）

**18. excuse / exempt**
International students are ______ from the military service requirement.
> International students are **exempt** from the military service requirement.
（exempt = 免除的（合法/正式）；excuse = 原谅/借口（日常用语））

**19. foster / adopt**
The program aims to ______ creativity and critical thinking in young minds.
> The program aims to **foster** creativity and critical thinking in young minds.
（foster = 培养/促进（抽象概念）；adopt = 采用/收养（具体行为））

**20. forthcoming / future**
The ______ election has generated great public interest.
> The **forthcoming** election has generated great public interest.
（forthcoming = 即将到来的（近期的）；future = 未来的（泛指的））

## 📝 写作常用句型

### 使用今日词汇的高级句型

1. **表达促进发展**
   > It is essential to **foster** an environment where...
   > 培养...的环境是至关重要的

2. **表达脆弱性**
   > The **fragile** balance between A and B can easily be disrupted.
   > A与B之间的脆弱平衡很容易被打破

3. **表达对比关系**
   > Compared with their **counterparts** in..., Chinese...
   > 与...的对应方相比，中国的...

4. **表达免除/豁免**
   > No one should be **exempt** from the obligation to...
   > 任何人都不应免除...的义务

5. **表达即将到来的**
   > With the **forthcoming** challenges of..., we must...
   > 面对...即将到来的挑战，我们必须...
