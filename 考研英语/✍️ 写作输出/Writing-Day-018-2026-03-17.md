## Part 1: 写作替换升级（5题）

**请用更高级的表达替换划线部分：**

### 1. 原句：The study **shows** that regular exercise improves mental health.

**替换目标**：用 reveal / demonstrate / indicate 替换

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The study reveals that regular exercise improves mental health.**

或：The study **demonstrates** that regular exercise improves mental health.

> 💡 **词汇升级**：show → reveal（揭示）/ demonstrate（证明）
</details>

---

### 2. 原句：Companies need to **find** new ways to reduce costs.

**替换目标**：用 pursue / seek / explore 替换

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**Companies need to pursue new ways to reduce costs.**

或：Companies need to **seek** new ways to reduce costs.

> 💡 **词汇升级**：find → pursue（追求）/ seek（寻求）
</details>

---

### 3. 原句：His performance **was better than** everyone expected.

**替换目标**：用 surpass / exceed / outperform 替换

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**His performance surpassed everyone's expectations.**

或：His performance **exceeded** everyone's expectations.

> 💡 **词汇升级**：be better than → surpass（超过）/ exceed（超越）
</details>

---

### 4. 原句：The new policy **caused** significant changes in consumer behavior.

**替换目标**：用 result in / lead to / bring about 替换

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The new policy resulted in significant changes in consumer behavior.**

或：The new policy **brought about** significant changes in consumer behavior.

> 💡 **词汇升级**：cause → result in（导致）/ bring about（带来）
</details>

---

### 5. 原句：The government **made** new regulations on environmental protection.

**替换目标**：用 issue / promulgate / enact 替换

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The government issued new regulations on environmental protection.**

或：The government **promulgated** new regulations on environmental protection.

> 💡 **词汇升级**：make → issue（颁布）/ promulgate（公布）
</details>

## Part 2: 汉译英练习（5题）

**请将以下中文句子翻译成英文，使用括号内的目标词汇：**

### 1. 由于经济危机，许多小企业没能幸存下来。（survive）

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**Many small businesses failed to survive the economic crisis.**

> 💡 **要点**：survive = 幸存；survive + 名词 = 从...中幸存
</details>

---

### 2. 调查揭示了一些令人不安的事实。（reveal）

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The investigation revealed some disturbing facts.**

> 💡 **要点**：reveal = 揭示；常与 truth, fact, information 搭配
</details>

---

### 3. 公司需要采取系统化的方法来解决问题。（systematic）

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The company needs to adopt a systematic approach to solve the problem.**

> 💡 **要点**：systematic approach = 系统化的方法
</details>

---

### 4. 这项政策的结果是税收收入大幅增加。（revenue, result）

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**As a result of this policy, tax revenue increased significantly.**

或：**This policy resulted in a significant increase in tax revenue.**

> 💡 **要点**：revenue = 收入（特指政府收入）；result in = 导致
</details>

---

### 5. 会议将在午餐后继续进行。（resume）

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

**The meeting will resume after lunch.**

> 💡 **要点**：resume = 重新开始；继续（非"简历"义）
</details>

## 📝 写作词汇积累

### 高级动词替换表

| 基础动词 | 高级替换 | 适用场景 |
|----------|----------|----------|
| show | **reveal, demonstrate, indicate** | 研究结果、数据展示 |
| find | **pursue, seek, explore** | 寻找方法、途径 |
| cause | **result in, lead to, bring about** | 因果关系 |
| make | **issue, promulgate, enact** | 政策、法规 |
| be better than | **surpass, exceed, outperform** | 比较、超越 |
| continue | **resume, proceed** | 中断后继续 |

### 学术写作高频词组

- **result in** - 导致
- **as a result of** - 由于
- **reveal that** - 揭示...
- **pursue a strategy** - 追求策略
- **surpass expectations** - 超出预期
- **issue regulations** - 颁布法规
- **systematic approach** - 系统化方法
- **survive a crisis** - 从危机中幸存

## 💡 写作技巧提示

### 1. 动词升级原则

- 避免使用过于简单的动词（如 make, get, have）
- 选择更精准的学术动词（如 issue, obtain, possess）
- 考研写作中，动词升级能显著提升语言质量

### 2. 僻义使用技巧

| 单词 | 常见用法 | 写作高级用法 |
|------|----------|--------------|
| resume | n. 简历 | v. 重新开始 |
| issue | n. 问题 | vt. 颁布 |
| average | adj. 平均的 | adj. 普通的 |
| survive | vi. 幸存 | vt. 比...活得长 |

### 3. 句式升级示例

**基础版**：The policy caused many changes.

**升级版**：The policy **resulted in** substantial transformations.

**基础版**：He showed that the method works.

**升级版**：He **demonstrated** the effectiveness of the method.
