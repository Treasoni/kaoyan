## Part II: 中译英练习

**1. 法院将孩子的监护权判给了母亲。**

<details>
<summary>✅ 参考译文</summary>

The court **awarded custody** of the child to the mother.
</details>

<details>

The government needs to take measures to **cushion** the impact of the economic recession.

## Part III: 词汇辨析

### 1. desert vs dessert

| 单词 | 词性 | 含义 | 记忆方法 |
|------|------|------|----------|
| desert | n. | 沙漠 | 只有一个s，沙漠里什么都没有 |
| dessert | n. | 甜点 | 多一个s，甜点多了一份甜蜜 |

**练习**: 填入正确的词
1. The Sahara is the largest hot _____ in the world. (desert)
2. Would you like to order a _____ after the main course? (dessert)

---

### 2. dispatch vs despatch

| 单词 | 说明 |
|------|------|
| dispatch | 更常用的拼写 |
| despatch | 英式英语变体，含义完全相同 |

**结论**: 考试中优先使用 **dispatch**

---

### 3. china vs China

| 写法 | 含义 | 用法 |
|------|------|------|
| China | 中国 | 专有名词，首字母大写 |
| china | 瓷器 | 普通名词，首字母小写 |

**考研真题可能出现**: "The exhibition features fine china from the Ming Dynasty." → 这里的china = 瓷器

---

### 4. coast vs coast (僻义)

| 词性 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| n. | 海岸 | The ship sailed along the coast. |
| vi. | 靠惯性滑行 | The car coasted downhill. |

**考研注意**: "coast"作动词时是不及物动词，常与down/to连用

---

### 5. cave: 名词 vs 动词短语

| 用法 | 含义 | 例句 |
|------|------|------|
| n. | 山洞 | They explored the deep cave. |
| cave in | 塌落 | The roof caved in under the weight of snow. |
| cave in | 认输/屈服 | He finally caved in to their demands. |
