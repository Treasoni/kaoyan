### Exercise 3: 段落翻译

**11. 翻译以下段落：**

> "近年来，随着科技的快速发展，人类社会经历了前所未有的变革。人工智能、卫星技术和互联网的出现改变了我们的生活方式。然而，科技进步也带来了不利影响，如环境污染、隐私泄露和就业压力。因此，我们需要一致行动，在利用科技益处的同时解决其负面影响。"

> **参考译文**:
>
> **Lately**, with the rapid development of technology, human society has experienced unprecedented changes. The **advent** of artificial intelligence, **satellite** technology, and the Internet has transformed our way of life. However, technological progress has also brought **adverse** effects, such as environmental pollution, privacy breaches, and employment pressure. Therefore, we need **unanimous** action to **address** the negative impacts while harnessing the benefits of technology.

## ✍️ Part 3: 写作应用场景

### 场景1: 环境保护类作文

**题目**: The Role of Technology in Environmental Protection

**范文段落**:

> With the **advent** of green technology, we now have powerful tools to **address** environmental challenges. **Satellite** monitoring allows us to track deforestation and climate change in real time. However, we must not **deceive** ourselves—technology alone cannot solve these problems. **Unanimous** action from governments, businesses, and individuals is required. We must **adhere to** sustainable practices and not settle for **faulty** solutions that create more problems than they solve.

**重点词汇**:
- advent (出现)
- address (解决)
- satellite (卫星)
- deceive (欺骗)
- unanimous (一致的)
- adhere to (坚持)
- faulty (有缺陷的)

---

### 场景2: 社会问题类作文

**题目**: Inequality in Modern Society

**范文段落**:

> Economic inequality has **adverse** effects on social stability. While some enjoy **decent** lives, others struggle in **agony**. The **central** question is how to create a more just society. We cannot **deceive** ourselves into thinking this problem will disappear. It requires **unanimous** effort from all sectors. Governments must **address** the root causes rather than merely treating symptoms.

**重点词汇**:
- adverse (不利的)
- decent (体面的)
- agony (极度痛苦)
- central (核心的)
- deceive (欺骗)
- unanimous (一致的)
- address (解决)

---

### 场景3: 科技发展类作文

**题目**: The Impact of AI on Employment

**范文段落**:

> With the **advent** of artificial intelligence, the job market is undergoing transformation. While some positions remain **vacant** due to skills mismatch, others are being automated. This creates **agony** for workers in affected industries. We must **address** this challenge through education and training. **Adolescents** today need to prepare for jobs that don't yet exist. **Unanimous** action from educators and policymakers is essential.

**重点词汇**:
- advent (出现)
- vacant (空缺的)
- agony (痛苦)
- address (解决)
- adolescent (青少年)
- unanimous (一致的)
