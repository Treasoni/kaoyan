## 第一部分：写作替换升级（4题）

> [!info] 将初级词汇替换为高级词汇，提升写作档次

**1. 初级句**: The government should **solve** the problem of inequality.
   **高级替换**: The government should ______ the problem of inequality.

   **答案**: **address**

   > [!success] 替换说明
   > address作"处理、解决"讲时，是考研高频僻义，比solve更正式、更高级

   **造句练习**:
   - We must **address** the issue of climate change.
   - The committee failed to **address** public concerns.

---

**2. 初级句**: Students **get** knowledge from various sources.
   **高级替换**: Students ______ knowledge from various sources.

   **答案**: **acquire**

   > [!success] 替换说明
   > acquire表示"（通过努力）获得、取得"，比get更正式，常用于acquire knowledge/skills

   **造句练习**:
   - Students **acquire** language skills through practice.
   - We should **acquire** a deeper understanding of the issue.

---

**3. 初级句**: Many factors **cause** this phenomenon.
   **高级替换**: Many factors ______ to this phenomenon.

   **答案**: **contribute**

   > [!success] 替换说明
   > contribute to表示"促成、导致（某结果）"，是cause的高级替换词

   **造句练习**:
   - Several factors **contribute** to economic growth.
   - Stress can **contribute** to various health problems.

---

**4. 初级句**: The meeting was very **effective**.
   **高级替换**: The meeting was very ______.

   **答案**: **productive**

   > [!success] 替换说明
   > productive表示"富有成效的、多产的"，比effective更具体强调"产出成果"

   **造句练习**:
   - The discussion was highly **productive**.
   - We need a **productive** approach to problem-solving.

## 第二部分：汉译英（3题）

> [!info] 使用目标词汇翻译句子，注意词汇的正式程度和搭配

**5. 政府应该为经济危机承担责任。**
   Government should ______ responsibility ______ the economic crisis.

   **答案**: **assume** / **for**

   > [!tip] 搭配要点
   > assume responsibility for sth. 表示"为某事承担责任"，比take responsibility for更正式

   **其他表达**:
   - The company should **assume** full responsibility for the accident.
   - Leaders must **assume** responsibility for their decisions.

---

**6. 我们需要评估这项政策的影响。**
   We need to ______ the impact of this policy.

   **答案**: **assess**

   > [!tip] 词汇辨析
   > - assess: 评估、评定（正式用语，用于价值、影响评估）
   > - evaluate: 评价（更侧重于判断价值）
   > - estimate: 估算（侧重于数量推测）

   **其他表达**:
   - We need to **assess** the effectiveness of the program.
   - Experts were called to **assess** the damage.

---

**7. 这个问题引起了广泛争议。**
   This issue has ______ widespread ______.

   **答案**: **aroused** / **controversy**

   > [!tip] 搭配要点
   > arouse controversy是固定搭配，表示"引起争议"
   > 注意arouse（及物动词）vs arise（不及物动词，"出现"）

   **其他表达**:
   - The proposal **aroused** intense **controversy**.
   - His remarks **aroused** public **controversy**.

## 第三部分：词义辨析（3题）

> [!danger] ⚠️ 选择正确的词汇，注意语境和搭配

**8. The government has ______ new regulations to control pollution.**
   A. instituted
   B. constituted
   C. substituted
   D. distributed

   **答案**: **A. instituted**

   > [!tip] 辨析要点
   > - **institute**: vt. 建立、制定（政策、制度）→ 正式用语
   > - constitute: vt. 构成、组成
   > - substitute: vt. 代替
   > - distribute: vt. 分配

   **语境**: 表示政府制定法规，用institute最准确

---

**9. Scientists continue to ______ into the causes of climate change.**
   A. process
   B. proceed
   C. probe
   D. produce

   **答案**: **C. probe**

   > [!tip] 辨析要点
   > - **probe**: v. 探究、深入调查（常与into连用）
   > - process: v. 加工、处理
   > - proceed: v. 继续进行
   > - produce: v. 生产

   **语境**: probe into表示"深入探究"，符合科学研究语境

---

**10. The two roads ______ at the city center.**
    A. convey
    B. converge
    C. convene
    D. contravene

    **答案**: **B. converge**

    > [!tip] 辨析要点
    > - **converge**: vi. 相交、聚集、趋同
    > - convey: vt. 传递、运输
    > - convene: v. 召集（会议）
    > - contravene: vt. 违反

    **语境**: converge表示道路"相交、汇合"，符合物理空间语境

## ✍️ 写作应用示例

### 议论文段落（使用目标词汇）

> The issue of climate change has **aroused** widespread **controversy** in recent years. Many **experts** from research **institutes** have **converged** on the conclusion that human activities **contribute** significantly to global warming. Therefore, governments must **institute** effective **policies** to **address** this challenge. We need to **assume** responsibility for our actions and **acquire** a deeper understanding of the problem. Only through **productive** international cooperation can we **assess** the situation accurately and **convey** our concerns to future generations.

**使用的高级词汇**（按出现顺序）：
1. aroused controversy - 引起争议
2. experts - 专家
3. institutes - 机构
4. converged - 汇聚
5. contribute - 导致
6. institute - 制定
7. policies - 政策
8. address - 处理
9. assume responsibility - 承担责任
10. acquire - 获得
11. productive - 富有成效的
12. assess - 评估
13. convey - 传递
