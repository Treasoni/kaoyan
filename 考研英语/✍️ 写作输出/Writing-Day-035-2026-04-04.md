## 📝 写作替换练习（10题）
### 说明
将括号中的简单表达替换为更高级的考研词汇。

---

**1. The company must (solve/deal with) this crisis.**
> The company must **address** this crisis.

---

**2. She (really likes/is into) helping others.**
> She **delights in** helping others.

---

**3. We should (keep/stay) alert to potential dangers.**
> We should **remain alert to** potential dangers.

---

**4. The team (worked together) to complete the project.**
> The team **collaborated** to complete the project.

---

**5. The economic situation (got worse/worsened) rapidly.**
> The economic situation **slumped** rapidly.

---

**6. He (gave/assigned) the task to his assistant.**
> He **delegated** the task to his assistant.

---

**7. The plan was (broken/destroyed) by unexpected events.**
> The plan was **shattered** by unexpected events.

---

**8. Farmers (get/harvest) their crops in autumn.**
> Farmers **reap** their crops in autumn.

---

**9. She (kept going/continued) despite the difficulties.**
> She **persisted** despite the difficulties.

---

**10. The information is (easy/readily) available online.**
> The information is **readily** available online.

## 📋 词义辨析（5题）
### 说明
区分每组词的细微差别，选择最合适的词填空。

---

**16. persist / persevere**
Despite the failures, she **______** in her efforts to learn English.

> Despite the failures, she **persevered** in her efforts to learn English.
（persevere = 锲而不舍，强调面对困难时的坚韧； persist = 坚持，强调持续性）

---

**17. shatter / break**
The glass **______** into tiny pieces when it hit the ground.

> The glass **shattered** into tiny pieces when it hit the ground.
（shatter = 粉碎，更强调彻底破碎； break = 打破，更通用）

---

**18. menace / threat**
Pollution is a **______** to public health.

> Pollution is a **menace** to public health.
（menace = 威胁，强调危险的人或物； threat = 威胁，更通用）

---

**19. delegate / represent**
She was chosen to **______** our company at the conference.

> She was chosen to **represent** our company at the conference.
（represent = 代表，指代表某人/某组织； delegate = 授权/委派代表）

---

**20. slump / decline**
There has been a sharp **______** in oil prices.

> There has been a sharp **slump** in oil prices.
（slump = 骤降，强调急剧下跌； decline = 下降，更平缓）
