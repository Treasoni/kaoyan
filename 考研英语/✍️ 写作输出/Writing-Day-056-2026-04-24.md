## 四、重点句型模板

### 1. Since 引导原因状语从句
> **Since** [原因], [主句].
> 例：**Since** education is the foundation of society, we should invest more in it.

### 2. so...that... 结果状语
> **so** [形容词/副词] **that** [结果]
> 例：The problem is **so** complex **that** we need more time to solve it.

### 3. spend...doing 花费时间做某事
> [主语] **spend** [时间] (in) **doing** sth
> 例：Researchers **spend** years **studying** these phenomena.

### 4. stop to do vs stop doing
> **stop doing** sth = 停止正在做的事
> **stop to do** sth = 停下来去做另一件事
> 例：We should **stop ignoring** the problem and **start to address** it.

### 5. such as 举例
> [名词] **such as** [例子1], [例子2], and [例子3]
> 例：Traditional methods, **such as** memorization and repetition, are no longer sufficient.
