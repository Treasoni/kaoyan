$$\alpha\beta^T = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & -1 & 2 \\ -2 & 2 & -4 \\ 3 & -3 & 6 \end{pmatrix}$$
则 $\alpha^T\beta = \underline{\quad \quad}$。
